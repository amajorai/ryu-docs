---
title: "Auto-Capture a Meeting and File the Notes into a Space"
description: "How-to for the Meetings app: detect a call, record both sides, and file structured AI notes into an editable, searchable Space."
---

The Meetings app in Ryu Desktop can detect a call, record it, and write structured notes into an
editable, searchable Space. This recipe walks through one meeting from detection to notes.

## What you'll use

- [Meetings](/docs/surfaces/desktop/productivity/meetings): record a call, get a live transcript and AI notes.
- [Meeting Notes (internals)](/docs/core/meeting-notes): how detection, capture, and finalize work.
- [Spaces and RAG](/docs/core/spaces-rag): where the finalized notes are stored and indexed.

<Callout type="warn">
  The Meetings app is experimental. System-audio (loopback) capture needs no setup on Windows. On
  Linux it uses a PulseAudio or PipeWire monitor source, and on macOS it needs a virtual output
  device such as BlackHole. Without one, the meeting records the microphone only. Automatic
  detection reads the microphone state on Windows and Linux, but on macOS it only checks whether a
  known meeting app is running. Loopback capture and live detection have not been verified on a
  real call. See [Meeting Notes (internals)](/docs/core/meeting-notes) for detection details.
</Callout>

## Steps

<Steps>

<Step>

### Turn on auto-detection

Open **Settings → Meetings** and enable auto-detection. While you are there, review the list of apps
that count as meeting apps and add any you use. Detection does not watch which window is in focus. It
watches the operating system for a process actively holding the microphone, then matches that process
against your meeting-app list.

</Step>

<Step>

### Start a call and let the chip appear

Join a call as usual. When Ryu sees an app from your list grab the microphone, it surfaces a prompt
(on the island's suggestion chip) offering to start recording. Accept it to begin. You can also start
manually from the **Meetings** section in the sidebar by clicking **+** to reach the record screen.

</Step>

<Step>

### Record the meeting

Ryu captures your **microphone and the system loopback** together, so you get both sides of the
call. It keeps the two sides as separate channels and transcribes the audio in chunks of 8 to 24
seconds as the call runs. Each chunk is cut at a pause where possible. The meeting row shows a
pulsing red dot while it records, and the transcript fills in live as each chunk is transcribed.

</Step>

<Step>

### Finalize to generate the notes

When the call ends, **finalize** the meeting. Ryu turns the accumulated transcript into structured
notes with a model call routed through the Gateway. The notes come back in four parts:

```json
{
  "summary": "a short paragraph",
  "key_points": ["..."],
  "action_items": ["... ideally naming an owner ..."],
  "decisions": ["..."]
}
```

An empty transcript skips the model call entirely. You can choose the notes model, reasoning effort,
and prompt back in **Settings → Meetings**.

</Step>

<Step>

### Open the notes in the Spaces editor

On finalize, Ryu writes the notes and transcript as a document into an auto-created, hidden
**Meetings** Space. The meeting detail's **Open notes** button takes you straight to that document in
the standard [Spaces editor](/docs/surfaces/desktop/user-guide/spaces-memory), so you can edit it like any
other doc. Because it lives in a Space, it is RAG-indexed and searchable for retrieval. A second
meeting reuses the same Space, so over time you build one Space holding all your meeting notes.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Check your work

- While recording, the meeting row shows a pulsing red dot and the transcript fills in live, in
  chunks of 8 to 24 seconds.
- On **finalize**, the notes come back in four parts (`summary`, `key_points`, `action_items`,
  `decisions`). An empty transcript skips the model call entirely.
- **Open notes** takes you to the document in the Spaces editor. Because it lives in a Space, a
  search retrieves it, and a second meeting reuses the same auto-created Meetings Space.
