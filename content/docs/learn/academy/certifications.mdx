A certification shows that you can do a task with Ryu. There are four official credentials, each
mapped to an Academy path. Community certifications are reviewed before they appear in the public
catalog.

<Callout type="info">
  **Current as of October 25, 2026.** The Academy is the free preparation for every official exam.
  Exam rules are in [Taking the exam](#taking-the-exam).
</Callout>

<Callout type="warn">
  **What a certificate shows.** The official exam questions are the public Academy lesson quizzes, so
  the question bank and its answers are readable by anyone. The exams are not proctored and the name
  on a certificate is not identity-verified. A certificate is a completion check that you passed the
  exam on a Ryu account, not proctored proof of skill.
</Callout>

Search and filter the full credential catalog at [ryuhq.com/certifications](https://ryuhq.com/certifications).
You can browse without an account. Sign in when you are ready to start an exam, save progress, or
view an earned certificate.

If the live catalog is unavailable, you can still browse the official preparation cards. Exam
start, continue, and retake actions return when the catalog reconnects.

The [learning progress guide](/docs/learn/academy/learning-progress) explains the difference
between finishing a path, earning a Ryu Academy completion badge, and passing an official exam.

## Certified User

**Prepares with** [Ryu Essentials](/docs/learn/academy/essentials).

For everyone who uses Ryu day to day. No code is required.

You should be able to:

- Explain what Ryu is and why it sits above any engine
- Install Ryu and complete first-run onboarding
- Start a chat, use voice input, and branch a conversation
- Recognize a tool call in a conversation and explain the agent loop

## Certified Operator

**Prepares with** [Operating Ryu](/docs/learn/academy/operator).

For power users who set Ryu up for themselves or a team.

You should be able to:

- Create and edit an agent's setup, including its model, tools, memory, and safety settings
- Browse and install models from the catalog and switch the active model
- Install and activate skills, and understand installed versus active
- Connect tools and reason about an agent's allowlist
- Use Spaces and memory for retrieval, and set up an automation or monitor

## Certified Gateway Admin

**Prepares with** [Governing Ryu](/docs/learn/academy/governing).

For the person who owns model-call policy.

You should be able to:

- Explain the Core versus Gateway split: the Gateway owns model-request policy for routed traffic, while Core owns orchestration and execution access checks
- Configure model routing, including smart classifier-driven routing
- Set up guardrails such as the firewall and PII or DLP controls
- Apply budgets and manage keys with a BYOK vault
- Run evals and read the audit trail

## Certified Builder

**Prepares with** [Building on Ryu](/docs/learn/academy/builder).

For developers who extend the platform.

You should be able to:

- Explain the Runnable contract and how Agent, Workflow, Tool, and Skill unify under it
- Scaffold a project with the SDK and define a Runnable
- Package an app with a manifest and understand install, enable, and disable
- Publish to the marketplace and explain how publishing is governed by the Gateway

## Taking the exam

Exams are hosted on [ryuhq.com](https://ryuhq.com/certifications). Sign in, pick your track, answer
10 questions, and get your result immediately. Each official pool is generated from the current
Academy knowledge checks for that path.

### Before you start

Your account's display name is printed on the certificate. Set your full name in
[Settings](https://ryuhq.com/settings) before you start an exam; an exam cannot be started or
submitted while the name is blank. The exam start screen shows the exact name the certificate will
read. Ryu does not verify that the name is yours, and the verification page says the name is as
entered by the holder. Changing your name later does not update certificates that were already
issued.

You cannot sit, or earn a certificate from, a community certification you authored yourself.

### Exam rules

- **One attempt at a time.** Only one attempt per certification can be in progress. Starting the
  exam again, or choosing **Continue exam** from the catalog, resumes that attempt with the same
  questions it started with.
- **Answers are kept on your device.** Answers you have chosen are saved in this browser until you
  submit, so reloading the page keeps them. They are not synced to other devices.
- **Answer every question.** Submitting with gaps lists the unanswered question numbers and moves
  you to the first one. The server scores any missing answer as zero.
- **Scores are rounded down.** The score is a whole percentage rounded down, so 79.5% is 79% and
  does not pass an 80% threshold.
- **A pass is final.** Once you pass, starting the exam again does not open a new attempt; it points
  you to your existing certificate.
- **Retakes follow the latest failure.** The retake wait counts from your most recent failed
  attempt. Countdowns on the exam page and in the catalog update live and unlock the retake when the
  wait ends, without a reload. Official exams wait 24 hours; a community certification sets its own
  wait, which is never shorter than 24 hours.
- **If the question bank changes mid-attempt.** If a question in your attempt is retired or
  changed before you submit, submission is refused and the exam refreshes. Only the affected
  questions are replaced, each by a question that was not already on your paper; every other
  question keeps its place and your answer to it. This does not count as a failed attempt or start
  a retake wait.
- **If a community certification goes back into review.** While a community certification is under
  review, unlisted, or rejected, an attempt you already started is saved but cannot be resumed or
  submitted. You can continue once it is live again.

### What your result shows

- **After a pass**, the review lists any questions you got wrong, with the correct answer and
  explanation for each. A perfect score has nothing to review.
- **After a failed attempt**, you see your score and the pass mark, and nothing per question. You do
  not see which questions you missed, the correct answers, or the explanations. Showing which questions
  were wrong would let repeated attempts map the question bank. Use the linked course material to
  prepare for the retake. The result screen offers a retake (once the wait has passed), a link to
  all certifications, and the preparation course.
- The result of a completed attempt stays available on that certification's exam page after you
  leave or refresh, with the same rules for what it reveals, even if a community certification is
  later unlisted or rejected. A question that has since been retired or changed is left out of the
  review, so it never pairs your answer with content you were not scored on.
- If your certificate is revoked, the exam page and the catalog show it as revoked instead of
  certified, and the review no longer shows correct answers or explanations.

## Community credentials

Community authors can package lessons and an exam, submit the credential for review, and publish it
to the same searchable catalog. A community credential shows its author, policy, skills, and course
lesson count separately from official Ryu Academy credentials. Ryu reviews published community
content, and a community issuer cannot present itself as Ryu Academy.

If you built the app or plugin the course teaches, use the [app and plugin certification authoring
guide](/docs/learn/academy/certification-authoring).

The **Ryu for Business** path has no official certification. Use the role-based
credential that matches your work, then complete the Business lessons for deployment and multi-node
practice.

## Your certificates

View earned certificates and track your progress at [ryuhq.com/console/certifications](https://ryuhq.com/console/certifications).
Each certification card shows where you are:

- **In progress**, with **Resume exam**;
- **Failed**, with your score and a countdown until you can retake;
- **Retake available**, with **Retake exam**;
- **Earned**, with **View certificate**;
- **Revoked**, with **View certificate**, which opens the verification page and its revocation
  notice.

Each earned certificate offers **Copy link** and **Add to LinkedIn**. Both are described in
[Certificate verification](#certificate-verification). The issuer is Ryu for official certificates
and the named author for community certificates.

## Certificate verification

Every certificate has a verification page at `ryuhq.com/cert/<id>`. Anyone with the link can open
it without signing in, and link previews work when you paste it into chat or social apps. Because
the page shows the holder's name, it is not indexed by search engines; share the link directly.

When you view your own certificate while signed in, the page also offers share actions. Other
visitors do not see them.

- **Add to LinkedIn** opens LinkedIn's own form for adding a license or certification to your profile. It fills in the credential name, the issuer, the issue month and year, the verification URL, and the certificate ID. You review and save the entry on LinkedIn.
- **Share on LinkedIn** and **Share on X** post the verification link.
- **Image, video or GIF** exports the certificate as a card showing the holder, the credential, the score, the issue date, and the issuer.
- **Copy link** copies the verification URL.

When the link is posted, the preview image shows the same certificate details. A community credential names its own issuer and is marked as verified on Ryu. It is never shown as issued by Ryu Academy.

The verification page is always fetched fresh, so a revoked certificate stops showing as verified
as soon as it is revoked. It also states that the holder's name is as entered by the holder and was
not identity-verified.

An unknown or malformed certificate link shows a standard "not found" page. If verification is
temporarily unavailable, the page says so instead of reporting the certificate as missing.

### Revoked certificates

Ryu can revoke a certificate that was issued in error or obtained through misconduct. Only Ryu
administrators can revoke a certificate. Its verification page then shows it as **Revoked**, with
the revocation date and a category (issued in error, misconduct, or withdrawn by the issuer),
instead of verified. Free-text notes an administrator records stay internal and are never published.
The holder's name and the credential stay visible so the record remains checkable. A revoked holder cannot sit that certification again.

Unlisting or rejecting a community certification never revokes certificates that were already
issued.
