title: Learning progress

Ryu Academy is open to everyone. You can browse paths, read lessons, open the real Ryu surface, and use every knowledge check without an account.

When you want Ryu to remember where you are, use [My learning](https://ryuhq.com/academy/learn). Sign in, open a path, and mark a lesson complete after you have worked through it. Progress is saved to your Ryu account rather than to a single browser, so you can return from another device.

## The learner loop

1. Choose a path in the [Academy catalog](https://ryuhq.com/academy/catalog).
2. Open its native path view to see outcomes, prerequisites, practice, and the ordered lessons.
3. Read the lesson in Fumadocs, use its **Open in Ryu** action when one is available, and complete the knowledge check.
4. Return to the path view and mark the lesson complete.
5. Continue from the next lesson in [My learning](https://ryuhq.com/academy/learn).

The native path view is where progress lives. Fumadocs remains the public lesson and reference surface, so a lesson can be read directly or reached from the path sequence.

## Completion badges are not certifications

When every lesson in a path is marked complete, Ryu shows a **Ryu Academy completion badge** in your learner dashboard and on that path. The badge means you finished the published learning sequence; it is not an external accreditation and it does not issue an exam certificate.

Official certifications are a separate step. A credential-bearing path prepares you for a server-scored exam with its own question pool, passing threshold, retake policy, and public certificate verification. Start from the [certifications guide](/docs/learn/academy/certifications) when you are ready.

## Reopening a lesson

Progress is reversible. If you want to revisit a lesson, choose **Reopen lesson** on the path view. The lesson becomes part of your remaining sequence again, while your existing exam attempts and certificates stay unchanged.

If progress is temporarily unavailable, the public course and lesson content remain readable. Retry after the service reconnects; a failed mutation is rolled back rather than displayed as saved.
