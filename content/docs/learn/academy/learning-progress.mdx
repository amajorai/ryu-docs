title: Learning Progress

Ryu Academy is open to everyone. You can browse paths, read lessons, open the real Ryu surface, and use every knowledge check without an account.

To save your place, use [My learning](https://ryuhq.com/academy/learn). Sign in, open a path, and mark a lesson complete when you finish it. Progress is saved to your Ryu account, not to one browser, so you can continue on another device.

If you [merge two Ryu accounts](/docs/billing/merging-accounts), lesson progress, exam attempts, and certificates from both come across to the account you keep. If both accounts have an exam in progress for the same certification, the attempt you started first is kept.

If you are signed out, the path view shows **Sign in to save progress** under the progress bar and in place of each lesson's **Mark complete** button. Signing in returns you to the same path.

## The learner loop

1. Choose a path in the [Academy catalog](https://ryuhq.com/academy/catalog).
2. Open its native path view to see outcomes, prerequisites, the ordered lessons, and each lesson's practice task (**Try it**).
3. Read the lesson in these docs, use its **Open in Ryu** action when one is available, and complete the knowledge check.
4. Follow the **Mark this lesson complete on the path view** link at the end of the lesson. It opens the path view at that lesson, where you mark it complete.
5. Continue from the next lesson. The path view has a **Continue** link to your next lesson, and each path card in [My learning](https://ryuhq.com/academy/learn) opens the path view at that lesson.

Progress is stored in the native path view. Lessons stay public in these docs, so you can read a lesson directly or reach it from the path sequence.

## What My learning shows

Once your progress loads, [My learning](https://ryuhq.com/academy/learn) summarizes it:

- **Stats.** **Overall progress** shows the percentage of all Academy lessons you have completed,
  with the lesson count under it. **Time left** adds up the estimated minutes of the lessons you
  have not finished. **Paths complete** counts finished paths, and **Last active** shows the date
  of your most recent lesson completion.
- **Continue where you left off.** A card points to the next lesson in the unfinished path you
  worked on most recently, with its estimated time. It does not appear before you complete a lesson
  or once every path you started is finished.
- **Path groups.** Path cards are grouped under **In progress**, **Not started**, and
  **Completed**. In-progress paths are ordered by your most recent activity. Each unfinished card
  shows the lessons and minutes left, or the path's total minutes if you have not started it.
- **Learning streak.** **Current streak** counts the consecutive days, ending today, on which you
  completed at least one lesson. If you have not completed a lesson yet today, yesterday's streak
  still counts until the day ends. **Longest streak** is your best run. A calendar of the last 12
  weeks shows how many lessons you completed each day, Monday to Sunday, with the totals written out
  underneath. Days follow your browser's time zone.
- **Recent activity.** Your five most recent lesson completions, newest first, each with its path
  and completion date and a link back to the lesson on the path view.
- **Exam attempts.** Your 20 most recent certification exam attempts, newest first, with the
  certification, status (**In progress**, **Passed**, **Failed**, or **Certificate revoked**), score
  and pass mark, and the date you started or submitted. Passed attempts link to their certificate.
  Only your newest attempt for each certification offers **Resume exam** or **Retake the exam**
  (once the wait has passed); during the wait that row shows the date a retake becomes available.
  Older attempts are kept as a record. The list never shows questions or your answers.

On the path view, each completed lesson shows the date you completed it next to **Reopen lesson**.
Dates use the language you have selected on the Ryu site and your browser's time zone.

## Completion badges are not certifications

When every lesson in a path is marked complete, Ryu shows one **Ryu Academy completion badge** on that path's learner-dashboard card and in the path's progress summary. The badge means you finished the published lessons. It is not an external accreditation, and it does not issue an exam certificate.

Official certifications are a separate step. A credential-bearing path prepares you for a server-scored exam with its own question pool, passing threshold, retake policy, and public certificate verification. Start from the [certifications guide](/docs/learn/academy/certifications) when you are ready.

Each credential-bearing path card in My learning links to its certification and reflects your exam status:

- **Prepares for** the certification while you work through the path and have not started the exam.
- **Take the** certification **exam** once every lesson is complete.
- **Resume the** certification **exam** when an attempt is in progress.
- **Retake available** with a date while you wait after a failed attempt, then **Retake the** certification **exam** once the wait has passed.
- **View your** certification **certificate** once you pass, with a **Certified** badge on the card. The link opens the certificate's verification page.
- **Certificate revoked** if the certificate was revoked. The card no longer shows **Certified**.

Finishing the path does not take the exam for you. If exam status cannot load, My learning says **Certification status unavailable** with a **Retry** link, and the cards fall back to **Prepares for** and **Take the exam** based on your lesson progress.

## Reopening a lesson

To revisit a lesson, choose **Reopen lesson** on the path view. The lesson returns to your remaining sequence. Your exam attempts and certificates do not change.

Only lessons in the current Academy catalog can be marked complete. Progress for a lesson that
was retired stays visible and can still be reset.

## Resetting a path

To start a path over, choose **Reset path progress** under the path's progress summary on the path
view. It appears once you have completed at least one lesson in that path. Ryu asks you to confirm
how many lessons will be reopened; choose **Reset** to reopen all of them, or **Cancel** to keep
your progress.

Resetting a path only clears that path's lesson completions and their dates. Other paths, your
exam attempts, and your certificates do not change. If the reset fails, the path view says so and
your progress stays as it was.

## Downloading your learning data

Once you are signed in, the **Your learning data** section at the bottom of
[My learning](https://ryuhq.com/academy/learn) has a **Download my learning data** button. It saves
a JSON file of your Academy record:

- **Your account**: your account ID, name, and email address.
- **Completed lessons**, each with its path, lesson, and completion time.
- **Exam attempt summaries**, each with the certification, status, score, start and finish times,
  and the time a retake becomes available.
- **Certificates**, each with the credential, score, issue date, issuer, the holder name and email
  printed on it, its badge image, status (valid or revoked, with the revocation date and
  category), and its verification link.

Because the file contains your name and email address, check who you share it with.

The file does not include your individual exam answers or the questions you were given, and it
does not include internal notes made when a certificate is revoked. Certification courses you
author are not part of your learner record and are not included. Downloading does not change your
progress, attempts, or certificates. If your session has expired, the section asks you to sign in
again.

## When progress cannot load

The public course and lesson content stay readable when progress is temporarily unavailable. My
learning and the path view say that progress could not be loaded:

- Choose **Retry** to load it again. Until it loads, stats show a dash instead of zeros and **Mark complete** is unavailable.
- If your session has expired, choose **Sign in again**.
- If only your exam attempts cannot load, My learning says **Exam history unavailable** with a
  **Retry** button. The rest of the page still works. When the retry succeeds, focus moves to the
  **Exam attempts** heading.

A save that fails is rolled back rather than displayed as saved.
