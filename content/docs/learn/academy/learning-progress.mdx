title: Learning Progress
description: Save lessons, continue a path, and understand how Academy completion badges relate to official certifications.
---

Ryu Academy is open to everyone. You can browse paths, read lessons, open the real Ryu surface, and use every knowledge check without an account.

To save your place, use [My learning](https://ryuhq.com/academy/learn). Sign in, open a path, and mark a lesson complete when you finish it. Progress is saved to your Ryu account, not to one browser, so you can continue on another device.

If you are signed out, the path view shows **Sign in to save progress** under the progress bar and in place of each lesson's **Mark complete** button. Signing in returns you to the same path.

## The learner loop

1. Choose a path in the [Academy catalog](https://ryuhq.com/academy/catalog).
2. Open its native path view to see outcomes, prerequisites, the ordered lessons, and each lesson's practice task (**Try it**).
3. Read the lesson in these docs, use its **Open in Ryu** action when one is available, and complete the knowledge check.
4. Follow the **Mark this lesson complete on the path view** link at the end of the lesson. It opens the path view at that lesson, where you mark it complete.
5. Continue from the next lesson. The path view has a **Continue** link to your next lesson, and each path card in [My learning](https://ryuhq.com/academy/learn) opens the path view at that lesson.

Progress is stored in the native path view. Lessons stay public in these docs, so you can read a lesson directly or reach it from the path sequence.

## Completion badges are not certifications

When every lesson in a path is marked complete, Ryu shows one **Ryu Academy completion badge** on that path's learner-dashboard card and in the path's progress summary. The badge means you finished the published lessons. It is not an external accreditation, and it does not issue an exam certificate.

Official certifications are a separate step. A credential-bearing path prepares you for a server-scored exam with its own question pool, passing threshold, retake policy, and public certificate verification. Start from the [certifications guide](/docs/learn/academy/certifications) when you are ready.

Each credential-bearing path card in My learning links to its certification. It reads **Prepares for** the certification while you work through the path, and changes to **Take the exam** once every lesson is complete. Finishing the path does not take the exam for you.

## Reopening a lesson

To revisit a lesson, choose **Reopen lesson** on the path view. The lesson returns to your remaining sequence. Your exam attempts and certificates do not change.

If progress is temporarily unavailable, the public course and lesson content remain readable. My learning and the path view say that progress could not be loaded and offer a **Retry** button; until it loads, stats show a dash instead of zeros and **Mark complete** is unavailable. If your session has expired, choose **Sign in again**. A save that fails is rolled back rather than displayed as saved.
