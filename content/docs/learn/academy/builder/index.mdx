This track is for developers who extend Ryu. You build agents, workflows, tools, and skills, bundle them into installable apps, and publish them to the Marketplace. Every lesson builds on one idea, the Runnable contract.

## Who this is for

- Developers who want to ship agents, tools, or workflows on Ryu.
- People building plugins or apps to share with others.
- Developers who want model calls to go through the Gateway without wiring it by hand.

## What you will learn

- How Ryu pieces fit together and why the kinds you author share one shape.
- How to scaffold and run a project with the Ryu SDK.
- How to bundle Runnables into an installable app (plugin).
- How to publish that app to the federated Marketplace.
