## What it does

Ryu includes nine output styles: ELI5, I have ADHD, Explanatory, Learning, Proactive, Plain text, No AI slop, Bro, and Gen Z. A style is appended to or replaces the agent's base instructions for the turn. It changes the agent's role, tone, and default response shape, but not its knowledge, tools, or model. Pick a style in the composer or browse the list in the Store's Output Styles tab.
