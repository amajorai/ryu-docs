---
title: "Free-tier limits"
description: "The numeric caps enforced on the free baseline and what Teams and Business include."
---

Numeric caps apply on Ryu's managed free path. A fully self-hosted deployment
does not inherit these commercial caps: the operator owns the machine and may
set its own safety limits. Teams seats are separate from the desktop agent
count: they are organization members who can use the shared workspace, while
nodes provide runtime capacity.

Personal managed plans remain supported for existing subscriptions through their
recorded pricing versions, but they are not part of the current public offer.

## Numeric caps

| Limit | Free (managed) | Teams | Business | Enterprise |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Open tabs | **3** | Org policy | Contracted |
| Agents created in the app | **3** | Org policy | Contracted |
| Spaces | **1** | Org policy | Contracted |
| Concurrent background runs | **1** | **8** | Org policy | Contracted |
| Eval runs / month | **10** | Org policy | Contracted |
| Space storage | **1 GB** | **50 GB** | Org policy | Contracted |
| Remote nodes | **1** | Org policy | **1 performance node** | Contracted |

## Teams seats

Teams starts with five member seats at $250/month. Additional seats are
$50/month each through 50 seats. The shared AI-credit pool is $50 at five billed
seats and increases by $50 for every additional five billed seats. Private
negotiated seats increase member capacity only. Enterprise capacity is written
into the organization's agreement. Business starts at $300/month for five
member seats, uses the same $50 marginal seat price, and grants $100 of pooled
credits at five seats plus $100 per additional five-seat bundle. See [Teams
seats](/docs/billing/seats) for seat lifecycle enforcement.

## What the caps mean

- **Open tabs** — simultaneously open desktop chat tabs.
- **Agents** — agents created in the app; conversations are not counted here.
- **Spaces** — collaborative workspaces with shared context.
- **Concurrent background runs** — agents that may run at once in the background.
- **Space storage** — files, images, and indexes stored in spaces.
- **Remote nodes** — managed runtime nodes attached to the organization.

Marketplace apps, plugins, skills, MCP servers, workflows, monitors, meetings, and
their catalog prices are not plan quotas. Their install and runtime paths use the
same signed-package and node-lifecycle checks for free and paid listings.

The free baseline cannot top up managed credits. Teams receives a $50 monthly
shared pool at five billed seats, with another $50 at each additional five-seat
bundle. Business receives $100 at five seats and another $100 per bundle;
Enterprise limits are set by contract.
