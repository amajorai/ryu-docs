<Callout type="warn">
  During the waitlist beta the desktop caps are off: the `billing.desktop_trial` rollout flag is
  off by default, so there is no tab or remote node limit. The desktop caps below apply once it
  is turned on. Hosted storage, mail, and Box limits apply now.
</Callout>

Numeric caps apply on Ryu's managed free path. A fully self-hosted deployment
does not inherit these commercial caps. The operator owns the machine and may
set its own safety limits. Teams seats are separate from the desktop agent
count. A seat is an organization member who can use the shared workspace; cloud
computers provide runtime capacity.

Pro and Max are public individual managed plans when the individual shelf is
available. Pro includes **10 GB** of Space storage and Max **50 GB**. The
desktop license includes **20 GB**, and A Major Pass keeps the **1 GB** free
baseline.
Separately issued private individual plans keep the same personal quota
boundary, fixed by the pricing version recorded at subscription time; the
organization rows below describe the shared Teams and Business offer.

## Numeric caps

| Limit | Free (managed) | Teams | Business | Enterprise |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Open tabs | **3** | Org policy | Org policy | Contracted |
| Agents created in the app | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Spaces | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Concurrent background runs | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Eval runs / month | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Space storage | **1 GB** | **50 GB** | **50 GB** | Contracted |
| Remote nodes | **1** | Unlimited | Unlimited | Contracted |
| Included cloud computers | None | **1** always-on server | **1** always-on server | Contracted |
| Running Ryu Boxes | **1** | **10** | **20** | Contracted |

Running Ryu Boxes counts the Boxes an organization has running, starting, or
stopping at once, including conversation session Boxes; stopped Boxes do not
count. Pro runs **2** and Max **4**. A paid plan in
its free trial runs **1** until the trial converts. A create or resume over the
limit is refused until another Box stops. See
[Ryu Box pricing](/docs/standalone/box#ryu-capacity-pricing).

The cloud-computer row describes capacity, not purchase permission; see
[Who can buy or upgrade](/docs/billing/cloud-computers#who-can-buy-or-upgrade).

Agents, simultaneous runs, and the number of Spaces have no plan-based caps, including on the
free desktop. Managed AI still requires its execution entitlement and available credits; hosted
storage and computer capacity retain their separate limits. Runtime safety settings can also
restrict concurrency.

## Free local desktop features

Prompt Studio, evals, GraphRAG, the Companion overlay, and Gateway governance UI are free.
They require no desktop license or active trial. Eval runs have no monthly plan cap.
Model calls use your configured local model or provider; managed AI still requires paid access
and available credits. Gateway authorization and operator safety policies still apply.

## Desktop upgrade at the tab limit

On the managed free desktop path, opening a fourth tab offers the desktop license while keeping
your existing tabs available. The license unlocks unlimited tabs; it does not include managed AI,
monthly credits, or hosting. A fully self-hosted deployment retains its existing uncapped behavior.

## Teams seats

Teams and Business plans have a five-seat minimum, and the shared AI-credit pool grows with
the number of billed seats. Prices and pool sizes are on [Plans and Pricing](/docs/billing/plans#teams) and
[Business](/docs/billing/plans#business). Private negotiated seats increase member capacity only.
Enterprise capacity is written into the organization's agreement. See
[Teams seats](/docs/billing/seats) for seat lifecycle enforcement.

## What the caps mean

| Cap | What it counts |
|---|---|
| Open tabs | Simultaneously open desktop chat tabs |
| Agents | Agents created in the app; conversations are not counted |
| Spaces | Collaborative workspaces with shared context |
| Concurrent background runs | Agents that may run at once in the background |
| Space storage | Files, images, and indexes stored in Spaces |
| Remote nodes | Remote nodes connected to the desktop app |
| Included cloud computers | Managed runtime computers the plan includes for the organization |

Marketplace apps, plugins, skills, MCP servers, workflows, monitors, meetings, and
their catalog prices are not plan quotas. Their install and runtime paths use the
same signed-package and server-lifecycle checks for free and paid listings; a paid
listing additionally requires a purchase or a plan that includes it.

The free baseline has no included monthly pool, but owners and admins can still
buy prepaid credits without a managed plan. Those top-ups use the base deposit
fee, and automatic top-up still requires an active managed plan; see
[Credits](/docs/billing/credits). Enterprise limits are set by contract.
