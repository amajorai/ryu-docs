The **referrals page** doubles as a battle pass: a season of quests with a progress tracker, grouped by how often they reset. The waitlist page exposes the same quests in its own **Quests** tab and shows your current points balance there. Completing quests earns **points** or **pool-restricted credits**, and points are spent in the **rewards store**.

## Quests

Every quest has a **target** (the number of the thing it takes), a **reward**, and a cadence that decides when it resets:

| Cadence | What it means | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Today** (daily) | Check in once per UTC calendar day | Daily check-in |
| **Get started** (one-time) | Claim once, ever | Download and open the Ryu desktop app, connect your X account, join the Discord, link your Discord account |
| **This week** (weekly) | Resets every calendar week | Post on X, share what you're doing, reach out to people, reply to your mentions |
| **This month** (monthly) | Resets every calendar month | Refer 3 friends this month, refer 5 friends this month |
| **Always on** (permanent) | Repeatable — claim again whenever you meet it | Refer one more friend |

### How completion is detected

- **Auto-verified quests** are detected from data Ryu already trusts. The desktop-app quest is completed when the signed-in desktop client opens and sends its one-time event; downloading alone is not treated as proof until the app has actually launched. Referral quests count the accounts that signed up through your link in the current window — the same numbers your referral dashboard shows, so nothing to submit.
- **Submit-verified quests** (posting, outreach, connections) ask you to submit a public link as proof. A human reviews it before the reward becomes claimable, the same way the "post about Ryu" campaign works.

## Referrals and free credits

A valid signup through an `/r/<code>` referral link awards referral points to
both people: the inviter and the new member. The award is added once to each
person’s points balance, even if the referral is retried while the new member
finishes verification.

The invite page calls out the current $10 referral credit and points directly.
The inviter’s referral count is what moves them up the waitlist queue; the new
member’s referral points are tracked in their Battle Pass balance.

The invite CTA keeps the reward details in the copy beside the action: the
credit and points are described on the left, inside one animated smoky
**Join the waitlist** button. On a narrow screen, the action label stays inside
the same full-width button below the copy.

The new member’s free credit comes from the current referral offer. It is a
separate offer from the `/card` campaign, so visiting a referral link does not
silently claim two credit campaigns.

The signed-in desktop app, browser extension, mobile app, and CLI each support
the referral sync event. It asks the control plane to reconcile the current
account’s authoritative referral attribution, so a delayed verification can
repair the referral points award without relying on a client-supplied count or
referral id. Referral progress for the inviter still comes from the server’s
`CreditReferral` rows.

Being on the waitlist does not by itself block the `/card` offer. A waitlisted
member can still claim the active card campaign when the normal claim conditions
are met, such as a verified email, an organization, and remaining campaign
budget.

### Scanning a Ryu business card

The QR code on a printed Ryu business card opens `/card`, which routes to the
current card offer. The landing page shows the same card as an interactive
digital object: the front and back artwork are preserved, and you can drag the
card to turn it over. The card preview is presentation only; claim the active
offer below it when your account is eligible. The claim action uses the same
high-contrast card treatment: the current credit amount and account note sit
inside one animated smoky **Claim credits** button, with the action label below
the copy on small screens and beside it on larger screens.

### Rewards

Quests pay in one of two forms:

- **Points** — added to your points balance, spendable in the rewards store.
- **Ryu Credits** — a pool-restricted grant, placed into the wallet of an organization **you choose**. When you claim a credit reward, a dialog asks which org should receive it; the default is your personal org.

## Points

Points are the battle pass's own currency. You earn them from usage, streaks, milestones, referrals, and quests. Points **are** the thing the rewards store exists for — they do not expire, and they are not the same as Ryu Credits.

When the waitlist reaches 100 people, its Quests tab also exposes a **Leaderboard** tab. It shows the first 100 waitlist members by points, with trophies for the top three and a generated placeholder avatar when a member has no profile image. The referrals page does not show this leaderboard.

## Rewards store

Points are spent in the rewards store, which offers:

- **Provider credits** — Ryu Credits in fixed amounts ($5, $25), spendable on Ryu's cloud models.
- **Desktop access, plan time, and node time** — entitlement grants (a month of desktop access, a month of Pro, or a month of managed node time). These cost a lot more points than credits, by design.

Credit rewards from the store land in the org you choose, exactly like quest credit rewards.

<Callout type="info">
Points are a reward, not a refund. Nothing here is cash or withdrawable — points buy store items, and quest rewards are Ryu Credits spendable on Ryu's cloud models.
</Callout>
