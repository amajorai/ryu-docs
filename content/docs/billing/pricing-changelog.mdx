---
title: "Pricing versions"
description: "How Ryu keeps agreed prices and included credits stable when the public offer changes."
---

Ryu versions pricing instead of silently rewriting it. The price and included
credit pool agreed at checkout are recorded for the organization. A later
public change applies to new subscriptions; it does not reduce or reprice an
existing contracted organization.

Personal managed plans remain supported for existing customers under their
recorded pricing versions, but are currently hidden from the public pricing
shelf and are not public checkout options.

## How future changes work

The managed free starter caps are intentionally smaller than the original
pilot values: 3 tabs, 3 agents, and 3 workflows. Fully self-hosted deployments
remain uncapped because the operator owns the infrastructure.

When a future snapshot changes pricing or the included pool:

- **Existing price stays pinned.** The amount agreed by an existing organization
  remains its contracted price.
- **Existing credits stay pinned.** The included pool that price was designed to
  fund remains attached to that organization.
- **New customers get the new package.** A higher public price can fund a larger
  included pool, more capacity, or more support for new organizations without
  taking anything away from the earlier cohort.
- **The change is explained before checkout.** The new price, pool, and effective
  date are visible before a new organization commits.

This is the practical founding-rate promise: joining earlier can create a
lasting economic benefit, while later customers receive a package designed for
the costs and capacity of their cohort.
