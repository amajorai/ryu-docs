title: "Pricing Versions"
description: "How Ryu keeps agreed prices and included credits stable when the public offer changes."
---

Ryu versions its pricing. The price and included credit pool agreed at checkout are recorded
for the organization. A later
public change applies to new subscriptions; it does not reduce or reprice an
existing contracted organization.

## How future changes work

Pricing versions cover plan prices and included pools. Free-tier caps are listed on
[free-tier limits](/docs/billing/limits).

When a future snapshot changes pricing or the included pool:

- **Existing price stays pinned.** The amount agreed by an existing organization
  remains its contracted price.
- **Existing credits stay pinned.** The included pool that price was designed to
  fund remains attached to that organization.
- **New customers get the new package.** A higher public price can fund a larger
  included pool, more capacity, or more support for new organizations. The earlier
  cohort keeps its recorded package.
- **The change is explained before checkout.** The new price, pool, and effective
  date are visible before a new organization commits.
