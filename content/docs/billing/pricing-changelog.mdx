Plan pricing is **versioned** and **grandfathered**. The price and included credit pool you buy are pinned the moment you subscribe. A later price change never moves a plan you already hold.

## How future changes work

When a future version changes pricing:

- **Your price is pinned.** Polar records the amount you agreed to at checkout, so a later list-price change never touches a subscription you already hold.
- **Your included pool is pinned too.** You keep the pool your price was meant to fund. A later, larger pool does not reach you, and you are not reduced to a smaller one.
- **New versions reach new sign-ups.** A version bump applies to subscriptions created after the change, not to existing customers.
