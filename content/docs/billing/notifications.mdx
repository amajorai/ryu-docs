---
title: "Billing notifications and the Ryu inbox"
description: "The billing, payment, balance, subscription, invitation, and in-app notifications Ryu sends."
---

Ryu sends transactional billing email to organization owners and admins when an important
payment or credit event completes. The messages use the same organization Billing link and
plain, copyable links as the rest of Ryu's account email.

The same account-scoped events also appear in the website **Inbox**, alongside product
announcements and received organization invitations. Open **Inbox** from the portal header or
the account menu to review everything that needs your attention without searching through email.

## Website inbox

The inbox has three streams:

| Stream | What appears there |
|---|---|
| **Marketing** | Active product announcements published by Ryu administrators |
| **Transactional** | Billing, balance, subscription, budget, transfer, and organization workflow events for the personal account or shared organization |
| **Invitations** | Pending organization invitations received by the signed-in account |

Rows are stored server-side and scoped to the signed-in user. Provider retries and source
reconciliation use stable dedupe keys, so reopening the page does not create duplicate rows. Marking
a row or category read clears the unread badge and keeps announcement read state in sync with the
desktop surface; invitation actions continue through the existing organization invitation flow.

The email switches in **Organization → Notifications** control email delivery only. They do not
remove the organization’s transactional events from the website inbox, so a billing owner can
pause email and still review the account history in Ryu.

For authenticated API clients, the website inbox is available through the control-plane server:

| Method + path | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `GET /api/inbox` | List the caller’s rows and category counts |
| `GET /api/inbox/unread-count` | Read the portal badge counts |
| `POST /api/inbox/:id/read` | Mark one caller-owned row as read |
| `POST /api/inbox/read-all` | Mark all rows, or one `category`, as read |

Organization workflow notifications are written by the server for the owner and admins of each
organization involved. This applies to managed-node transfers, credit transfers, Marketplace
listing transfers, and organization-invitation lifecycle changes. A transfer between two
organizations therefore reaches the owner/admin roster on both sides; a member invitation also
keeps the invited person on the invitation email flow.

## What you'll receive

| Moment | What the email tells you |
|---|---|
| Manual top-up succeeds | How much credit was added and what the charge was |
| Automatic top-up succeeds | That the configured balance pack was charged and added |
| Balance crosses the low-balance threshold | The current balance, threshold, and where to top up |
| Subscription starts | That the plan is active and included monthly credits are ready |
| Subscription renews | That the recurring payment completed and the next period is ready |
| Payment becomes past due | How to update the payment method, the grace-period action, and what can pause if payment is not recovered |
| Subscription is revoked | That managed plan features and included capacity are paused, with the path to restore access |
| Managed node transfer starts, is accepted, completes, declines, is cancelled, or expires | The current hand-off state and the organization-specific page to review it |
| Credit transfer completes | The amount moved and the billing page for the organization side |
| Marketplace listing transfer is requested, approved/rejected, completed, cancelled, or expires | The listing transfer state and the Marketplace page for the organization side |
| Organization invitation is sent, accepted, declined, or cancelled | The invitation state and the members/invitations page for the relevant recipient |

Low-balance notifications follow the recipients and threshold configured in organization Billing.
The other payment confirmations and subscription recovery notices go to the organization's
owners and admins so a billing issue has someone responsible to act on it.

## Organization controls

Organization owners and admins can open **Organization → Notifications** to control these
transactional emails as a group. Every switch is enabled by default, including for existing
organizations; a missing setting is treated as enabled so adding a new notification does not
silently change delivery. Organization members can view the switches but cannot change them.

The **Organization activity** switch covers node, credit, and Marketplace transfers plus
invitation status confirmations. The **Organization invitations** switch covers the invitation
email sent to the invited person and the corresponding organization-side notifications. Turning a
switch off pauses email only; the server-side transactional inbox rows remain available to the
owner/admin recipients.

This page is separate from personal account preferences and from a server's **Email & alerts**
delivery settings. The low-balance switch controls whether the event can send; its threshold and
recipient list remain configured on **Organization Billing**.

## Organization invitations

Invitation email is account-aware:

- Someone who already has a Ryu account gets a direct invitation link and is asked to sign in
  before accepting.
- Someone new to Ryu gets a signup link that returns them to the invitation after account
  creation.

## If a payment needs attention

Update the payment method from **Organization Billing** while the account is in its grace period.
Ryu waits for the payment provider to recover the subscription; if the provider marks it
terminal, managed runs and included plan capacity can pause. A managed cloud instance follows
its paid-period and grace-window removal process rather than being removed immediately from an
email event.

Duplicate provider callbacks are suppressed by the delivery record, so retrying a webhook does
not normally send the same billing email twice.
