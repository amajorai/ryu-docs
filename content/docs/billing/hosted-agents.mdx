---
title: "Hosted Agent Deployments"
description: "Shared AI-agent deployments with Teams, Business, and Enterprise plans."
---

A hosted agent deployment gives an organization one shared agent workspace on
Teams, Business, or Enterprise. Each agent owns a named process, such as
document intake, working-paper preparation, lead follow-up, or translation
review, with its instructions, tools, files, and approval points.

Start with a workflow app, then ask Ryu to change its form, approval rule, view,
or integration. Ryu presents that change as a proposal, and an authorized person
publishes it. Seats measure the people who operate and review the deployment, not
the number of apps the organization creates.

## The public offer

Teams and Business are self-serve organization plans. Business has a larger
pooled grant. Enterprise is a contracted plan for organizations that need custom
capacity, deployment, or procurement terms. Prices, seat minimums, and included
AI pools are on [Plans and Pricing](/docs/billing/plans#teams).

Personal managed plans are separate individual subscriptions. Pro and Max are
shown on the Individual pricing shelf for one person who wants Ryu-managed AI.
Separately issued private individual offers use the same personal ownership
boundary. Neither grants shared organization process capacity. A business process used
by multiple people belongs on the Teams, Business, or Enterprise path.

Qualified organizations may receive a private paid pilot. Its organization-scoped discount
code is entered on the hosted checkout page for monthly Teams billing. It is not a
public checkout tier.

## What a Teams seat means

A seat is a person with access to the shared organization workspace. A seat
does not give that person a separate AI wallet, and it does not assign one agent
to one person.

The seat price and the way the included pool grows with billed seats are on
[Plans and Pricing](/docs/billing/plans#teams). Private negotiated seats may expand
member capacity without adding credits or changing the billed quantity; see
[Teams Seats](/docs/billing/seats#billed-seats-and-negotiated-capacity).
[Business](/docs/billing/plans#business) keeps the same roles, wallet, audit history,
and seat meaning as Teams, with a larger pooled grant.

## Organization boundary

The subscription and seat quantity belong to the selected organization. Members
share the organization's access controls, wallet, budgets, and audit history.
Teams and Business support up to 50 active organization members. Organizations with
51 or more active members need an Enterprise agreement.

See [Teams seats](/docs/billing/seats) for invitation, acceptance, upgrade,
downgrade, and proration rules.

## Multi-tenant boundary

Ryu runs a pooled control plane for many organizations, but an organization is
the tenant boundary. Every hosted request carries an organization identity, and
the server checks membership and role before reading or changing organization
data. Billing, seat quantity, workflow capacity, wallets, spend controls, audit history,
files, mail, and Gateway credentials are scoped to that organization.

Background work carries the same organization identity instead of relying on a
browser's active tab. Queries and service-to-service calls fail closed when the
organization is missing, and the organization id is never taken from an
untrusted cross-tenant request body.

## Cloud computers and isolation

Cloud computers are runtime capacity, not the commercial allowance. Teams and
Business each get one organization-specific Ryu server at every seat count; the
provider's underlying shared-vCPU hardware is not a shared Ryu tenant VM. Ryu
can resize or replace a computer without changing the organization's contracted
seat quantity. Enterprise can use dedicated or self-hosted deployment when a
stronger infrastructure boundary is required.

Local model execution is off by default on shared hosted capacity. If you
self-host Ryu, you control the machine and can enable local inference according
to your own resource and isolation choices.

Which plans can buy, resize, or move cloud computers, and what happens when a
server is out of stock, is on [Cloud Computers](/docs/billing/cloud-computers).

## Adding people

When another person needs access, an owner or billing manager adds a billed
seat in the organization billing screen. The slider shows the monthly total and
routes organizations above the self-serve limit to Enterprise. See
[Teams Seats](/docs/billing/seats#adding-and-removing-members).
