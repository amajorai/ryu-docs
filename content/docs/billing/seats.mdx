---
title: "Teams seats"
description: "How Polar bills and manages organization seats while Ryu protects workspace access."
---

Polar owns the commercial seat contract: quantity, seat assignments, claim and
revocation state, invoices, and proration. Ryu synchronizes those events into
the organization billing view and remains the authority for authentication,
workspace membership, and Ryu-specific roles and permissions.

Teams and Business are billed by organization member seat:

- **$50 per seat/month**;
- **five seats minimum**, so the monthly floor is **$250**;
- **$500 per seat/year** when billed annually, with five seats minimum;
- self-serve up to **50 seats**; larger organizations use Enterprise;
- one shared AI-credit pool: **$50/month at five billed seats, plus $50 when
  the quantity crosses each additional five-seat bundle** (10 billed seats
  includes $100/month);

Business uses the same human-seat boundary with a premium graduated floor:

- **$300/month for five seats**;
- **$50/month for each additional seat**;
- **$3,000/year for five seats** when billed annually, then $500/year per
  additional seat;
- one shared AI-credit pool: **$100/month at five through nine billed seats,
  plus $100 at each completed five-seat bundle**;
- one included performance managed server.

Business is available to eligible organizations of any size, including fewer
than 25 members. It is a premium option and price anchor, not a different seat
meaning or an AI-automation-user allowance.

The seat is the person who can access the shared organization workspace. It is
not a separate credit wallet, server, workflow allowance, or one-to-one agent
assignment. Credits remain pooled so the organization can decide how to use its
capacity.

## Billed seats and negotiated capacity

Polar stores the **billed seat quantity** and uses it for the invoice and seat
benefits. Ryu reads that quantity for included-credit and server-sizing
projections. Ryu may also record a private, support-approved
**negotiated seat allowance** for a specific organization. For example, five
billed seats plus five negotiated seats lets ten members use the workspace while
the organization still pays the five-seat $250/month floor.

Negotiated seats are capacity-only. They do not add another $50 credit bundle,
change the server band, or become a public discount. The allowance can have an
expiry date and is checked by the server at invitation and acceptance time. This
keeps the offer auditable: the invoice remains five seats, the effective member
capacity is ten, and the shared included pool remains $50/month.

## Personal workspace conversion

The automatically created personal workspace has one member and no invitation
path. An owner with a consumer mailbox should change and verify that address on
the same Ryu account first. A completed company-email change moves the existing
workspace into the Teams boundary; buying the five-seat minimum then activates
the paid plan after Polar confirms payment. A legacy personal workspace whose
owner already has a company email is promoted by the same paid webhook. Existing
data and the organization wallet are not migrated or copied.

Teams requires every current and future member to use a verified company-domain
email. Public mailbox domains such as Gmail, Outlook, and Live are blocked at
invitation, direct member addition, invitation acceptance, and checkout. If a
personal-workspace owner used a consumer address, they can change and verify the
email on the same Ryu account before checkout; creating a second account is not
the normal upgrade path.

## Who consumes a seat

An active Better Auth organization member consumes one seat. Owners, admins, and
members are all billable members. A pending invitation also reserves one seat
until it is accepted, rejected, cancelled, or expires.

When a provider-native CustomerSeat flow is used, Polar's pending or claimed
seat is the durable capacity claim. Ryu's current Better Auth invitation hooks
keep only a short-lived admission record while the membership write completes;
that record is not a second billed-seat ledger. Failed claims expire
automatically, and a browser count cannot authorize a membership change by
itself.

## Adding and removing members

An owner or admin must allocate enough billed seats before inviting or accepting
more members. Active members and pending invitation reservations both use the
effective capacity. Ryu subtracts any active negotiated allowance first, then
checks the live member and reservation collections; the number shown in the
browser is not authoritative.

Removing a member releases the seat for reassignment. Ryu does not automatically
reduce the Polar quantity when a member leaves: the organization keeps the seat
available until an owner/admin chooses to lower the quantity. This avoids
interrupting a replacement invite and matches the normal seat-billing model.
The reconciled organization billing identity remains attached to the paid
contract if the original billing owner leaves; it does not silently move or
cancel the subscription.

## Upgrades and downgrades

Seat changes go through the Ryu organization billing screen:

- increasing seats is checked against the 50-seat self-serve ceiling and sent to
  Polar with **Prorate and charge now**;
- decreasing seats cannot go below five or the number of active members and
  pending invitation claims not covered by an active negotiated allowance;
- a request above 50 is rejected and routed to Enterprise rather than silently
  clamped;
- a stale browser count cannot authorize a change—the server rechecks members,
  reservations, and the active Teams subscription;
- if Polar cannot be verified, the change fails closed and no membership change
  is authorized from that unknown state.

Polar remains the source of truth for the subscription quantity, seat objects,
and invoice. Ryu's Better Auth organization membership remains the source of
truth for who may use the organization. The two are reconciled at checkout,
seat changes, invitation creation, and invitation acceptance.

With **Prorate and charge now**, Polar settles the unused seat difference in the
same change. After the effective subscription update, Ryu adjusts the current
included credit pool by the remaining-period seat difference and resizes the
included managed server to the new seat band. A pending future update does not
change seats, credits, or server capacity early. Top-up credits are not part of
this adjustment.

## Payment and cancellation

Payment methods and hosted invoice details are handled through the Polar customer
portal after Ryu verifies that provider-side plan and seat editing is disabled.
Plan cancellation and seat changes stay in Ryu so member admission and proration rules are applied
consistently. Included credits remain a single organization pool. Teams grows
when the billed quantity crosses another five-seat bundle; Business grows only
at each completed five-seat bundle. The current-period increase or decrease is
adjusted by the remaining billing-period fraction.
Negotiated capacity does not increase the pool. Purchased top-up credits are
separate and continue to follow the wallet's normal rollover/refund rules.

If the organization needs SSO/SCIM, dedicated or self-hosted deployment, custom
terms, or more than 50 seats, use [Enterprise](/docs/billing#enterprise).
