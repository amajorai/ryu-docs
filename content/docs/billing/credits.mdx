title: "Credits & AI pools"
description: "How Polar-backed prepaid credits, included pools, deposit fees, and organization usage work."
---

Ryu Cloud uses **prepaid USD credits**. Polar keeps the authoritative credit
meter and billing state; Ryu sends server-side usage events, reads the current
customer state, and applies the product access decision. Your balance is spent
down as work runs. When it reaches zero, managed inference pauses or falls back
to a free local model; Ryu does not bill you after the fact.

## Who owns the balance

Polar owns subscriptions, credit grants, top-up products, usage meters, and the
provider-side balance. Ryu keeps a small synchronized projection so the desktop,
web, and gateway can render and enforce the same organization state. A signed
provider event or a fresh provider-state read is authoritative; a stale browser
value is never permission to spend.

Polar does not block an action automatically when a meter reaches zero. Ryu
therefore still checks the balance before managed work and stops or falls back
according to the active product policy. This is enforcement, not a second
financial ledger.

Promotional and referral credits for allocated providers use separate Polar
meters. A grant is recorded on the meter for its own Ryu tier, and only usage
served by that matching provider can spend it. When that allocation is empty,
the request may continue against the shared Polar meter if the organization has
plan or purchased credit. Ryu keeps the grant expiry and routing metadata as a
small local projection so the gateway and the Credits view can enforce and
explain the same provider restriction; the balance itself remains Polar-owned.

## How credits work

1. **Included pool** — Teams and Business receive one shared grant each billing
   period. It is use-it-or-lose-it and does not roll over; Teams grows at each
   additional five billed seats, while Business grants one $100 pool for five
   through nine seats and adds another at each completed five-seat bundle.
2. **Top-up balance** — you can buy additional credits. Top-ups roll over and
   never expire.
3. **Spend order** — matching provider-specific credit is spent first, then the
   expiring included plan pool, then your rollover top-up balance.

The Credits view keeps these sources separate. **Available now** is the total;
it includes the remaining plan credits, on-demand credits, and any
provider-specific allocations. The view also shows each provider-specific
allocation on its own row, including how much is left and any expiry date.

| Balance | What it means | Where it can be used |
|---|---|---|
| **Free-provider credits** | A promotional allocation funded for one free provider | Only the allocated free provider shown on that row; it cannot be moved to another provider |
| **Plan credits** | The remaining included credit from the current plan | Managed providers and eligible first-party metered work; resets at the next billing period and does not roll over |
| **On-demand credits** | Credit purchased by an organization owner or admin | Managed providers and eligible first-party metered work; rolls over between billing periods |

Info icons beside each balance explain the rule in the product. Matching
provider-specific credit is spent first, followed by plan credit and then
on-demand credit. Provider-specific balances are part of the total, not an
extra balance to add on top.

Before payment, the Credits page shows a **Current → After top-up** preview with
your wallet balance and the amount that will be added. Continue to checkout only
after the balance change looks right; the deposit fee is shown as an extra
payment-side charge and does not reduce the face value credited to the wallet.

Every card-funded action requires an enrolled authenticator. Entering the current authenticator-app
code is required in a blocking confirmation dialog before a manual top-up, automatic-recharge
change, plan change, or purchase can be submitted. Email codes are not accepted for billing, and an
account without 2FA must enroll before it can spend or move money.

Checkout APIs also require an `Idempotency-Key` header. Keep the same key when
retrying a request so a timeout cannot create a second checkout; the web and
desktop clients generate this header for you.

After hosted checkout, Ryu returns to a receipt page with a printed payment confirmation. The
signed Polar webhook is the normal fast path. The return page also sends the opaque checkout id to
Ryu, which re-reads the provider checkout and paid order before applying the same idempotent ledger
write; this closes the localhost/sandbox case where Polar cannot deliver a webhook to a development
server. Until the provider order is visible, the receipt says the wallet is syncing instead of
claiming credit that has not been verified. The Credits page refreshes the provider state when the
payment settles.

## Included pool

| Plan | Monthly pool |
|---|---:|
| Free | $0 |
| Pro | **$15/mo for one personal workspace** |
| Max | **$30/mo for one personal workspace** |
| Teams | **$50/mo at five billed seats; +$50 per additional five billed seats, pooled across the organization** |
| Business | **$100/mo for 5–9 billed seats; +$100 at each completed five-seat bundle, pooled across the organization** |
| Enterprise | Contracted |

Pro and Max are individual managed plans with fixed personal pools. Their price
and pool remain attached to the recorded pricing version for existing
customers; new customers can choose them from the Individual pricing shelf.

A Major Pass and the local desktop license do not include managed inference and
cannot open a managed-credit top-up. Top-ups require an active managed plan.

The included pool is attached to the organization's pricing version and shared
across its seats, workflows, and agents. It is calculated from **billed** seats,
not from private negotiated capacity. Teams receives $50 at five seats and $100
at six through ten; Business receives $100 at five through nine and $200 at ten
through fourteen. If Ryu changes the public price and pool in the future, existing
organizations keep the snapshot attached to their agreement.

## What the included pool can pay for

Teams and Business included pools are the organization's unrestricted subscription balance. They
can fund eligible managed provider inference and first-party metered work such
as tool calls, media, and sandbox runtime when those surfaces are enabled for
the deployment. The pool is shared across the organization; it is not locked to
one agent.

## What credits cover

Credits cover managed model inference, agent tool calls, image/video generation,
audio, voice recognition, and sandbox compute. Model usage is metered at cost by
default; a provider-level billing policy can explicitly select raw-cost
pass-through or inherit the Gateway's configured markup. See
[Metering rates](/docs/billing/metering) for the current unit details.

## When credits run out in chat

Chat stops the failed turn instead of leaving it spinning or presenting an
empty assistant reply. The error card stays in conversation history and includes
a **Retry** action for after you restore capacity or change models.

- **Ryu managed OpenRouter** shows **Ryu credits exhausted**. Open **Settings →
  Credits** to top up, or select a BYOK or local model before retrying.
- **OpenRouter BYOK** shows **OpenRouter credits exhausted**. Refill the
  OpenRouter account connected to that API key, or select another provider
  before retrying. BYOK spend remains on the provider account and never consumes
  the organization's Ryu wallet.

Sending a different follow-up also clears the stopped error state, so the
conversation does not remain locked after a provider rejects one turn.

## Usage analytics

In the desktop app, open **Settings → Usage** to see the credit ledger alongside
usage analytics. Choose **You**, **Organization**, or **This server**, then narrow
the view by date range, time resolution, provider, or model.

The dashboard includes activity-over-time, consumption-source, provider, model,
feature, and activity-rhythm charts. **Credit spend** counts only recorded credit
debits. BYOK, local, and self-hosted activity remains visible for comparison but
is shown as **Not billed** when Ryu has no managed-cost record for it.

Organization and server charts use complete, server-owned 15-minute rollups rather
than totals inferred from a capped raw-event page. The desktop combines those
buckets into the selected resolution and compacts very long chart series for
rendering without changing the KPI, source, provider, model, or feature totals.
Organization results include organization-wide servers plus the signed-in member's
team and personal servers; raw server audit and rollup reads remain owner-only.

### Subscription account view

The same **Usage** page also shows a live card for every ChatGPT, Claude, or GitHub
Copilot subscription account connected through Ryu on the selected server. Accounts
stay grouped under their provider, and each row shows the plan, whether it is
selected for you or the shared Gateway, every provider-reported usage window, reset
time, and any provider-reported balance or extra-usage meter.

This is a view of the provider's own limits, not an invoice. API-equivalent token
cost is a comparison metric; direct subscription traffic does not debit Ryu credits,
and Ryu cannot infer the provider's subscription bill from a percentage-used window.
If a provider does not expose a readable account window, the row says whether the
account needs reconnection, has no metered plan, is rate-limited, or is unavailable.
Usage is read per saved account without switching the account used for the next chat
turn.

## Managed server billing and sidebar warnings

Managed-server inference uses the **active organization's wallet**. The server is the
execution path, not a separate billing account. When a managed server connects, its
organization binding tells the gateway which organization balance to check and
debit. All managed servers in that organization therefore draw from the same
wallet. BYOK provider balances remain with the provider credentials configured on
the server and are not Ryu credit balances.

The desktop shows the organization wallet below the active managed server in the
sidebar. A subscription with an included pool shows a low-credit warning when
20% or less of that pool remains, and the warning includes the next subscription
reset when the server provides one. Select **Add credits** to open the Credits
settings. Switching to a local or BYOK server hides the managed-wallet warning.

## Deposit fees

The one-time deposit fee covers upstream credit purchase and payment handling.
The fee is charged **on top of** the amount deposited, and the full face value
is credited to your wallet.

| Plan | Fee rate | Minimum |
|---|---:|---:|
| Pro | **16.5%** | **$2.75** |
| Max | **16%** | **$2.75** |
| Teams | **16%** | **$2.75** |
| Business | **16%** | **$2.75** |
| Enterprise | Contracted | Contracted |

The fee is the greater of the percentage and the minimum. A $100 Teams or Business top-up
charges $116 and credits the wallet the full $100. A $5 face-value top-up charges
$7.75 at the base rate. A negotiated seat allowance
does not change the deposit-fee tier.

## Auto-recharge and spend controls

Teams, Business, and Enterprise organizations can enable automatic top-up, set a monthly
cap, and configure low-balance alerts from **Organization settings → Billing → Spend
controls**. The tab shows the amount charged this month and keeps the refill bounded by the
monthly cap. Each agent run may also have a per-run dollar budget cap.

Automatic top-up uses a fixed, verified one-time payment product. A missing or zero monthly cap
uses a bounded default of ten refill amounts; a configured cap cannot exceed one hundred refill
amounts per calendar month. If the payment provider, product price, or accounting response cannot
be verified, Ryu does not issue the credit and managed provider calls pause until the state is
reconciled.

Organization owners and admins manage top-ups and auto-recharge. Polar records
the credit movement; Ryu stores only the organization policy that decides when
an automatic refill may be requested. The balance is shared by the organization,
not split into individual seat wallets.

## Changing or cancelling

Changing or cancelling a plan never claws back top-up credits. Included credits
follow the effective plan and seat change for the remainder of the current
billing period; credits already spent are never returned. Cancellation stops
future included grants and leaves top-up balance available. See [Changing or
cancelling your plan](/docs/billing/plan-changes).
