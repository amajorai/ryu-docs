Ryu Cloud runs on **prepaid credits** (USD). You spend your balance down as you go. When it runs out, Ryu pauses managed inference (or falls back to a free local model). You are **never** billed after the fact.

<Callout type="info">
</Callout>

## How credits work

1. **Monthly included pool** — Pro, Max, and Teams include a credit grant each billing period. It's use-it-or-lose-it: unused pool credits do **not** roll over.
2. **Top-up credits** — you buy additional credits any time. These **do** roll over and never expire.
3. **Spend order** — monthly pool credits are spent first (since they expire), then your topped-up balance.

## Monthly included pools

| Plan | Monthly pool |
|---|---|
| Free | $0 |
| Lifetime | $0 |
| Pro | **$15/mo** |
| Max | **$30/mo** |
| Teams | **$15/seat/mo** |

- Pools are **use-it-or-lose-it** — they reset each billing period and do not roll over.
- Top-ups DO roll over, and are spent only once the included pool is exhausted.
- **Teams** pool scales per seat: $15/seat × seat count = the org-wide pool.

## What credits cover

Credits are spent on:

- **Model inference** — metered **at cost**, no per-token markup
- **Agent tool calls** — **$0.299 per 1,000 executions**, at cost
- **Image and video generation** — metered on the compute time the provider reports
- **Speech** (text-to-speech and transcription) — per call
- **Sandbox compute** — per second, only while a sandbox is running

See [Metering rates](/docs/billing/metering) for the exact figure on each.

## Named credits

Credit you **buy** is ordinary balance. It spends on anything.

Credit you are **granted** (a referral reward, a campaign, a free-tier allowance)
arrives with a **name**, and that name decides what it can be spent on. A named
credit is not convertible: Ryu Frontier credit cannot be spent on fast models and
Ryu Fast credit cannot be spent on frontier ones. This is what lets us hand out
generous amounts of the cheap kind without it becoming a claim on the expensive
kind.

| Credit | What it buys |
|---|---|
| **Ryu Fast** | Fast, everyday models — the default for most work, and where free-tier and referral grants land |
| **Ryu Frontier** | The most capable models, for hard reasoning and long context |
| **Ryu Vision** | Frontier models that can also read images and video |
| **Ryu Reasoning** | Frontier models tuned for step-by-step problem solving |

Pick the matching entry in the model picker to spend a named credit. Selecting
**Ryu Fast** there is what draws down your Ryu Fast balance. Your Credits page
lists each named balance separately, so you can see what you actually hold.

<Callout type="info">
If you hold only named credit and pick a model outside that name, the request is
declined rather than billed to the wrong balance, even though your total looks
positive. Switch to the matching entry in the picker.
</Callout>

## Deposit fees

When you top up, a one-time deposit fee applies. It covers what our upstream provider charges to buy credits, plus card processing, so model tokens themselves stay at cost, with no per-token markup.

| Plan | Fee rate | Minimum |
|---|---|---|
| Lifetime | **Cannot top up** | — |
| Pro | **13%** | $2.40 |
| Max / Teams | **12%** | $2.40 |

The fee is the **greater of** the percentage and the $2.40 floor:

| Top-up amount | Pro fee | Max/Teams fee | Effective rate (Pro) |
|---|---|---|---|
| $5 | $2.40 | $2.40 | 48% |
| $10 | $2.40 | $2.40 | 24% |
| $20 | $2.60 | $2.40 | 13% |
| $50 | $6.50 | $6.00 | 13% |
| $100 | $13.00 | $12.00 | 13% |

The floor stops mattering once the percentage overtakes it, at **$18.46** on Pro and **$20** on
Max and Teams. Below that the $2.40 minimum dominates, so small top-ups are poor value: deposit
more at once for a better effective rate.

<Callout type="info">
The fee is charged **on top of** the amount you deposit, not taken out of it. A $100 top-up on Pro
charges $113 and credits your wallet the full $100.
</Callout>

## Auto-recharge

Pro, Max, and Teams users can enable **automatic top-up**: when the wallet balance drops below a configurable threshold, Ryu charges a configurable amount to your saved payment method (off-session via Polar). You can set a monthly cap to prevent runaway charges.

Organization owners and admins can also enable a **low-balance email alert** from the organization billing page. Choose whether the email goes to owners or to owners and admins, then set the dollar threshold. This alert is independent of automatic top-up and does not require a saved payment method; it is sent once per low-balance episode and re-arms after the wallet recovers.

Configured from **Settings → Billing → Spend controls** in the desktop app.

## Sandbox budget

Each agent run can have a **per-run budget cap** (in dollars). When the run's cumulative spend hits the cap, the agent stops making inference calls. This prevents a looping agent from burning your entire wallet.

Set from **Settings → Billing → Spend controls**. Default is $0 (no cap).

## Wallet model

- Credits live in an **org-scoped wallet** (one wallet per organization)
- Team wallets are **pooled** across all seats, so every member draws from the same balance
- The wallet balance is visible to all org members; only owner/admin can top up or change auto-recharge

## Changing your plan

Upgrading, downgrading, changing seats, or cancelling never prorates or claws back credits. See [Changing or cancelling your plan](/docs/billing/plan-changes) for exactly what happens to your pool and top-ups at each step.
