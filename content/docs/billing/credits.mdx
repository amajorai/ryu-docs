---
title: "Credits & AI pools"
description: "How prepaid credits, included pools, deposit fees, and the organization wallet work."
---

Ryu Cloud uses **prepaid USD credits**. Your balance is spent down as work
runs. When it reaches zero, managed inference pauses or falls back to a free
local model; Ryu does not bill you after the fact.

## How credits work

1. **Included pool** — Teams and Business receive one shared grant each billing
   period. It is use-it-or-lose-it and does not roll over; Teams starts at $50 at
   five billed seats and Business starts at $100, with each growing by its
   documented five-seat bundle amount.
2. **Top-up balance** — you can buy additional credits. Top-ups roll over and
   never expire.
3. **Spend order** — the expiring included pool is spent first, then your
   rollover top-up balance.

Before payment, the Credits page shows a **Current → After top-up** preview with
your wallet balance and the amount that will be added. Continue to checkout only
after the balance change looks right; the deposit fee is shown as an extra
payment-side charge and does not reduce the face value credited to the wallet.

If 2FA is enabled on your account, entering the current authenticator-app code is required in a
blocking confirmation dialog before a manual top-up or automatic-recharge change can be submitted.
The dialog cannot be closed or bypassed from another client. Accounts without 2FA keep the normal
top-up flow.

After hosted checkout, Ryu returns to a receipt page with a printed payment confirmation. The
wallet update is webhook-driven, so the receipt can appear before the new balance is visible; the
Credits page refreshes the wallet when the payment settles.

## Included pool

| Plan | Monthly pool |
|---|---:|
| Free | $0 |
| Teams | **$50/mo at five billed seats; +$50 per additional five billed seats, pooled across the organization** |
| Business | **$100/mo at five billed seats; +$100 per additional five billed seats, pooled across the organization** |
| Enterprise | Contracted |

Personal managed subscriptions remain attached to their recorded pricing
versions for existing customers, but are not currently public checkout options.

The included pool is attached to the organization's pricing version and shared
across its seats, workflows, and agents. It is calculated from **billed** seats,
not from private negotiated capacity: five billed seats receive $50, six to ten
receive $100, and so on. If Ryu changes the public price and pool in the future,
existing organizations keep the snapshot attached to their agreement.

## What the included pool can pay for

Teams and Business included pools are the organization's unrestricted subscription balance. They
can fund eligible managed provider inference and first-party metered work such
as tool calls, media, and sandbox runtime when those surfaces are enabled for
the deployment. The pool is shared across the organization; it is not locked to
one agent.

Named promotional credits are different. **Ryu Fast** and other named grants
are restricted to their capability pool, so they cannot be used to pay for
unrelated runtime or integration costs. The billing screen shows the balance
and any pool restrictions separately.

## What credits cover

Credits cover managed model inference, agent tool calls, image/video generation,
audio, voice recognition, and sandbox compute. Model usage is metered at cost by
default; a provider-level billing policy can explicitly select raw-cost
pass-through or inherit the Gateway's configured markup. See
[Metering rates](/docs/billing/metering) for the current unit details.

Outpost Treg calls use Ryu's managed provider connection. Each debit uses the active organization's
shared wallet and appears in the usage statement with provider `treg` and the Treg
endpoint/transaction reference.

## When credits run out in chat

Chat stops the failed turn instead of leaving it spinning or presenting an
empty assistant reply. The error card stays in conversation history and includes
a **Retry** action for after you restore capacity or change models.

- **Ryu managed OpenRouter** shows **Ryu credits exhausted**. Open **Settings →
  Credits** to top up, or select a BYOK or local model before retrying.
- **OpenRouter BYOK** shows **OpenRouter credits exhausted**. Refill the
  OpenRouter account connected to that API key, or select another provider
  before retrying. BYOK spend remains on the provider account and never consumes
  the organization's Ryu wallet.

Sending a different follow-up also clears the stopped error state, so the
conversation does not remain locked after a provider rejects one turn.

## Usage analytics

In the desktop app, open **Settings → Usage** to see the credit ledger alongside
usage analytics. Choose **You**, **Organization**, or **This node**, then narrow
the view by date range, time resolution, provider, or model.

The dashboard includes activity-over-time, consumption-source, provider, model,
feature, and activity-rhythm charts. **Credit spend** counts only recorded credit
debits. BYOK, local, and self-hosted activity remains visible for comparison but
is shown as **Not billed** when Ryu has no managed-cost record for it.

Organization and node charts use complete, server-owned 15-minute rollups rather
than totals inferred from a capped raw-event page. The desktop combines those
buckets into the selected resolution and compacts very long chart series for
rendering without changing the KPI, source, provider, model, or feature totals.
Organization results include organization-wide nodes plus the signed-in member's
team and personal nodes; raw node audit and rollup reads remain owner-only.

## Managed node billing and sidebar warnings

Managed-node inference uses the **active organization's wallet**. The node is the
execution path, not a separate billing account. When a managed node connects, its
organization binding tells the gateway which organization balance to check and
debit. All managed nodes in that organization therefore draw from the same
wallet. BYOK provider balances remain with the provider credentials configured on
the node and are not Ryu credit balances.

The desktop shows the organization wallet below the active managed node in the
sidebar. A subscription with an included pool shows a low-credit warning when
20% or less of that pool remains, and the warning includes the next subscription
reset when the server provides one. Select **Add credits** to open the Credits
settings. Switching to a local or BYOK node hides the managed-wallet warning.

## Named credits

Credit you buy is ordinary balance. Campaign and referral grants may carry a
name that limits which model class they can fund. The Credits page shows each
named balance separately, so a positive total cannot hide an incompatible pool.

Desktop onboarding also offers a connection quest: each newly active eligible
app connection adds **$0.50** of Ryu Fast bonus credit, up to **$10** total (20
connections). The reward is recorded once per app, after the connection is
ready; retrying a connection cannot pay it twice. Ryu chooses the pool and
amount, and the connection bonus is not a charge or a subscription discount.

## Deposit fees

The one-time deposit fee covers upstream credit purchase and payment handling.
The fee is charged **on top of** the amount deposited, and the full face value
is credited to your wallet.

| Plan | Fee rate | Minimum |
|---|---:|---:|
| Teams | **12%** | **$2.40** |
| Business | **12%** | **$2.40** |
| Enterprise | Contracted | Contracted |

The fee is the greater of the percentage and the minimum. A $100 Teams or Business top-up
charges $113 and credits the wallet the full $100. A negotiated seat allowance
does not change the deposit-fee tier.

## Auto-recharge and spend controls

Teams, Business, and Enterprise organizations can enable automatic top-up, set a monthly
cap, and configure low-balance alerts from **Organization settings → Billing → Spend
controls**. The tab shows the amount charged this month and keeps the refill bounded by the
monthly cap. Each agent run may also have a per-run dollar budget cap.

Organization owners and admins manage top-ups and auto-recharge. The wallet is
shared by the organization, not split into individual seat wallets.

## Changing or cancelling

Changing or cancelling a plan never claws back top-up credits. Included credits
already granted remain spendable; cancellation only stops future included
grants. See [Changing or cancelling your plan](/docs/billing/plan-changes).
