title: "Changing or Cancelling Your Plan"
description: "What happens to included credits, top-ups, agent capacity, and cloud computers when billing changes."
---

Owners and billing managers (the Billing role) change a recurring plan from **Organization →
Billing → Change plan**. Ryu verifies the active subscription, keeps the change within the same
audience and billing interval, checks organization members and pending seat
claims, and asks Polar to apply the catalog product with **Prorate and charge
now**. Pro and Max are individual-plan changes; Teams and Business are
organization-plan changes. A private plan offer, when issued, keeps its own
server-validated price and included-credit snapshot without changing the public
contract for its parent plan. Enterprise changes happen through a conversation.

On the web, **Change plan** and **Cancel plan** open in a window over the organization
billing page or the dashboard;
close it, or press Escape or Back, to return. Opening the link directly shows the same
step as a full page. Each step says whose plan it is, either your personal plan or the
organization it belongs to. Billing changes act on your active organization, so if the
window was opened for one organization and another is now active (for example after
switching in another tab), Ryu refuses the change, leaves both plans as they were, and
links to the right organization's billing page.

Payment methods and invoices are
[sections of the billing page](/docs/billing/checkout#payment-methods-and-invoices). Checkout
and credit top-ups finish on [Ryu's checkout page](/docs/billing/checkout), which
only renders checkouts Ryu created.

**Cancel plan** schedules cancellation at the end of the current billing period. You keep access
until then, and no refund is issued. To confirm, pull the card's ticket stub down along the
perforation, tap the stub five times, or focus it and press the Down Arrow key five times. A
partial pull springs back when released and leaves the subscription unchanged; a complete tear
submits the cancellation. If the plan card is loading, use **Schedule cancellation** instead.
**Keep my plan** closes the confirmation without changing the subscription. A failed request keeps
the plan active and restores the confirmation for retry. After success, **Resume plan** becomes
available.

Ryu verifies that provider-side plan and seat editing is disabled before issuing a portal session.
Polar owns subscription and credit-meter state. Ryu projects signed provider events into effective
entitlements and keeps runtime access and included cloud-computer capacity aligned with them.

When you start a new organization plan from Ryu, the review step shows the
**Current → New** plan, the recurring amount, and the organization-level seat
quantity before opening checkout. Polar remains the source of truth for the
final total and proration. After checkout, Ryu returns to a receipt page that
confirms the payment and the plan's next state.

The public pricing page uses one seat selector for Teams and Business. It
starts at five seats, carries a selected quantity through sign-in and
organization selection, and routes 51 or more seats to Enterprise. Checkout
still validates the live organization member and pending-invitation counts on
the server.

For an effective plan or seat change, Ryu asks Polar to use **Prorate and charge now**:
the unused difference is charged for an upgrade or credited for a downgrade in
the same change. Ryu applies the corresponding current-period entitlement
change after the active subscription update is confirmed.

## Reviewing an organization plan change

An organization owner or a member with billing management permission can open
**Change plan** from the organization billing page. Review the current and new
plans and the proration summary before continuing. **Cancel** leaves the current
plan in place.

While confirmation is pending, the dialog cannot be dismissed or submitted
again. A failed request keeps the review and its error visible for another
attempt. If the provider's renewal price changed before you confirmed, Ryu prices the
change again; a different amount needs a fresh agreement. After a successful change,
the window closes and the billing page shows the updated plan.

## Renewal agreement

Recurring plan cards show the renewal amount and the monthly or yearly interval beside the
purchase button, with cancellation instructions and a link to the subscription terms. Select the
automatic-renewal agreement before you continue. A change to the price, seat count, or interval
needs a fresh agreement. One-time desktop licenses do not renew automatically. The payment
provider's checkout confirms the final total, taxes, and subscription details before it charges you.

Paid cloud-server purchases and compute add-ons show their own monthly renewal terms and
agreement. A change of cloud region needs a fresh agreement, even when the price stays the same.
A switch back to an earlier offer also needs a fresh agreement. Organization checkout review needs
an agreement before you continue with the displayed recurring quote. Existing-plan changes fetch a
renewal quote from the billing provider before you can agree and confirm. Ryu checks that the
quote still matches before it applies the change. If a price cannot be confirmed, the change stays
blocked.

## 2FA confirmation

Ryu requires enrolled 2FA and the current authenticator-app code before opening checkout, changing
billed seats, or opening the billing portal. The same confirmation protects cloud-computer
purchases and resizes. It is a blocking dialog with no close button, Escape action, or outside-click
dismissal, and the server enforces it for every client. Email-only confirmation is not accepted for
card-funded billing actions.

## What changes with your plan

- **Included credits follow the effective change.** Polar records the meter
  credit grant and its proration state. Ryu never creates a second grant for a
  webhook retry, and a downgrade cannot return credits that were already spent.
  If a reduction exceeds the remaining included balance, the consumed difference
  offsets later included credit increases and renewals, so cycling between seat
  counts cannot refill spent credit.
- **Free credit stays separate.** A plan change only changes the shared plan
  meter; it does not merge or move free credit. Free credit keeps its own
  expiry and is still spent first on models marked Free until it runs out.
- **Top-up credits are never touched.** They roll over and survive plan changes
  and cancellation.
- **Upgrades never resize your included server.** Renewals, billed-seat changes,
  and moves to a higher plan keep the existing server as it is. A move to a
  cheaper plan may schedule a resize in the server's quiet hours after Polar
  confirms the new subscription state; see
  [Your Ryu server when the plan changes](#your-ryu-server-when-the-plan-changes).
  A future pending update does not change the computer early.
- **Cloud computer purchases and upgrades are plan-gated.** See
  [Who can buy or upgrade](/docs/billing/cloud-computers#who-can-buy-or-upgrade).
- **Existing contracted pricing is preserved.** A later public price increase
  does not move an organization already on a recorded pricing version.
- **Cancelling stops future grants.** Credits already granted remain available
  until spent or expired under their normal rules.
- **Member seats are organization-level.** They are separate from cloud-computer size and
  from the number of workflows or agents the organization creates.

## Starting Teams or Business

Prices, seat minimums, and the pooled AI grant for each plan are on
[Plans and Pricing](/docs/billing/plans#teams).
Both plans include an always-on Ryu server, and both
plans attach their included cloud computer and pooled wallet to the organization. If the
first process needs more configuration, Ryu may scope a private founding proof
arrangement in conversation; any price, duration, and workflow scope are
written into the agreement.

The automatically created personal workspace can be upgraded in place. A
consumer-mailbox owner must change and verify the address on the same Ryu account
first; the completed change moves the workspace into the Teams boundary. Ryu
requires every current member to have a verified business-domain email before
checkout, and the paid conversion becomes active only after Polar confirms the
subscription. The conversion keeps the existing organization id, data, wallet,
agents, workflows, and audit history.

## Your Ryu server when the plan changes

Every plan that includes a cloud computer includes an always-on Ryu server, and
the price is the same whatever server stock looks like. A server you already
have keeps its size when you renew, change seats, or move to a higher plan.

When you move to a cheaper plan, Ryu may resize your server to the one the new
plan includes. That happens during the server's quiet hours (3:00 in the
server's own time zone) with a brief restart, and the organization gets an email first. Your data
and disk are kept. A server that can't be resized that way keeps its size. If
you change plans again before the quiet hours, Ryu checks again and cancels the
resize when the new plan no longer needs it.

## Sold-out plans

A plan is shown as **Sold out** only when no server it can use is available. New
checkouts and upgrades to it are then refused until stock returns, while plans
whose servers you already have keep working. See
[When a server is out of stock](/docs/billing/cloud-computers#when-a-server-is-out-of-stock).

## Expanding capacity

When another person needs access, an owner or billing manager adds a seat in the
organization billing screen. The organization keeps its existing data, wallet,
audit history, and pricing version. A negotiated allowance may cover additional
members for a documented period, but it does not increase the included credit
pool or cloud-computer band. A pending invitation reserves one of the available seats;
cancel it if the invitation should no longer hold capacity. Removing or leaving
releases a member seat without lowering the billed quantity. Workflow capacity
and cloud-computer size are managed separately from the seat meter. An organization may
choose Business when the larger pooled grant is worth the premium.

## Upgrading to Enterprise

Enterprise is the path for organizations with more than 50 active members or
with requirements such as dedicated capacity, custom deployment, SSO/SCIM,
procurement terms, data-region controls, or named onboarding. The Enterprise
agreement states the seat allowance, workflow capacity, credit pool, support
level, and cloud-computer arrangement. When the agreement includes a cloud computer, an
owner or billing manager can upgrade it through the same checkout flow used for Business
and Teams.

## Cancelling

When you cancel:

- future included monthly grants stop;
- included credits already granted remain spendable until their normal expiry;
- top-up balance remains available and does not expire;
- an included cloud computer runs until the paid period ends, then is switched off and kept as
  a disk snapshot for 3 days, with email reminders before it is deleted; resubscribing within
  that window restores it with its data;
- the cloud computer's automated daily backups are kept until the paid period ends and are then
  permanently deleted; only the snapshot above remains through the 3-day window.

Cancellation never claws back credit you already hold.

## What is prorated

Polar handles the money-side proration and credit-meter bookkeeping shown on your
invoices. Ryu reads the effective provider state and applies its runtime policy.
At the next billing period, Polar starts the new plan's credit grant; Ryu does not
keep a second balance ledger.
