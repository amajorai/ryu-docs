---
title: "Changing or cancelling your plan"
description: "What happens to your credits and cloud node when you upgrade, downgrade, change seats, or cancel."
---

Plan changes happen in the Polar billing portal (**Settings → Billing → Manage plan**). Polar handles the money side, charging or refunding your payment method the prorated difference. This page covers what happens to your **credits** and **cloud node** on our side.

## The short version

- **Credits are never prorated.** A new plan's monthly pool is granted at its full amount, whatever day of the month you change plans.
- **One pool grant per organization, per calendar month.** Upgrading partway through a month does not add a second, bigger grant that month. The larger pool starts at the next monthly renewal.
- **Downgrades reduce your remaining pool**, but only the unspent part, and never below what you've already used.
- **Top-up credits are never touched.** They always roll over and survive upgrades, downgrades, seat changes, and cancellation.
- **Cancelling stops future grants but never takes back what you already hold.**

## Upgrading

Upgrading (Pro → Max, or adding Teams seats) changes your features and limits the moment it lands: bigger cloud node, more storage, lower deposit fee, and so on.

What it does **not** do is top up your wallet on the spot. Your included pool is granted once per month; if you've already received this month's pool, the upgrade applies to the **next** monthly renewal. Nothing is lost; the difference simply isn't granted mid-month.

## Downgrading

Downgrading (Max → Pro, or removing Teams seats) reduces your remaining included pool, but only by the part you haven't spent:

- Credits you've already spent stay spent. They are never clawed back.
- The remaining pool is reduced to the smaller plan's amount.

## Changing seats (Teams)

Seat changes follow the same rules as plan changes:

- **Adding seats** mid-month: the larger pool (more seats) applies at the next monthly renewal.
- **Removing seats** mid-month: the remaining pool is reduced to the new seat count's amount, again only the unspent part.

## Cancelling

When you cancel:

- Your included monthly pool **stops being topped up** each month. Included credits you already hold remain spendable until used.
- Your **top-up balance is untouched**; it rolls over and never expires.
- Your included cloud node is scheduled for removal after a grace period. See [Plans & Pricing](/docs/billing) for the node lifecycle.

Cancellation never claws back credit you already hold.

## What is prorated

The only proration that happens is **on the money side, in Polar**. If you change plans partway through a billing period, your payment method is charged or credited the difference. Your credit pool is not divided up by days remaining: it is always a full-month grant, or none.
