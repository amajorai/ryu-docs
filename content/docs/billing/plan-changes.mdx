---
title: "Changing or cancelling your plan"
description: "What happens to included credits, top-ups, agent capacity, and servers when billing changes."
---

Owners and admins change a recurring plan from **Organization → Billing → Change
plan**. Ryu verifies the active subscription, keeps the change within the same
audience and billing interval, checks organization members and pending seat
claims, and asks Polar to apply the catalog product with **Prorate and charge
now**. Pro and Max are individual-plan changes; Teams and Business are
organization-plan changes. Enterprise changes happen through a conversation.

Payment methods, cancellation, and hosted invoice details remain in the Polar
billing portal. Ryu verifies that provider-side plan and seat editing is disabled
before issuing that portal session, so the portal cannot bypass Ryu's access and
seat rules. Polar owns the subscription and credit-meter state. Ryu listens for
the signed provider events, projects the effective entitlement, and keeps runtime
access and included server capacity aligned with it. This page covers that
Ryu-side projection and enforcement behavior.

When you start a new organization plan from Ryu, the review step shows the
**Current → New** plan, the recurring amount, and the organization-level seat
quantity before opening checkout. Polar remains the source of truth for the
final total and proration. After checkout, Ryu returns to a receipt page that
confirms the payment and the plan's next state.

For an effective plan or seat change, Ryu asks Polar to use **Prorate and charge now**:
the unused difference is charged for an upgrade or credited for a downgrade in
the same change. Ryu applies the corresponding current-period entitlement
change after the active subscription update is confirmed.

## 2FA confirmation

Ryu requires enrolled 2FA and the current authenticator-app code before opening checkout, changing
billed seats, or opening the billing portal. The same confirmation protects managed cloud-server
purchases and resizes. It is a blocking dialog with no close button, Escape action, or outside-click
dismissal, and the server enforces it for every client. Email-only confirmation is not accepted for
card-funded billing actions.

## The short version

- **Included credits follow the effective change.** Polar records the meter
  credit grant and its proration state. Ryu never creates a second grant for a
  webhook retry, and a downgrade cannot return credits that were already spent.
- **Provider-specific promotional credit stays separate.** A plan change only
  changes the shared plan meter; it does not merge or re-route a free-provider
  allocation. Each restricted allocation continues to expire and spend only on
  its assigned provider until its Polar meter reaches zero.
- **Top-up credits are never touched.** They roll over and survive plan changes
  and cancellation.
- **Included server capacity follows the effective change.** A plan or billed-seat
  upgrade/downgrade resizes or reconciles the included managed server after Polar
  confirms the new subscription state. A future pending update does not change
  the server early.
- **Existing contracted pricing is preserved.** A later public price increase
  does not move an organization already on a recorded pricing version.
- **Cancelling stops future grants.** Credits already granted remain available
  until spent or expired under their normal rules.
- **Member seats are organization-level.** They are separate from server size and
  from the number of workflows or agents the organization creates.

## Starting Teams or Business

Teams starts with five billed member seats at a $250/month floor. Each
additional billed seat is $50/month through the 50-seat self-serve ceiling. The
shared AI pool is $50 at five billed seats and adds $50 whenever the quantity
crosses another five-seat bundle. Business starts at $300/month for five seats,
keeps the $50 marginal seat price, and grants $100 at five through nine billed
seats plus $100 at each completed five-seat bundle. Business also includes a
performance managed server.
Teams attaches its CX43 capacity and Business attaches its CX53 capacity; both
plans attach their included server and pooled wallet to the organization. If the
first process needs more configuration, Ryu may scope a private founding proof
arrangement in conversation; any price, duration, and workflow scope are
written into the agreement.

The automatically created personal workspace can be upgraded in place. A
consumer-mailbox owner must change and verify the address on the same Ryu account
first; the completed change moves the workspace into the Teams boundary. Ryu
requires every current member to have a verified business-domain email before
checkout, and the paid conversion becomes active only after Polar confirms the
subscription. The conversion keeps the existing organization id, data, wallet,
agents, workflows, and audit history.

## Expanding capacity

When another person needs access, an owner or admin adds a seat in the
organization billing screen. The organization keeps its existing data, wallet,
audit history, and pricing version. A negotiated allowance may cover additional
members for a documented period, but it does not increase the included credit
pool or server band. A pending invitation reserves one of the available seats;
cancel it if the invitation should no longer hold capacity. Removing or leaving
releases a member seat without lowering the billed quantity. Workflow capacity
and server size are managed separately from the seat meter. An organization may
choose Business below 25 members when the larger pooled grant and performance
server are worth the premium.

## Upgrading to Enterprise

Enterprise is the path for organizations with more than 50 active members or
with requirements such as dedicated capacity, custom deployment, SSO/SCIM,
procurement terms, data-region controls, or named onboarding. The Enterprise
agreement states the seat allowance, workflow capacity, credit pool, support
level, and server arrangement.

## Cancelling

When you cancel:

- future included monthly grants stop;
- included credits already granted remain spendable until their normal expiry;
- top-up balance remains available and does not expire;
- an included managed server is scheduled for removal after its grace period.

Cancellation never claws back credit you already hold.

## What is prorated

Polar handles the money-side proration and credit-meter bookkeeping shown in the
billing portal. Ryu consumes the effective provider state and applies only its
runtime policy. At the next billing period, Polar starts the new plan's credit
grant through the configured meter grant path; Ryu does not maintain a second
balance ledger.
