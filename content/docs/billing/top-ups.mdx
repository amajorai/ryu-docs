Prepaid credits are bought as top-ups, and each top-up carries a one-time deposit fee. This page
is the canonical reference for the fee schedule and for top-up, recharge, and credit-transfer
behavior. How the balance is spent is on [Credits and AI Pools](/docs/billing/credits).

## Deposit fees

The one-time deposit fee covers upstream credit purchase and payment handling.
The fee is charged **on top of** the amount deposited, and the full face value
is credited to your wallet.

From **September 18, 2026**, the economics reserve 9% Singapore GST on Ryu's
OpenRouter credit-purchase subtotal when that upstream tax is not recoverable.
This provider cost is included in the deposit-fee guard; any tax that applies to
your own checkout is itemized separately by the payment provider.

| Plan | Fee rate | Minimum |
|---|---:|---:|
| No managed plan | **27%** | **$5** |
| Pro | **26.5%** | **$5** |
| Max | **26%** | **$5** |
| Teams | **26%** | **$5** |
| Business | **26%** | **$5** |
| Enterprise | Contracted | Contracted |

Separately issued private offers use the rate of their public counterpart: a
private individual offer pays the Pro rate and a private organization offer pays
the Teams rate.

The fee is the greater of the percentage and the minimum. Manual top-ups offer
$100, $250, and $1,000 USD presets plus a custom face value from $5 to $1,000.
Presets are convenience amounts, not discounted credit bundles: the full face
value goes to the wallet and the plan-based fee is added. At the no-plan rate, those presets
charge $127, $317.50, and $1,241.12 respectively (the $1,000 preset hits the $10 margin cap below), before any checkout tax Polar
calculates. A $100 Teams or Business top-up charges $126 and credits the wallet
the full $100. A $5 face-value top-up charges $10 at the base rate. A negotiated
seat allowance does not change the deposit-fee tier.

### Where the deposit fee goes

The deposit fee looks high next to a typical card fee because it is the
**only** place Ryu charges for managed inference. Model usage is debited at the
provider-reported cost with no per-token markup, so the costs of buying the
upstream credits and taking your payment have to be covered once, at deposit.
Three external costs sit under every top-up:

- **OpenRouter funding fee:** 5.5% of the credits Ryu buys, with a $0.80
  minimum. Managed models are served through OpenRouter credits that Ryu buys on
  your behalf.
- **Singapore GST:** 9% on that OpenRouter purchase (the credits plus
  OpenRouter's fee), from September 18, 2026. The schedule assumes this tax is
  not recoverable.
- **Polar payment processing:** up to 6.5% + $0.50 of the total you are
  charged. This is the highest listed card path, including the
  international-card surcharge; domestic cards cost less.

**What Ryu keeps.** Ryu takes **no margin on usage**: every model request is
debited at the provider-reported cost. Ryu's only margin is whatever is left of
the deposit fee after the three costs above. On a typical top-up that is about
**2 to 3.5% of the credits you buy**, and it is **capped at $10 per top-up**: once
the plan percentage would leave Ryu more than $10 (from about $280 of credits at
27%, or $373 at 26%), the fee stops growing and covers only the provider costs
plus $10. Above that point every plan pays the same fee. Only very small top-ups are different: on a $5
top-up, $2.53 of the $5 minimum fee is left over, because the minimum is sized
to cover fixed charges, not the amount you deposit.

Worked examples under the conservative assumptions above, rounded to the cent:

| Top-up | You pay | You get (wallet credit) | Paid to OpenRouter (fee + GST) | Paid to Polar | Ryu keeps | Ryu keeps, % of credits |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| $5 (minimum fee) | $10.00 | $5.00 | $1.32 | $1.15 | $2.53 | 50.6% |
| $100, 27% (no plan) | $127.00 | $100.00 | $15.00 | $8.75 | $3.25 | 3.3% |
| $100, 26% (Max, organizations) | $126.00 | $100.00 | $15.00 | $8.69 | $2.31 | 2.3% |
| $250, 27% (no plan) | $317.50 | $250.00 | $37.49 | $21.14 | $8.87 | 3.5% |
| $500, any plan (capped) | $626.17 | $500.00 | $74.98 | $41.20 | $9.99 | 2.0% |
| $1,000, any plan (capped) | $1,241.12 | $1,000.00 | $149.95 | $81.17 | $10.00 | 1.0% |

Read a row left to right: of the $127 you pay for $100 of credits, $100 goes into
your wallet and is spent at cost, $15.00 buys those credits from OpenRouter
(its $5.50 fee plus $9.50 GST reserve), $8.75 covers Polar's card processing, and
$3.25 is Ryu's margin. These are worst-case costs, and the $10 cap is measured
against them. With a domestic card, or if the upstream GST becomes recoverable,
Ryu's real costs are lower, so Ryu can keep somewhat more than the table shows.
The fee you are quoted does not change.

The rates are the smallest rounded schedule that does not lose money on any
whole-cent top-up under these assumptions. The $5 minimum exists because
OpenRouter's $0.80 minimum and Polar's $0.50 fixed charge stay the same no
matter how small the top-up is.

Promotional and provider-specific credits that Ryu grants (for example, credits
on a campaign or a challenge prize) carry no deposit fee: they are funded from
Ryu's own provider allocations, not from a card payment.

Any sales tax or VAT on your own checkout is separate: Polar calculates and
itemizes it at checkout. To avoid the deposit fee entirely, use a local model or
connect your own provider key. BYOK usage is billed by that provider and never
touches the Ryu wallet.

## Auto-recharge and spend controls

Teams, Business, and Enterprise organizations can enable automatic top-up, set a monthly
cap, and configure low-balance alerts from **Organization settings → Billing**. The page
shows the amount charged this month and keeps the refill bounded by the monthly cap. Each
agent run may also have a per-run dollar budget cap.

Low-balance email alerts are separate from automatic top-up. Choose **No one (off)**,
**Owners only**, or **Owners and admins**, then set the balance threshold in dollars. A
recipient change saves immediately; a threshold change saves when you leave the field.

Changes to automatic top-up and low-balance alerts are recorded in the organization activity
log with the member who made them and the previous and new settings.

Automatic top-up uses a fixed, verified one-time payment product. A missing or zero monthly cap
uses a bounded default of ten refill amounts; a configured cap cannot exceed one hundred refill
amounts per calendar month. If the payment provider, product price, or accounting response cannot
be verified, Ryu does not issue the credit and managed provider calls pause until the state is
reconciled.

Automatic top-up charges only the payment customer bound to the organization. Ryu verifies that
binding again before each charge. If a saved setup points to a different or missing customer,
automatic top-up is disabled and must be configured again after the organization's billing
identity is reconciled. A shared payer email does not authorize another organization's card.

Organization owners and billing managers (the Billing role) manage top-ups and auto-recharge; admins do not. Polar records
the credit movement; Ryu stores only the organization policy that decides when
an automatic refill may be requested. The balance is shared by the organization,
not split into individual seat wallets.

## Moving credit and quest rewards

When a completed referral quest awards Ryu Credits, choose the organization
wallet that receives them. Your personal organization is selected by default;
you can choose another organization from the available list. **Not now** or
closing the dialog leaves the reward unclaimed.

While placement is pending, the organization selector and dialog actions are
locked. If placement fails, the reward remains claimable and reopening it keeps
your chosen organization for another attempt. A successful claim refreshes the
quest as claimed. Without an available organization, the claim cannot proceed.

In the desktop Credits page, Move credits lists the active account’s transferable grants and top-up balance. It leaves out any organization whose access policy (IP allowlist, required two-factor authentication, or session age) does not admit the current session, and a transfer that names such an organization is refused. If the list cannot load, Retry reads it again. Verification errors and failed transfers keep your selected grants and amount so you can try again; a successful transfer refreshes the available balance. While verification or a transfer is pending, Move credits waits for that action to finish.

A completed transfer is recorded in the activity log of both organizations, naming the member who moved the credit, the amounts, and the other organization. The transfer notification sent to owners and admins names them too. Retrying a transfer that already went through returns the same result but adds no second activity entry or notification, because no credit moved again.
