Ryu has four self-serve plans and one enterprise tier. The marketing pricing page at [ryuhq.com/pricing](https://ryuhq.com/pricing) shows the headline features; this page covers the enforced limits, credit pools, capability bands, and fine print.

<Callout type="info">
  These are the **enforced** limits, not marketing rounding. Annual billing gives two months free on every recurring plan.
</Callout>

## Plans at a glance

| Plan | Price | Billing | AI credit pool | Deposit fee |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | $0 | — | — | — |
| **Lifetime** | $29 one-time (launch price) | Once | $0 | cannot top up |
| **Pro** | $39/mo ($390/yr) | Monthly or annual | $15/mo | 13% |
| **Max** | $99/mo ($990/yr) | Monthly or annual | $30/mo | 12% |
| **Teams** | $49/seat/mo ($490/seat/yr) | Monthly or annual | $15/seat/mo | 12% |

Annual billing gives **two months free** (pay for 10 of 12 months).

---

## Free (no plan)

The desktop shell and local/BYOK chat are **always free**. The paywall is dismissible: closing it drops you into free local chat. Free users get:

- Local and BYOK chat (unlimited)
- Single agent, tool calling, MCP, skills
- Basic run-while-open background tasks
- All the numeric caps listed in [Free-tier limits](/docs/billing/limits)

Free users **cannot** top up credits or use managed inference.

---

## Lifetime (desktop license)

A one-time $29 purchase (launch price, normally $69). A personal, single-device license.

**What you unlock:**

- All Band 2 ("Pro") features forever: council, background runs, prompt studio, fine-tuning, evals, graphrag, companion overlay, clips
- Unlimited agents, workflows, spaces, plugins, skills, schedules
- 3 concurrent background runs, 20 GB space storage
- One year of app updates included (after that, buy again to extend or stay on your last version)

**What you do NOT get (Band 3 — subscription only):**

- Managed inference (no cloud models; you bring your own keys)
- Cloud sync, cloud nodes, hosted bots, team seats
- Agent Inboxes
- Monthly credit pool

**Billing scope:** personal (`"user"`). No org association.

---

## Pro

$39/mo or $390/yr. The entry-level managed plan. **Strictly single-seat:** one person, one price.

**What you unlock over Lifetime:**

- **Managed inference** — 300+ cloud models via OpenRouter, no API keys needed
- **$15/mo included AI credits** — use-it-or-lose-it each billing period; top up any time for more
- **Agent Inboxes** — unlimited inboxes, 1,000 sends/mo, 5 GB storage, branding removable
- **Cloud sync** — sync state across devices
- Unlimited everything (agents, tabs, workflows, etc.)
- 3 concurrent background runs, 20 GB space storage
- 13% deposit fee on credit top-ups

**Billing scope:** personal (`"user"`). Cannot be used by multi-member orgs. If your org has 2+ members, you need Max or Teams.

**Seats:** always one. If your org has 2+ members, you need Teams.

---

## Max

$99/mo or $990/yr. The power tier for one person running agents around the clock.

**What you unlock over Pro:**

- **Double the cloud node** — 4 vCPU, 8 GB RAM, 80 GB SSD, against Pro's 2 vCPU / 4 GB. Auto-provisioned when the subscription activates; auto-removed on cancellation. Your agents keep running when your computer is off.
- **12% deposit fee** on credit top-ups, down from Pro's 13%
- **$30/mo included AI credits**
- **Agent Inboxes** — unlimited inboxes, 25,000 sends/mo, 10 GB storage
- **50 GB space storage** (vs Pro's 20 GB)
- **Priority support**

**Billing scope:** org or personal.

**Seats:** single. Max is the individual power tier. For multiple people, use Teams.

### Max cloud node lifecycle

1. **Subscription activates** → your free node is provisioned (or reinstated if one was scheduled for removal)
2. **Subscription cancels** → the node is scheduled for removal after a grace period
3. **Larger instances** → available separately, priced from the live hardware catalog

The included node shows as "Included with your plan" in the org dashboard. There is no checkout step.

---

## Teams

$49/seat/mo or $490/seat/yr. Minimum 2 seats. For organizations that want shared billing, pooled credits, and role-based access.

A team seat costs more than Pro because it buys governance, not more inference. The per-person credit allowance is the same.

### What you unlock

- **One bill, one shared wallet** — $15/seat/mo of AI credits, pooled across the whole org
- **Volume pricing** — 5% off from 10 seats, 10% from 25, 15% from 50, applied automatically
- **A free cloud node that grows with your team** — 2 vCPU · 4 GB to start, stepping up to 4 vCPU · 8 GB at 10 seats and again at 25
- **Agent Inboxes** — unlimited inboxes, 100,000 sends/mo, 20 GB storage (org-wide, not per-seat)
- **8 concurrent background runs** (vs Pro/Max's 3)
- **50 GB space storage**
- **12% deposit fee** on top-ups
- **Channel users** — limit scales with seat count (1 channel user per seat)

### Billing scope

Teams is always **org-scoped** (`"org"`). Billing is charged under the canonical org billing email (the earliest owner's email). Only **owner** or **admin** members can manage billing.

### Seat model

Billed per seat, minimum 2. The seat count is validated when you buy:

- Must be at least the org's live member count
- Must be at least 2
- Changing seats requires the owner or admin role

The credit pool scales with seats: 5 seats = 5 × $15 = $75/mo pooled.

### RBAC (role-based access control)

Teams has a full permission system with four built-in roles:

| Role | Billing | Members | Config | Runs | Data |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Owner** | Full | Full | Full | Full | Full |
| **Admin** | — | Full | Full | Full | Full |
| **Member** | — | — | View | Run | Read/Write |
| **Viewer** | — | — | View | — | Read |

**Custom roles** are also supported. You can create roles with any combination of 17 granular permissions:

`gateway.view`, `gateway.configure`, `workflow.view`, `workflow.run`, `workflow.edit`, `workflow.delete`, `space.read`, `space.write`, `space.delete`, `agent.view`, `agent.run`, `agent.edit`, `tool.exec`, `members.manage`, `roles.manage`, `billing.manage`, `audit.view`

### Shared wallet

Teams has a **pooled org wallet**. Every member draws from the same balance:

- Monthly plan credits are injected per billing period (use-it-or-lose-it)
- Top-up credits roll over and never expire
- All members can see the balance; only owner/admin can top up or change auto-recharge
- The wallet is visible in **Settings → Billing** (desktop) and **Organizations → Billing** (web)

### Org dashboard

Teams gets an aggregation dashboard at `/organizations/[orgId]/dashboard`:

- Eval leaderboard across all agents
- Shared budgets and spend tracking
- Audit table of agent actions
- Request/error/cost/token stats across all gateways

### Teams management

The org admin can:

- **Invite members** by email with a role (admin/member)
- **Change member roles** (owner/admin/member/viewer dropdown)
- **Create teams** (groups of org members, not to be confused with the "Teams" plan name)
- **Manage seats** — adjust billed seat count, see over-allocation alerts
- **Config-as-code** — org-wide gateway config with revision history and rollback
- **Policies** — locked guardrails, approved models, allowed regions

### What Teams does NOT include

- **Cloud capacity scales by team SIZE, not per seat** — you get one free node that is resized up as the team grows (2 vCPU · 4 GB, then 4 vCPU · 8 GB at 10 seats and again at 25), not a machine per seat. Additional or larger instances are purchased separately.
- **No `team_workspace` feature** — that's the agent-team orchestration feature (groups of agents with coordination strategies), which is a separate concept from the Teams billing plan.

## Capability bands

Features are gated in three bands. The rule: runs on the user's machine at zero marginal cost → Band 1/2; Ryu pays a recurring bill → Band 3.

| Band | Name | Unlocked by | Features |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Free | Always | Local/BYOK chat, agents, tool calling, MCP, skills, basic runs |
| **2** | Pro | Subscription, license key, OR trial | Council, local background runs, gateway governance UI, prompt studio, fine-tuning, evals, graphrag, companion overlay, clips |
| **3** | Subscription | Active recurring plan only | Managed inference, cloud sync, cloud node, hosted bots, team seats, Agent Inboxes |

<Callout type="warn">
  Lifetime license holders get Band 2 but **not** Band 3. Band 3 features cost Ryu money every month, so they require an active subscription.
</Callout>

## What the deposit fee actually buys

Ryu is **keyless**. You do not sign up for OpenRouter, Composio, Replicate, Fal,
OpenAI, Anthropic or Groq. You do not hold seven accounts, seven billing
relationships, seven sets of API keys, or seven invoices to reconcile. Ryu holds
them and runs the calls on your behalf (models, tool calls, image and video
generation, speech in and out) against one balance.

**Models are passed through at cost. We do not mark up tokens.** Most platforms
take their margin inside the unit price, where you cannot see it: a GPU that
costs a few cents a minute is resold at several dollars an hour. We take ours
once, visibly, when you add credit, so $1,000 of credit buys $1,000 of models at
list price.

That fee is mostly not margin. It covers what it costs to hold those
relationships for you:

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Upstream credit purchase | **5.5%** — what our model provider charges us to buy the credits you spend |
| Payment processing | **~4.5%** — card, tax handling and merchant-of-record fees |
| **Cost floor** | **~10%** |

Which is why the rate starts at 15% and **falls when you subscribe**: 13% on
Pro, 12% on Max and Teams. A subscription is the cheapest way to lower it.

## Deposit fees (credit top-ups)

Credits are prepaid: you spend your balance down as you go. Models are metered **at cost** (no per-token markup). A one-time deposit fee applies on each top-up:

| Plan | Fee rate | Minimum |
|---|---|---|
| Free | Cannot top up | — |
| Lifetime | Cannot top up | — |
| Pro | 13% | $2.40 |
| Max / Teams | 12% | $2.40 |

The fee is the **greater of** the percentage and the $2.40 floor, so small top-ups are poor value and larger ones are better.

The deposit fee is the only place Ryu takes anything on inference. It covers what our upstream provider charges to buy credits, plus card processing. Model tokens themselves are passed through at cost, with no per-token markup, which is why a plan's included pool and a top-up buy exactly the same thing.

<Callout type="info">
  Teams gets **volume pricing**: 5% off from 10 seats, 10% from 25, and 15% from 50. It is applied automatically at checkout.
</Callout>

## Cloud node changes and downtime

Your included cloud node is resized in place when your plan changes or your team
crosses a seat band (Pro → Max, a Teams seat tier, or back down), and **a resize
requires a brief restart**: Hetzner cannot change a machine's CPU and memory
while it is running. Expect a short interruption, typically a minute or two,
during which agents running on that node are paused.

Two things worth knowing:

- **Nothing is deleted and nothing is lost.** The disk is untouched by a resize.
- **It works in both directions.** If your team shrinks, the node scales back
  down, so you are not stranded on a larger machine you no longer need.

If the timing matters, and it usually does because agents run unattended, the
resize follows the seat change that triggered it. Making the seat change during a
quiet hour is what controls when the restart happens.
