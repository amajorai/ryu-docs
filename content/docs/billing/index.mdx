title: "Billing and Plans"
description: "Ryu's individual and organization plans, credits, metering, limits, and organization billing, for account owners and builders."

This realm explains what each Ryu plan includes, how prepaid credits and metering work, and how
an organization manages seats, members, and billing. Self-hosting stays free when you run the
software and infrastructure yourself; the managed plans add hosted AI, cloud computers, and
shared organization controls. For the business overview, see the
[public pricing page](https://ryuhq.com/pricing). The full plan table is on
[Plans and Pricing](/docs/billing/plans).
