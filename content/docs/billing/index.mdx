Ryu's current public managed offer has an Individual shelf for Marketplace
Membership, Pro, and Max, plus Teams, Business, and Enterprise for shared
organization work. Self-hosting remains free when you want to run the software and the
infrastructure yourself. See [Teams seats](/docs/billing/seats)
for the organization membership and billing boundary.

## Organization billing settings

Open the organization switcher, choose **Organization settings**, then select
the **Billing** tab. The tab is organization-scoped, so owners and admins manage
the shared plan and payment details while members can still view the shared
credit balance and usage.

The billing tab includes:

- the current plan, renewal status, upgrade action, and a link to view all plans;
- payment-method management through the secure billing portal;
- invoice history and a link to hosted invoice details;
- the shared credit balance, included monthly pool, rollover top-up balance, and
  credit usage;
- manual credit top-ups, low-balance email alerts, and bounded automatic top-up
  settings.

Plan changes, payment methods, cancellations, and invoice downloads open in the
provider portal. Ryu keeps the organization role check on every billing action.

This is the implementation reference for builders integrating with Ryu. For the business overview,
see the [public pricing page](https://ryuhq.com/pricing); the limits and billing rules below are the
source of truth for how the product behaves.

Marketplace Membership starts at **$9.99/month for one user**. Additional users are
billed at the same **$9.99/user/month** rate. It includes only publisher-opted-in
paid Marketplace apps; it does not include managed inference, hosted nodes, or AI
credits.

The Teams rate is **$50 per member seat/month**. Annual billing is **$500 per
seat/year**, with five seats required (**$2,500/year minimum**; two months
free), shown as the equivalent of about **$208/month** at the minimum;
the public pricing page opens on the yearly option, with monthly billing still
available. A private proof arrangement may be offered in conversation; it is
not a public price or a self-serve checkout option.

Business is the optional premium organization plan. It starts at **$300/month
for five member seats**, then adds **$50/month per additional seat**. Its pooled
AI-credit grant starts at **$100/month** for five billed seats and adds $100 for
each additional five-seat bundle. Business is available to organizations of
any eligible size, including teams with fewer than 25 members; it is a premium
anchor, not a 25-member gate.

## Free

The free local/self-hosted baseline includes local and bring-your-own-key chat,
agents, tools, MCP, skills, and basic runs. A self-hosted operator is not bound
by Ryu's managed-path starter caps. The signed-in managed free path is smaller:
three tabs, three agents, and three workflows, with the full starter limits
listed in [Free-tier limits](/docs/billing/limits).

The local desktop license is a separate local-first path. It does not include
managed inference, hosted nodes, hosted agents, or a monthly AI pool.

## Individual plans

Pro and Max provide managed inference and a personal workspace for one person;
Marketplace Membership provides Marketplace app access only. Individual plans
do not include shared organization ownership, member roles, a shared wallet,
organization audit history, or the hosted business-automation allowance.

If another person needs their own login, shared agents, shared budgets, or a
single organization bill, use Teams or Business. Sharing a personal login is not a
team entitlement and does not create organization controls or an audit trail.

## Marketplace Membership

Marketplace Membership is a **$9.99/user/month recurring plan** for publisher-opted-in paid
Marketplace apps, with a one-user minimum. It does not include managed inference, hosted nodes,
hosted agents, or AI credits. It is an individual plan, and access is resolved against the buyer's
personal organization so the same server-side entitlement path works for desktop, web, and
Marketplace installs.

Existing recurring Pro, Max, and Teams plans also carry Marketplace app access. The one-time
Desktop license is intentionally excluded: it grants the desktop product, not Membership. A direct
license bought for an individual Marketplace app remains valid independently of Membership.

The buyer experience is explicit: eligible catalog cards say **Included with Membership**; a user
without an active qualifying recurring plan sees **Upgrade to use**. If a plan lapses, Ryu leaves the
installed package in place but blocks launching or using the Membership app until the plan renews or
the buyer has a direct item license.

Publishers opt in per paid app after enabling Stripe Connect payouts. For each paid recurring
period, Ryu allocates a default **70% publisher pool** using first-use signals and deterministic
price-tier weights. Transfers are idempotent and use the publisher's existing Connect account. A
publisher can review eligible apps, pending totals, paid totals, failed transfers, and usage-signal
counts in the Marketplace Sell tab. One-time and bounded-update Marketplace sales remain direct
item transactions; they do not grant Membership by themselves, but an opted-in app can still earn
from the separate recurring pool.

## Teams

Teams starts at **$250/month for five member seats**. Additional seats are
**$50/month each** through the 50-seat self-serve limit. A seat is a person who
can access the organization's shared workspace; it is not a separate AI wallet,
node, or locked-to-one-person agent. Measure the hours returned, the backlog
cleared, and the dollars that capacity would otherwise require.

Teams includes:

- **$50/month of managed AI credits at five billed seats**, pooled across the
  organization and increased by $50 for every additional five billed seats;
- managed setup and an included 2 vCPU, 4 GB RAM, 40 GB SSD node;
- organization-scoped access, spend controls, and audit history;
- unlimited Agent Inboxes, 100,000 sends/month, and 20 GB stored mail;
- 20 GB of space storage and 8 concurrent background runs;
- 12% deposit fee on additional prepaid credits.
- Marketplace access for publisher-opted-in paid apps through the active recurring plan.

The billed seat count is enforced against Better Auth organization members after
any active negotiated capacity allowance is applied. Teams supports up to
50 billed seats in self-serve. Larger organizations use Enterprise. The full
invite, acceptance, upgrade, downgrade, and proration behavior is documented in
[Teams seats](/docs/billing/seats). Business uses the same human-seat boundary
and self-serve ceiling, but its pooled credit grant and included node are larger.

## Business

Business starts at **$300/month for five human seats**. Additional seats are
**$50/month each**, and annual billing charges ten monthly units. The shared
AI-credit pool is **$100/month at five billed seats**, increasing by $100 for
each additional five-seat bundle. Business also includes a performance managed
node, organization-scoped access, spend controls, audit history, Marketplace
access, and the same Agent Inbox/storage boundary as Teams.

Business is available to any eligible organization, including one with fewer
than 25 members. The plan is positioned for larger teams and serves as a clear
premium option beside Teams; it does not turn human seats into AI-automation
limits.

## Enterprise

Enterprise is the conversation path for organizations that need more process
capacity, dedicated or self-hosted deployment, SSO/SCIM, custom data-region or
retention requirements, procurement terms, or named onboarding. Contact us so
the contract reflects the workflows, hours, data boundaries, and support level
you actually need.

## Credits and deposit fees

Included Teams credits are **use-it-or-lose-it** each billing period. Credits you
buy separately roll over and never expire. Model usage is metered at cost by default; provider-level
billing policies may explicitly keep a provider at raw cost or apply the Gateway's configured
markup. The deposit fee is the visible platform fee for the default managed offer. See
[Credits & AI Pools](/docs/billing/credits) for the wallet and top-up rules.

## Price versions and grandfathering

The price and included pool agreed at subscription time are recorded for that
organization. If Ryu raises the public price later, existing contracted
organizations keep their contracted price and included pool; new organizations
see the new offer and any additional included credits that fund it. This keeps
the benefit of joining early concrete without making a future customer pay for
an older package.

## Cloud node changes and downtime

An included node is runtime capacity for the organization; it does not own the
seat quantity or workflow capacity. A resize or replacement can briefly restart
the node, but it does not cross organization data boundaries or change the
contracted allowance. Larger or dedicated capacity is scoped through Enterprise.
