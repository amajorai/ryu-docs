---
title: "Gift cards, credits, and subscriptions"
description: "Buy and redeem Ryu gift cards, credit gifts, and non-renewing personal subscriptions."
---

Ryu supports three separate gift products from the web at `/gifts`:

- **Gift cards** load stored value into the recipient's personal Ryu wallet.
- **Credit gifts** add expiring Ryu Credits for supported managed features.
- **Subscription gifts** provide non-renewing Plus, Pro, or Max access for a
  selected number of months.

You can reach **Gift Ryu** from the public [pricing page](/pricing), your signed-in
dashboard, or the account menu. The `/gifts` page is the complete purchase and
redemption flow.

See [Billing & Plans](/docs/billing) for the broader plan and credit boundary,
[Credits & usage](/docs/billing/credits) for credit spending, and
[organization access](/docs/billing/organization-access) for team-owned billing.

Gift purchases are available to active Plus, Pro, and Max personal plans. Team
and Business organization billing stays organization-owned; it is not changed by
a personal gift. The purchaser pays once, and the gift is not active or
shareable until payment has been confirmed.

## Buy a gift

1. Open **Gift Ryu** from the pricing page, dashboard, or account menu, or visit
   `/gifts` directly on the web.
2. Choose a gift type and amount, or choose a plan and duration for a
   subscription gift.
3. Optionally add the recipient's name, email address, and a message.
4. Complete the hosted checkout.
5. After payment clears, copy the private claim link from **Your gifts**, or let
   Ryu send it to the recipient email when email delivery is configured.

The recipient email is delivery information, not an ownership check. Anyone who
has the complete private claim link may be able to redeem it, so do not post the
link publicly. Ryu does not store the raw claim secret.

## Redemption rules

Open the link on the web, sign in to the personal account that should receive the
gift, review the details, and choose **Redeem**. A claim link can be used once.
The account selected at confirmation is the account that receives the value;
Ryu cannot promise to move a gift after it has been redeemed.

| Gift | Purchase range or choice | Claim window | After redemption |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gift card | $15–$250 | Does not expire | Stored value has no cash value, cannot be transferred, and is used for eligible Ryu usage |
| Ryu Credits | $5–$1,000 | 30 days after purchase | Credits expire 12 months after redemption and are spent before paid rollover balance |
| Subscription | Plus, Pro, or Max for 1, 3, 6, or 12 months | 365 days after purchase | Full personal plan access for the selected duration; it does not renew |

Subscription gifts use the selected plan's current monthly price multiplied by
the chosen duration. They are one-time purchases; annual subscription discounts
do not apply to a gift duration.

Gift cards and credit gifts load the recipient's personal workspace, never the
currently selected team workspace. If the account has no personal workspace,
redemption is refused until one is available. Credits are still subject to the
plan and feature that uses them; redeeming a credit gift does not itself start a
subscription.

Only one personal subscription can be active at a time. If the recipient already
has an active personal subscription or an active gifted subscription, Ryu refuses
the subscription gift instead of silently stacking, downgrading, or charging a
second plan. A gift can be claimed after the existing personal subscription ends
while its claim window remains open. Organization and Enterprise access cannot be
converted into a personal gift subscription.

## Expiry, refunds, and payment state

Ryu only activates a gift from a verified paid order. Returning from a hosted
checkout page is not proof of payment; Ryu re-checks the provider order if the
signed payment notification has not arrived yet. A retry with the same checkout
request is idempotent and cannot create a second gift or second charge.

An unclaimed credit or subscription gift expires after its claim window. Ryu
requests a refund to the purchaser's original payment method and keeps the gift
unavailable while the provider processes that refund. A gift card has no expiry
after purchase. Once any gift has been redeemed, its value cannot be transferred
to another account or exchanged for cash.

If a payment is refunded or charged back before redemption, the gift becomes
unavailable. If a refund or chargeback arrives after redemption, Ryu reverses
the full gifted value without taking paid top-ups; a recipient who already spent
the gift may see a negative personal wallet until it is replenished. A temporary
checkout, email, or webhook delay does not make a gift claimable early; check
**Your gifts** again after the payment settles. Never send a full claim link or
payment credentials in a public support request.

## Common problems

- **The gift is still pending:** the provider has not made a paid order visible
  yet. Reopen the receipt or try the link again later.
- **The link was already used:** the first confirmed redemption owns the gift;
  the same link cannot be moved to another account.
- **The recipient has a current subscription:** wait until that personal plan
  ends, then redeem before the gift's claim deadline. Team and Business members
  should use their organization billing instead.
- **The gift went to the wrong account:** contact support with the purchase
  receipt and approximate time. Do not include the full claim secret; recovery
  may be limited after redemption.
- **The email never arrived:** the purchaser can copy the link again from
  **Your gifts**. The recipient address does not reserve the gift.
