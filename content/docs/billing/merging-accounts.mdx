---
title: "Merging Two Accounts"
description: "Fold a second Ryu account into the one you use, what moves with it, and what has to be sorted out first."
---

If you signed up with one address and then again with another, you can fold the second account into the one you use. Its workspaces, credit, history, and sign-in methods come with it.

## You probably don't need this for the same address

If both signups used the **same** email address, there is only one account already. Signing up with an address that is already registered does not create a second account. Ryu emails you a note that you already have a Ryu account instead. Signing in with Google attaches that login to the existing account with the same address.

Merging is for the case email matching can't solve: **one person, two different addresses**.

## How it works

<Steps>

<Step>
### Start it from the account you're keeping

Sign into the account you want to **keep** on the Ryu website and open **Account Settings** (`/settings`). Under **Merge another account**, choose **Merge Account** and enter the other account's address. Direction is fixed: the account you're signed into always survives. Continue checks whether the merge can start without revealing whether the other address has a Ryu account. Editing that address clears the check; Cancel or Escape closes the dialog and clears the draft, so reopening starts fresh.
</Step>

<Step>
### Confirm it's you

You are asked for a second factor: an authenticator code, a backup code, or an emailed code. Merging can't be undone, so a password alone isn't enough. We email at most one code a minute to an address, and a sign-in code counts toward that minute, so if you choose **Send it again** sooner, the prompt tells you how long to wait and the code we already sent still works.
</Step>

<Step>
### Both inboxes confirm

We email a link to **both** addresses. The link to your own address is sent even if we emailed you a code a moment before. Opening a link shows which accounts are involved; nothing is confirmed until you press the confirm button on that page, and nothing moves until both sides have confirmed. The links last 24 hours, and either side can cancel until then.
</Step>

<Step>
### Done

The merge runs and we email you a summary of anything that needed a decision.
</Step>

</Steps>

## What comes across

| | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Workspaces** | Every workspace on the other account becomes one of yours. They stay separate workspaces and are not combined into one. |
| **Credit** | Stays in the workspace that holds it. Nothing is moved or lost. To pool it afterwards, use **transfer credit between your workspaces**. |
| **Google / social logins** | Move across. Signing in with that Google account still takes you to your merged account. |
| **Chats, agents, servers, licenses** | Move across with their workspace. |
| **Your profile** | Yours is kept. Anything you'd left blank is filled in from the other account. |
| **Referrals** | Counts add up. A referral between the two accounts is dropped, because you cannot refer yourself. |

## What doesn't

<Callout type="warn">
  **The other address stops being a sign-in.** Ryu stores one email address per account, so after the merge you sign in with the address you kept. Any Google or other social login attached to the old address keeps working and lands you in the merged account.
</Callout>

- **Personal access tokens and organization API keys** move across but arrive **disabled**. Create fresh ones when you need them.
- **Password**: the account you kept keeps its password. A password on the other account is discarded.
- **Two-factor**: your existing setup stays. The other account's is discarded.
- **Free-tier entitlements don't stack.** A desktop trial keeps its original start date, a promotional credit claimed on both accounts counts once, and two updates windows don't add up. Merging never buys a second free anything.
- **You stay signed in.** The other account's sessions end, along with any live connections they had open.
- **Scheduled personal mail is paused.** Drafts the other account had scheduled from its personal inbox arrive as ordinary drafts, so nothing sends until you review and reschedule them. A send that was already under way waits for you to confirm whether it went out. Scheduled drafts in a shared workspace keep their schedule.
- **Webhook relay connections** move across. If both accounts used the same connection name, the other account's is renamed so neither is lost.

## When we'll say no

Some conditions block a merge until you resolve them. If the condition is about **your** account, the dialog tells you right away. If it is about the **other** account, Ryu emails the reason to that address. Ryu does not report another account's state to whoever typed its address.

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| The other account pays for a plan | Cancel it first. Subscriptions can't be moved between accounts. |
| A workspace on it pays for a plan under its address | Move or cancel that plan first. |
| It's the only owner of a shared workspace | Hand ownership to someone else first. |
| Both accounts are in the affiliate program | Contact support. Support combines those by hand. |
| The other account holds credit with no workspace to move it to | Contact support. |
| Support currently has access to either account | Revoke support access first. |
| The other account owns a family | Turn off family sharing on it first. |
| Together the accounts are in more than five families | Leave a family first. |
| Either account has an email change in flight | Finish or cancel it. |
| Either is a Ryu staff account | Contact support. |

## Safety

- **It can't be undone.** We keep a record of what moved so support can help, but there's no self-serve reverse.
- **Both sides have to agree.** A merge started from a stolen session still needs the other mailbox, and the address you are keeping gets an email the moment one is requested. If you did not ask for it, ignore the link, change your password, and sign out your other devices.
- **Repeated confirmations share one run.** Clicking either link while the merge
  is running returns its current processing state instead of starting another merge.
- **If a step reports a failure**, open the confirmation link again to resume.
  Steps that already finished are not repeated.
- **If the merge stays stuck in processing**, contact support.
