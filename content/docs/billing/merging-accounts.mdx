title: "Merging Two Accounts"
description: "Fold a second Ryu account into the one you use, what moves with it, and what has to be sorted out first."
---

Signed up with Google using one address, then signed up again with another? You can fold the second account into the one you actually use — its workspaces, credit, history and sign-in methods come with it.

## You probably don't need this for the same address

If both signups used the **same** email address, there is only one account already. Signing up with an address that's registered doesn't create a second account — we email you a "you already have a Ryu account" note instead. And signing in with Google attaches that login to the existing account with the same address.

Merging is for the case email matching can't solve: **one person, two different addresses**.

## How it works

<Steps>

<Step>
### Start it from the account you're keeping

Sign into the account you want to **keep**, open **Settings → Account**, and enter the other account's address. Direction is fixed: the account you're signed into always survives.
</Step>

<Step>
### Confirm it's you

You'll be asked for a second factor — an authenticator code, a backup code, or a code we email you. Merging can't be undone, so a password alone isn't enough.
</Step>

<Step>
### Both inboxes confirm

We email a link to **both** addresses. Nothing moves until both are clicked. The links last 24 hours, and either side can cancel until then.
</Step>

<Step>
### Done

The merge runs and we email you a summary of anything that needed a decision.
</Step>

</Steps>

## What comes across

| | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Workspaces** | Every workspace on the other account becomes one of yours. They stay separate workspaces — they are not combined into one. |
| **Credit** | Stays in the workspace that holds it. Nothing is moved or lost. To pool it afterwards, use **transfer credit between your workspaces**. |
| **Google / social logins** | Move across. Signing in with that Google account still takes you to your merged account. |
| **Chats, agents, nodes, licences** | Move across with their workspace. |
| **Your profile** | Yours is kept. Anything you'd left blank is filled in from the other account. |
| **Referrals** | Counts add up. A referral between the two accounts is dropped — you can't refer yourself. |

## What doesn't

<Callout type="warn">
  **The other address stops being a sign-in.** Ryu stores one email address per account, so after the merge you sign in with the address you kept. Any Google or other social login attached to the old address keeps working and lands you in the merged account.
</Callout>

- **API keys** move across but arrive **disabled**. Create fresh ones when you need them.
- **Password**: the account you kept keeps its password. A password on the other account is discarded.
- **Two-factor**: your existing setup stays. The other account's is discarded.
- **Free-tier entitlements don't stack.** A desktop trial keeps its original start date, a promotional credit claimed on both accounts counts once, and two updates windows don't add up. Merging never buys a second free anything.
- **You stay signed in.** The other account's sessions end.

## When we'll say no

Some things have to be sorted out first. If it's about **your** account, we'll tell you on the spot. If it's about the **other** account, we email that address the reason — we won't report someone else's account state to whoever typed their address in.

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| The other account pays for a plan | Cancel it first. Subscriptions can't be moved between accounts. |
| A workspace on it pays for a plan under its address | Move or cancel that plan first. |
| It's the only owner of a shared workspace | Hand ownership to someone else first. |
| Both accounts are in the affiliate program | Contact support — we combine those by hand. |
| Either account has an email change in flight | Finish or cancel it. |
| Either is a Ryu staff account | Contact support. |

## Safety

- **It can't be undone.** We keep a record of what moved so support can help, but there's no self-serve reverse.
- **Both sides have to agree.** A merge started from a stolen session still needs the other mailbox, and the address you're keeping gets an email the moment one is requested — if you didn't ask for it, ignore the link, change your password, and sign out your other devices.
- **If it stops partway, nothing is lost.** Open the confirmation link again and it picks up where it left off.
