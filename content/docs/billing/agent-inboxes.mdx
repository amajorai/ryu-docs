---
title: "Agent Inboxes"
description: "Hosted agent email: the organization mailbox, agent addresses, plan limits, and storage."
---

Agent Inboxes give your organization one permanent hosted mailbox, and each of
your agents its own permanent address on it, to send from and receive into
without SMTP setup. The mailbox stores received messages for the organization,
and each agent reads and sends only its own mail. See
[Agent Inboxes](/docs/extend/develop/agent-inboxes) for how addresses work.

## Availability

Every account has the Free baseline, a small branded allowance. It is one
allowance per person, not one per organization: an organization without a paid
plan draws on its owner's allowance. The owner's personal workspace and every
unpaid organization they own share one mailbox, three agent addresses, one
monthly email count, and one storage cap.

Pro and Max add a personal allowance, and a private individual offer uses
that same personal boundary. Teams and Business provide the
allowance for a shared organization; a private organization offer uses that
same organization boundary. Paid plans can remove the Ryu branding
footer.

A Major Pass and the local desktop license don't add hosted mail, so buying one
keeps the Free baseline. Enterprise limits are set by contract and replace the
plan allowance for that organization.

## Limits by plan

| Plan | Mailboxes | Agent addresses | Emails / month | Stored mail | Branding removable |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Free, A Major Pass, desktop license | **1** | **3** | **50** | **1 GiB** | No |
| Pro | **Unlimited** | **Unlimited** | **5,000** | **10 GiB** | Yes |
| Max | **Unlimited** | **Unlimited** | **100,000** | **100 GiB** | Yes |
| Teams | **Unlimited** | **Unlimited** | **100,000** | **20 GiB** | Yes |
| Business | **Unlimited** | **Unlimited** | **100,000** | **20 GiB** | Yes |
| Enterprise | Contracted | Contracted | Contracted | Contracted | Contracted |

Each organization or personal account has one mailbox. "Unlimited" mailboxes
means each organization on the plan gets its own; on the Free baseline, one
person's mailbox allowance covers one organization.

The monthly email count is per recipient, matching how mail providers bill: a
message to 20 `to`, 15 `cc`, and 15 `bcc` addresses uses 50. An address listed
twice counts twice, because both copies go to the mail provider and are billed.
A message can have at most 50 recipients. On the Free baseline and under an
Enterprise contract, a message that would take the month past its limit is
refused whole; paid plans can send past it with overage (below). Storage is
measured in GiB (1 GiB is 1,073,741,824 bytes).

## Sending past the monthly allowance

On paid plans (Pro, Max, Teams, Business, and private paid offers), a send beyond the monthly
allowance is accepted while the organization has credits (its plan pool or a
positive balance). Each recipient beyond the allowance costs **$1.00 per 1,000
recipients**, charged to the organization's credits once the mail provider
accepts the message. A message that straddles the limit is charged only for
the recipients past it. A message the provider refuses is not charged, and
neither is one whose send ended without a definite answer from the provider
(`unresolved`), because Ryu cannot confirm it was accepted.

Without credits, a send beyond the allowance is refused. The Free
baseline never overflows into credits, an Enterprise contract's limit stays a
hard cap, and stored mail is always a hard cap. Overage counts toward your
organization's spend limits; see [Metering rates](/docs/billing/metering#ryu-hosted-services).

Paid plans cap total stored mail, not the number of agent addresses. An
organization or personal account can give every agent its own address within its
storage and monthly email allowance.

## Custom domains

Sending and receiving from your own domain needs a paid plan (Pro, Max,
Teams, Business, or a private paid offer) or an Enterprise contract; the Free baseline
uses the shared mail domain. A personal account can hold up to 5 custom domains
and an organization up to 20. A domain that isn't verified within 7 days of
being added is removed, so it doesn't count toward the limit. See
[custom domains](/docs/extend/develop/agent-inboxes-api#custom-domains) for setup.

## Agent addresses

Each agent can claim one address on the organization's mailbox. Pending and
active addresses count toward the plan's agent address limit, and so do agent
inboxes created before mailboxes; a revoked address frees its place but is never
reissued. Agent addresses add no emails or storage of their own: every agent's
sends and stored mail count toward the organization's allowance. On the Free
baseline, the three addresses are shared across the person's unpaid
organizations, like the rest of the baseline. An organization already over a
limit when it moved to mailboxes keeps what it has; only new claims are refused.

## Branding

Free outbound mail keeps the Ryu branding footer. On a paid plan you can remove
it from the mailbox; the setting is off by default so the organization chooses when to
expose its own sender identity. If the plan ends, outbound mail carries the
footer again, including from inboxes where it was removed.

## Enforcement

Limits are checked server-side.

- **Emails.** Every send, reply, forward, and draft send reserves one unit per
  recipient before the provider call. Units are reserved atomically per paid
  organization or personal account, or per person on the Free baseline, so
  parallel requests cannot all consume the last units. A message accepted by the
  mail provider stays counted even if its history row needs reconciliation, and
  so does an `unresolved` send, which may have been delivered; a
  provider-rejected send releases its units. A refused request names the limit
  and the message's recipient count. On a paid plan with credits, the send goes
  ahead and its recipients past the allowance are charged as overage instead;
  retrying the same message (the same `clientId` or `Idempotency-Key`) never
  charges it twice.
- **Stored mail.** Message bodies, attachments, and unsent drafts count toward
  the cap, in every inbox the allowance covers, including inboxes paused or
  deactivated after a plan ends. A send, reply, forward, or draft that would
  exceed it is refused with the limit.
- **Inbound mail over the cap** is not stored, and the sender gets no bounce.
  The drop is never silent: Ryu records a `message.received.blocked` event with
  the sender, subject, and reason in the mail event log and delivers it to your
  signed mail webhooks, and it notifies the people who receive the inbox's mail
  notifications, at most once a day. Delete messages or drafts, or upgrade, to
  receive mail again.
- **Mailboxes and agent addresses.** The Free baseline's one mailbox and three
  agent addresses, and an Enterprise contract's limits, are enforced
  atomically, so parallel claims cannot exceed them. A claim over the address
  limit returns `403` with `code: "alias_limit_reached"`. Revoking an address
  frees its place.

`GET /api/mail/usage` reports the allowance and what it has used: the mailbox,
agent address, email, and storage limits (`null` when unlimited), the emails
sent this month, and the agent addresses in use. The inbox usage endpoint
(`GET /api/mail/inboxes/:id/metrics/usage`) also reports the inbox's stored
bytes.

## When a plan ends

When a paid plan ends, the account returns to the Free baseline. Inboxes beyond
the Free baseline's one inbox are not deleted. The oldest inbox stays active;
the others enter a 30-day grace period in which they keep receiving mail but
sending is paused. After the grace period they are deactivated and stop storing
new mail, which is recorded as a `message.received.blocked` event. Their stored
history is kept and still counts toward the Free baseline's 1 GiB storage cap.
Delete an inbox or upgrade again to bring another one back.

If the billing service can't confirm a paid plan for a while, for example during
a provider outage, Ryu keeps applying the last confirmed paid allowance for up
to 72 hours, so a temporary lookup failure doesn't hold a paying account to the
Free limits. For the same reason, a cancelled plan's limits can apply for up to
72 hours after it ends.

## API

- **Agent send:** `POST /api/mail/agents/:agentId/send`
- **Agent read:** `GET /api/mail/agents/:agentId/messages`
- **Mailbox send and read:** `POST /api/mail/inboxes/:id/send` and
  `GET /api/mail/inboxes/:id/messages`
- **Incoming mail:** delivered to your signed mail webhooks

Inbound provider delivery ids are idempotent per inbox and agent address, so a webhook retry does not create another
stored message or agent notification. Inbox webhooks are SSRF-checked, time-bounded, and
dispatched best-effort after storage; a slow tenant endpoint never holds the inbound response open.
Deliveries are signed, except for inbox webhooks configured before signing until their secret is
rotated; see [verifying webhook deliveries](/docs/extend/develop/agent-inboxes-sending#verifying-webhook-deliveries).

For the full mail API, see [Agent Inboxes for developers](/docs/extend/develop/agent-inboxes) and [standalone Mail](/docs/standalone/mail).
