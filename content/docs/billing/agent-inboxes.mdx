---
title: "Agent Inboxes"
description: "Hosted email inboxes for agents, with plan limits and storage."
---

Agent Inboxes are hosted email inboxes your agents can send from and receive
into. Each inbox has its own address, stores received messages, and exposes
send/read operations for end-to-end email workflows without SMTP setup.

## Availability

The Free baseline includes a small branded allowance. Paid managed subscriptions
can add hosted inboxes; Pro and Max provide a personal allowance, while Teams
and Business provide the same capability for a shared organization and can
remove the Ryu branding footer per inbox. The local desktop license does not
include hosted Agent Inboxes. Enterprise limits are set by contract.

## Limits by plan

| Plan | Inboxes | Sends / month | Stored mail | Branding removable |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Free | **1** | **50** | **1 GB** | No |
| Pro | **Unlimited** | **10,000** | **20 GB** | Yes |
| Max | **Unlimited** | **100,000** | **100 GB** | Yes |
| Teams | **Unlimited** | **100,000** | **20 GB** | Yes |
| Business | **Unlimited** | **100,000** | **20 GB** | Yes |
| Enterprise | Contracted | Contracted | Contracted | Contracted |

The cap is on total stored mail, not inbox count. Paid organizations can create
the inboxes their workflows need while staying within the organization's storage
and monthly send allowance.

## Branding

Free outbound mail keeps the Ryu branding footer. Teams and Business can remove it per inbox;
the setting is off by default so the organization chooses when to expose its own
sender identity.

## Enforcement

The send and storage limits are checked server-side. When a limit is reached,
the inbox reports the specific limit and the next action instead of silently
dropping mail.

Send slots are reserved atomically per organization or personal account. Parallel requests cannot
all consume the final monthly slot. A message accepted by the mail provider remains counted even if
its history row needs reconciliation; a provider-rejected send releases its reservation.

## API

- **Send:** `POST /api/mail/inboxes/:inboxId/send`
- **Read:** `GET /api/mail/inboxes/:inboxId/messages`
- **Incoming mail:** delivered to the configured agent webhook

Inbound provider delivery ids are idempotent per inbox, so a webhook retry does not create another
stored message or agent notification. Inbox webhooks are SSRF-checked, time-bounded, and dispatched
best-effort after storage; a slow tenant endpoint never holds the inbound response open.
