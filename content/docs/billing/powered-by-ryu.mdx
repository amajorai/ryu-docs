---
title: "Powered by Ryu Badge"
description: "Which public pages carry the Powered by Ryu badge, and which plans remove it."
---

Public pages that Ryu serves for you carry a small **Powered by Ryu** badge on
the Free baseline. It links to ryuhq.com, so people who see your page can find
out what built it. Paid subscription plans remove it automatically. These are the
same plans that can remove the [mail branding footer](/docs/billing/agent-inboxes#branding).

| Surface | Where the badge appears |
| --- | --- |
| Published Sites | Pinned to the bottom-right corner of every published page |
| Forms | A **Made with Ryu Forms** link under the respondent form |
| Public feedback boards | Pinned to the bottom-right corner of the public board |
| Embedded public agents | Pinned to the corner of the `/embed/agents/...` frame |
| Assistant widget | Under the transcript, when the host turns it on |

## Which plans remove it

| Plan | Badge |
| --- | --- |
| Free, trial | Shown |
| Desktop license, A Major Pass | Shown |
| Pro, Max, Teams, Business | Removed |

The badge stays on if Ryu cannot confirm your plan, for example on a headless
node that has never signed in, or while the plan lookup is unavailable. It does not
fall back to unbranded.

## When a plan change takes effect

- **Embedded agents** update within about ten minutes.
- **Sites, Forms and feedback boards** run on your node. The desktop app reports
  your plan to the node each time it checks your access, and each app picks up the
  change the next time it starts, for example after the node restarts.

Self-hosted operators running an app outside the desktop can set
`RYU_BRANDING_REMOVABLE=1` in that app's environment to remove the badge.
