---
title: "Organization Invitations"
description: "Invite people to an organization, handle invitation email limits, and review, decline, revoke, or block invitations."
---

Organization owners and admins invite people by email address. Each pending invitation reserves
a seat, and acceptance is checked again on the server. This page is for owners and admins who
manage membership, and for anyone who receives an invitation. Seat billing is on
[Teams Seats](/docs/billing/seats).

## Invite members

Organization owners and admins can open **Members → Invite a member** and enter
the email address of each person to invite. Invitees receive an
accept-invitation link, and people without an account receive a sign-up link
that returns them to the invitation.
Invitations are sent by email address only: a pending invitation shows its
address to the organization, so Ryu does not invite someone from their
username, and a username matches only people who are already members.
Personal workspaces cannot look up or invite recipients, and lookups are
rate-limited per account and follow the organization's access policy.
Team assignment is optional; choose **No team** to leave an invite unassigned.

A username shows the person's public profile name and picture, not their email
address. Ryu looks up the address when it sends the invitation. Recipient
lookups are rate-limited per account; if you add many recipients in a short
time, wait a few minutes before adding more.

Each pending invitation reserves one organization seat. The Members page, and
**Members** in the organization switcher, list only invitations that are still
pending: accepted, declined, cancelled, and expired invitations are not shown,
and older finished invitations never push a pending one off the list.
The Members page shows active members as `active / capacity`; the invite
control is disabled when all effective seats are active or reserved. Cancel a
pending invitation, remove a member, or buy more seats before sending another
invite. Acceptance is checked again on the server, so an invitation cannot
grant access after the seat was used by someone else.

Each of those two lists loads at most 2,000 pending invitations. Past that, a
notice says that pending invitations beyond it are not shown. If the list
cannot be loaded, the Members page and the dialog say so instead of showing
none.

### When an invitation email is not sent

Invitation email shares a per-recipient limit with Ryu's other notification
email: one email to the same address per minute and 20 per UTC day. When an
invitation's email cannot be sent, Ryu removes the invitation and releases its
seat; an invitation accepted or cancelled while its email was still being sent
keeps that outcome. If the limit refused the email, the Members page
says so and how long to wait: a minute, or the whole hours until the daily
limit resets, for example **Please wait 60 seconds before sending it again.** The
recipient stays in the form, so choose **Send invite** again after the wait. If
the email could not be sent for another reason, such as an email service
outage, the page says so and that no invitation was created; try again in a few
minutes.

If **Resend** cannot send the email, the Members page shows the message next to
that pending invitation, with the wait when the limit refused it. The
invitation stays pending with its original expiry, and **Resend** after the
wait sends the email and renews the invitation. API clients of Better Auth's
`invite-member` endpoint receive HTTP 429 with a `Retry-After` header and the
code `INVITATION_EMAIL_RATE_LIMITED`, or HTTP 503 with
`INVITATION_EMAIL_NOT_SENT` and no wait when the email could not be sent for
another reason.

If roster retrieval on **Organizations → Members** fails, choose **Retry loading
members** after the service recovers to fetch the current organization members.

## Review, decline, and block

The user-menu **Invitations** page separates requests into **Received** and
**Sent**. **Received** lists only invitations you can still accept: an expired
invitation is not shown, and its count excludes it. Accept an invitation or
choose **Decline and block**. **Sent** lists the active organization's
invitations that are still pending, newest first; accepted, declined,
cancelled, and expired invitations are not shown, and older finished
invitations never push a pending one off the list.
Resend a pending invitation, or choose **Revoke invitation** to prevent
acceptance while keeping its audit history.

Accepting and revoking settle the
same pending invitation exactly once: if the person accepts first, the revoke is
refused with the invitation's accepted state and their membership stays (remove
the member separately if needed); if the revoke lands first, the acceptance
fails and no membership is created. The tab loads at most 2,000 pending
invitations; past that, its count ends in **+** and a notice says that the
pending invitations beyond it are not shown. A blocked organization cannot send
that person another invitation.

For safety, the same organization cannot send another invitation to the same
email address for 24 hours. A decline is stronger than the cooldown and blocks
future invitations from that organization for that recipient.

Invitation activity is transactional. The invited person receives the invitation or account
confirmation email, and the organization's configured notification roles receive Inbox activity
for invitations sent, accepted, declined, or cancelled. See
[Billing notifications](/docs/billing/notifications#organization-controls) for recipients and switches.
