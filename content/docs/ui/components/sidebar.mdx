## Navigation presentation slots

Use `spacing="relaxed"` on `SidebarHeader` for a wider gap between its children. `SidebarGroupLabel` accepts `appearance="collapsible-section"` for a spaced section label with sidebar foreground and ordinary small text. Keep the rendered button, collapse state, and disabled behavior at the calling site.

Set `layout="navigation-stack"` on `SidebarGroupContent` for a vertical navigation stack. `SidebarMenuBadge` accepts `appearance="pending"` for a muted compact label; existing active and hovered menu-button states still control its foreground. The badge does not determine item availability or render a pending label automatically.

These props belong to the named slots, not the `Sidebar` root. Omitting them preserves each slot’s ordinary treatment. Existing render props, native attributes, and class-name overrides remain available. Compact and caption text follow the shared theme scale.

## Frames and navigation purposes

Use `appearance="settings-frame"` on `SidebarProvider` for a rounded sidebar surface. On `Sidebar`, `appearance="showcase-frame"` provides a transparent inset frame and `appearance="settings-navigation"` rounds the navigation frame. These `Sidebar` appearances apply to its non-collapsible or desktop frame; the mobile collapsible sheet retains its existing presentation.

On `SidebarTrigger`, use `framing="storyboard-floating"` for a translucent floating backdrop. Its inherited button `appearance` remains an independent choice. `SidebarInset` accepts `motion="assistant-dock"` for its padding transition and `appearance="os-canvas"` for a flush, transparent canvas.

Choose `spacing="showcase"` on `SidebarHeader` for its showcase inset, `spacing="compact"`, `"settings-mobile"`, or `"flush"` on `SidebarContent` for those content arrangements, and `spacing="compact"` on `SidebarGroup` for compact group spacing.

On `SidebarMenuButton`, use `purpose="showcase-thread"` for a compact thread row, `purpose="user-account"` for expanded-state account highlighting, or `purpose="settings-search"` for its search-row inset. Keep labels, expanded state, actions, size, variant, and render behavior at the calling site; caller classes follow the chosen slot defaults.
