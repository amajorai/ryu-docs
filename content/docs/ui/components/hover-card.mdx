## Presentation slots

Use `appearance="composer-preview"` on `HoverCardContent` for a compact composer preview. Use `appearance="citation-popup"` on its content slot, and `appearance="citation-pill"` or `appearance="citation-mark"` on `HoverCardTrigger`; its native function-valued `className` receives the trigger state. Keep the action and accessible label at the calling site.

## Context and navigation slots

On `HoverCardTrigger`, use `appearance="context-meter"` for a compact tabular context indicator. Set `interactive` for its pointer and hover treatment, and `contextTone="warning"` or `contextTone="destructive"` for the corresponding status. Supply the threshold decision, action, and accessible label at the calling site. Use `appearance="chat-navigation"` for a conversation navigation row. On `HoverCardContent`, `typography="compact"` selects smaller text and `spacing="rail"` supplies the navigation inset; keep width and placement at the calling site.

## Sidebar previews

On `HoverCardContent`, use `appearance="sidebar-preview"` for a viewport-bounded sidebar preview with its content inset and text size. Use `spacing="conversation-preview"` for a tighter conversation preview inset. Keep content, placement, open state, and interaction at the calling site; caller classes follow these defaults.
