## Tier palettes

Every plan badge animates its gradient background independently of the white sheen.

Teams uses the vertical three-stop Silk Blend palette from [21st.dev](https://21st.dev/community/gradients/bg-9204599f-25c3-4b59-ba38-5053bc3fde79): `#031C26`, `#1B6AA7`, and `#57D2F4`, with the pale white stop removed.

A Major Pass uses an animated dark indigo, violet, rose, and amber gradient inspired by the [21st.dev Animated Gradient Background](https://21st.dev/@hammamikhairi/components/animated-gradient-background); Desktop now uses the former champagne-gold A Major Pass palette. Business uses a wider 500% field to soften its repeated cyan, green, yellow, blue, violet, pink, and amber stops.

Pro keeps its soft holographic pastel palette. Subscription pricing borders reuse each plan badge palette. The standalone Ryu Gateway PAYG card uses the supplied [Ryu logo palette](https://irp.cdn-website.com/bb3e50d5/dms3rep/multi/logo+container2.svg) on its animated hover border. Self-Hosted Free keeps champagne-gold. Hover speeds up the rings. Reduced-motion preferences disable gradient drift and sheen.
