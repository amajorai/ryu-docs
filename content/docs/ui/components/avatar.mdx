## Profile framing

Use `framing="profile-overlap"` on `Avatar` for a profile image overlapping a card. Omit it for the ordinary avatar frame. `EntityAvatar` forwards the same `framing` choice to its `Avatar` root without changing image or fallback selection.

## Message reader slots

Use `appearance="message-reader"` on `Avatar` for the reader frame, `typography="tiny"` on `AvatarFallback` for its compact initials treatment, `spacing="message-readers"` on `AvatarGroup`, and `purpose="message-readers"` on `AvatarGroupCount`. Native avatar and group size states continue to apply; these slot treatments do not replace them. Supply images, initials, reader labels, and overflow counts at the calling site.

## Fallback emphasis

On `AvatarFallback`, `appearance="primary"` fills the fallback with the primary color and primary foreground, and `typography="large"` enlarges the initials for a larger avatar. The existing `appearance="artwork"` and `typography="tiny"` choices remain available; omitting these props keeps the ordinary muted fallback.
