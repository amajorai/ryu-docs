---

Ryu Mobile is the full Ryu app for iOS and Android. Connect it to a remote Ryu node and use that
node's agents, or run a GGUF model downloaded to the phone through `llama.rn`. Mobile uses the same
navigation and names as Desktop, with its own native bridge and without Desktop-only window and
system controls. To connect your first node, see
[Connect and Sign In](/docs/mobile/connect-and-sign-in).

A "Supported on Mobile" label on an app or plugin shows the support level that the app or plugin
declares for Mobile.

## Ryu Bot apps

Ryu Bot is a separate, account-only chat app for phone, tablet, watch, TV, and car. Choose it when
you only want managed chat and do not need nodes, on-device models, or configuration.
