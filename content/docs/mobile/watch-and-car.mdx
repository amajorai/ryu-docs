Ryu Mobile includes a watch app and a car screen for talking to the agents on your active node.
Both go through Ryu on your phone. For the separate account-only chat apps on watch, TV, and car,
see [Ryu Bot apps](/docs/mobile/bot).

## Watch apps

Open Ryu on your watch to see the agents on your active node as a honeycomb of bubbles, like the
Apple Watch app grid:

- **Pan** around the honeycomb with your finger. Bubbles are full size in the middle of the screen
  and shrink toward the edge. The first agents in your list sit in the middle.
- **Zoom** by turning the Digital Crown on Apple Watch, or the crown or rotating bezel on Wear OS.
- **Tap a bubble** to talk to that agent. Tap **Talk** and say your question (on Apple Watch you can
  also scribble or type). The agent's answer appears on the watch and is read aloud. Tap an answer
  to hear it again, or use the speaker button to turn reading aloud off.

A bubble shows the agent's emoji avatar, or its initials on its avatar colour. On Apple Watch, the
agent that is working on the current run has a pulsing ring; on Wear OS, the ring marks an agent
that is answering you.

The watch talks to Ryu on your phone, and your phone talks to your node. The watch holds no account
credentials and does not contact your node directly, so keep your phone nearby. Each question is
sent with the conversation so far. The conversation stays on the watch only while the app runs,
and nothing is saved to your node's chat history.

### Set up the watch app

1. Connect Ryu on your phone to a remote node over HTTPS. The on-device node has no agents for the
   watch to reach.
2. Keep **Apple Watch & Wear OS** turned on in Mobile Settings. Turning it off, or switching to the
   on-device node, removes the node credential from the watch relay on your phone.
3. Install the watch app: on Apple Watch from the Watch app on your iPhone, on Wear OS from Google
   Play on the watch.

If your phone has to start Ryu in the background to answer, the operating system can stop it before
a long answer finishes. The watch then says the agent didn't answer and offers **Try again**.

### Follow agent runs on the watch

The Apple Watch app also mirrors the current agent run from your iPhone:

- its status (working, needs input, ready for review, finished, or failed);
- its title and a short detail line;
- the agent and node running it.

With watchOS 11 and iOS 18 or later, an enabled Live Activity also appears in the watch's Smart
Stack. The run mirror uses the same **Live Activity** setting as the phone; see
[Native OS surfaces](/docs/mobile/native-surfaces). If Ryu on your iPhone closes before a run
finishes, the watch clears that run the next time Ryu opens, and stops showing it as working after
eight hours without an update.

Wear OS watches receive Ryu's finished-run alerts through the phone's notification bridge.

## CarPlay and Android Auto

Ryu shows up on the car screen as a voice-only assistant, like other AI chat apps for cars. The car
screen has two parts:

1. **Talk to** lists the bots (agents) on your active node, with the one you used last at the top.
2. **The voice screen** opens when you tap a bot. Start talking. Ryu shows **Listening…** while you
   speak and **Thinking…** while the bot works, then reads the answer aloud. When the answer
   finishes, Ryu listens again so you can keep talking. Tap **End** (or go back) to stop. If you
   say nothing twice in a row, the conversation ends on its own.

Answers are spoken and never shown as text on the car screen. Ryu leaves code blocks out of the
spoken answer and says that it skipped them.

Your speech goes to your active node for transcription. The node then sends it to the bot you
picked. Car conversations are ephemeral: they are not saved to your chat history, and the recent
turns are kept in memory only until the conversation ends.

### Set up the car screen

1. Connect a remote HTTPS node in the phone app. The on-device node cannot be used in the car.
   Until a node is connected, the car screen asks you to connect one on your phone.
2. Grant Ryu microphone access on the phone. CarPlay cannot ask for permission, so the car screen
   tells you to allow it on your iPhone. On Android Auto, the permission prompt opens on your phone.
3. Keep **CarPlay & Android Auto** turned on in Mobile Settings. Turning it off, or switching to the
   on-device node, removes the node credential from the car surface.

<Callout type="warn">
  On iPhone this needs iOS 26.4 or later, which added voice conversation apps to CarPlay. CarPlay
  only lists Ryu when your build includes Apple's CarPlay voice conversation entitlement. Siri still
  owns the car's voice button, so open Ryu from the CarPlay home screen.
</Callout>
