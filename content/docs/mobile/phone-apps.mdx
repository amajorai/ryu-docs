When you ask for it, Ryu can use some apps on your phone: add an event to your calendar, look up a
contact's number, check where you are, set an alarm, add a reminder, or turn on a light. The phone
does the work, and Ryu asks before it acts. This works in [Ryu Bot](/docs/mobile/bot) and in [Ryu Mobile](/docs/mobile) chats with a
node, on iPhone and on Android phones.

<Callout type="info">
  Phone apps are rolling out gradually. If **Connect apps** says phone apps aren't available for
  your account yet, or not available on this node yet, the feature isn't turned on for you yet.
</Callout>

## Turn it on

1. Open **Connect apps**: in Ryu Bot, from the account sheet; in Ryu Mobile, from **Settings**.
2. Turn on **Let Ryu use apps on this phone**. Ryu creates a key for this phone that never leaves
   it, and links the phone to your account.
3. For each app, tap **Allow** to show the phone's own permission prompt. If you said no before,
   use **Open Settings** to change it in the phone's settings.

On a computer you run Ryu on yourself, a new phone waits for approval on that computer before Ryu
can use its apps. **Connect apps** says so while it waits. To approve it, open Ryu on that
computer, choose **Gateway settings** from the node selector, open **Connect → Connections**, and
find **Paired phones** under **Control this device**. Approve only a phone you are holding that
asked to pair just now: the dialog shows the name the phone chose, its platform, when it asked and
a check code, so you can compare them with the phone. **Deny** removes the request, and the phone
can ask again. Phones can be approved only on the computer that runs the node, never from another
device.

Each person signed in to Ryu on the phone sets this up separately. It is off until you turn it on.

## What Ryu can do

| App | What Ryu can do | iPhone | Android |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calendar | Find events in a range of up to 14 days, read an event's notes, add events, change or delete events | Yes | Yes |
| Contacts | Look up a contact by name and read only the details it asked for, such as a phone number | Yes | Yes |
| Location | Get your current location, approximate by default | Yes | Yes |
| Alarms and timers | Set an alarm within the next 24 hours, or start a timer of up to 24 hours | iOS 26 and later | Through your Clock app |
| Reminders | List, add, change, complete, or delete reminders in your default list | Ryu Bot | No |
| Home | List your Home accessories and change one, such as turning on a light | Ryu Bot | No |

Reminders and Home work on iPhone in Ryu Bot only, not yet in Ryu Mobile.

Some limits keep these actions predictable:

- Ryu does not change or delete repeating events, or events that include other people. New events
  go to your default calendar, and Ryu never adds guests or makes an event repeat.
- Contact lookups are read-only, return at most five people, and need at least two letters of a
  name. Ryu does not add or edit contacts this way.
- Approximate location is rounded on the phone to about a kilometer. Ryu asks every time before it
  uses your precise location.
- Reminders uses only your default list, and Ryu does not change or delete repeating reminders.
- Changing a Home accessory always asks. Locks, garage doors, security systems, doors, and windows
  also need [confirmation on the iPhone](#confirm-on-your-iphone).
- Ryu can't list or cancel alarms. On Android, Ryu hands the alarm or timer to your Clock app,
  which can't confirm that it was set, and some Clock apps show their own screen.
- There are hourly limits on contact lookups, calendar searches, and location checks, and at most
  ten phone actions in one reply. Past a limit, Ryu asks again.

## Only while Ryu is open

Ryu uses your phone's apps only while Ryu is open on the screen and the phone is unlocked. It never
uses them in the background, and keeping a voice chat going in the background doesn't count as
open.

If Ryu needs the phone while it isn't open, the chat shows that it is waiting for your phone, for
up to five minutes. If notifications are on, the phone also gets one notification for that
action: **Ryu needs your phone** in Ryu, which names no app or action, or **Ryu is waiting for this
phone** in Ryu Bot on iPhone, which names only the app, such as Calendar. Neither shows the event,
contact, or place. Tap it to open Ryu. After that, the action shows **Retry** for up to an hour. Open Ryu on the phone
and tap it to try the action again.

Phone apps work only in your own chats, never in shared, team, or channel conversations, and not
from Siri, Shortcuts, routines, or other agents.

## When Ryu asks

Before a phone action runs, Ryu shows what it will do, such as the event's title and time, and
which phone it will use. Choose **Allow once**, **Always allow**, or **Deny**.

- **Always allow** applies to one action on one phone, for example adding calendar events on this
  iPhone. Ryu still asks the first time for each action, including looking something up.
- Some actions are never always-allowed and ask every time: deleting, changing a Home accessory,
  using your precise location,
  an action on a different phone than the one you're chatting on, and any action in a chat that
  already contains content from outside, such as a web page.
- If your node's [approval mode](/docs/security/command-approval) is Manual, every phone action
  asks.
- A request to use an app you haven't allowed yet shows **Allow** and **Not now** on that phone.
  It waits five minutes.

### Confirm on your iPhone

Unlocking a door or opening a garage needs more than a tap. For a Home accessory that controls a
lock, garage door, security system, door, or window, the card on the iPhone shows **Physical
access** and **Confirm**, and the iPhone asks for Face ID, Touch ID, or its passcode before Ryu
acts. Other screens, such as a computer or another phone, show **Confirm on** and the phone's name
instead of the buttons, and can only deny.

Ryu also asks for this confirmation when it doesn't yet know what kind of accessory it is, for
example before it has listed your Home accessories in the last day. Only an iPhone can confirm these
actions: Android phones show **iPhone only** and can only deny. If you set up the phone before this
was available, turn **Let Ryu use apps on this phone** off and on again to pair it with
confirmations.

If your organization manages Ryu for you, an admin can block an app or require Ryu to ask every
time. **Connect apps** then shows **Managed by your organization**. Admins can only restrict:
they can't skip your approval or turn an app on that you turned off.

## Privacy

- What the phone returns, such as an event or a contact's number, is sent to the AI model you
  chose so it can answer.
- Your chat history keeps only a short summary: "Location shared", contact names, or the number of
  events found. Coordinates and contact details are not kept in it.
- Notifications about phone approvals never include the details.
- Ryu does not record what phone actions read or wrote in its diagnostics, only which action ran
  and whether it worked.

## Turn it off

- **One app:** in **Connect apps**, switch the app off. Ryu stops using it on this phone even if the
  phone's permission stays on. You can also remove the permission in the phone's settings.
- **One Always allow:** open the app's row and tap **Revoke**.
- **This phone:** turn off **Let Ryu use apps on this phone**, or use **Forget**. Forgetting a
  phone removes its key, its Always allow choices, and any action waiting for it.
- **Another phone:** **Connect apps** lists your other phones with when they were last seen. Use
  **Forget** to remove one.
- **From the computer:** on a computer you run Ryu on yourself, **Paired phones** lists every
  paired phone. You can rename a phone, revoke an Always allow choice, or **Forget** the phone,
  which removes its key and its Always allow choices.
