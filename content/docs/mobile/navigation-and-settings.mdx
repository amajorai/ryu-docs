This page shows how to move around Ryu Mobile and where its settings live. Mobile uses the same
navigation and names as Desktop, and leaves out Desktop-only window and system controls.

## Open the menu

Tap **Open menu** in a screen header to open the drawer. On tablets and wide windows the drawer stays
pinned as a sidebar; see [Devices and layout](/docs/mobile/devices-and-layout).

The drawer footer has a computer icon, **Get Ryu on desktop and browser**. It opens the download
page for the desktop app and the browser extension in an in-app browser.

## Settings

Choose **Settings** in the drawer to manage:

- your account and the active node;
- theme and language;
- notifications and operating-system controls;
- app updates.

Theme, automatic updates, Live Activity, Finished alerts, Quick actions, and Haptics stay saved
after you fully close and reopen the app.

Settings also opens these screens:

| Screen | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Gateway** | Provider routing, firewall, and user budgets. Drag the form to move between sections; scrolling hides the keyboard so you can reach Save |
| **Channels** | Messaging integrations |
| **Credits** | The active organization's wallet and recent activity. Read-only; see [Plans and Marketplace](/docs/mobile/billing-and-marketplace) |

## Going back

Going back from a detail screen, such as the agent editor, a Space document, or a device, returns
you to the screen that opened it. This also applies after you save an agent or choose **Done** in a
Space document.

A detail screen opened from a link returns to its parent instead:

- the agent editor returns to **Agents**;
- a Space document returns to **Spaces**;
- device pairing returns to **Devices**.

After you sign out and sign in again, Mobile opens at the home screen.

## App updates

**Automatic updates** is on by default. When the app opens, Mobile checks for Expo Application
Services (EAS) updates, downloads an available JavaScript or asset update, and reloads to use it.
Turn the setting off to stop automatic checks; **Check for updates** stays available in Settings.
Updates that change native code, permissions, or bundled modules still need a new App Store or Play
Store build.

## Lists, sheets, and screen readers

- Lists keep their current rows visible while they refresh, and retry when the connection returns.
  Switching nodes loads the new node's rows and never shows the earlier node's rows.
- The node selector, the Spaces **Create space** and **Search** sheets, and the drawer's
  conversation **Search** and **Actions** sheets have labeled Close buttons. On iOS you can also
  swipe down to close the drawer sheets.
- A row's own control, such as a Space's search button or **Delete job**, is a separate control
  that screen readers and keyboards can reach. Tapping a row's status icons opens the row.
- While the drawer is closed, its controls are hidden from screen readers and keyboard focus.
  Collapsible drawer sections and project headers tell screen readers whether they are expanded.
- On a device's screen, the mirrored face changes expression in place, so **Disconnect face** and
  the controls below it stay where you tap them.

## Recover from a screen error

If a screen cannot render, Mobile shows the error and a **Try again** action. Choose **Try again** to
load the screen again.
