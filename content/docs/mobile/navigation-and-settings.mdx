This page shows how to move around Ryu Mobile and where its settings live.

## Open the menu

Tap **Open menu** in a screen header to open the drawer. On tablets and wide windows the drawer stays
pinned as a sidebar; see [Devices and layout](/docs/mobile/devices-and-layout).

The drawer lists Home, New chat, Search, Library and Store, then **More**, which holds Delegate,
Timeline and Phone assistant.

- Its sections (Agents, Groups, Spaces, Meetings, Apps and your chats) show a label only while they
  are empty. Groups and Meetings stay hidden until they have something in them.
- The footer shows your avatar and, when the approvals app is enabled, the Inbox bell.
- Tap your avatar to open the account sheet with **Settings**, **Organizations**, the light or dark
  theme, **Get Ryu on desktop** and **Sign out**.

**Get Ryu on desktop** opens the download page for the desktop app and the browser extension in an
in-app browser.

## Settings

Choose **Settings** in the account sheet to manage:

- your account and the active node;
- theme, Liquid Glass (on iOS 26 and later), and language;
- notifications and operating-system controls;
- app updates.

Each section is one group of rows. Tap anywhere on a row, such as **Gateway** or **Credits**, to
open it.

When you pick a language, Ryu also sets it as Ryu's language in the phone's system settings, so
system dialogs and the screens the phone draws for Ryu, such as CarPlay and Android Auto, follow it.
On Android 13 and later this applies at once. On iOS it applies the next time you open Ryu. Older
Android versions have no per-app language, so those screens keep the phone's language. **Auto
detect** removes the setting so Ryu follows the phone's language again. The Ryu keyboard and the
watch app keep using the phone's language.

It works the other way too. If you change Ryu's language in the phone's settings (**Settings →
Ryu → Language** on iOS, **App languages** on Android 13 and later) to a language Ryu includes,
Ryu switches to it the next time you open or return to the app. Choosing the phone's default
there turns **Auto detect** back on. With **Auto detect** on Android, a change to the phone's
language applies while Ryu is open.

Theme, Liquid Glass, automatic updates, Live Activity, Finished alerts, Quick actions, and Haptics stay saved
after you fully close and reopen the app.

Settings also opens these screens:

| Screen | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Gateway** | Provider routing, firewall, and user budgets. Choose the firewall policy from the **Policy** row. Drag the form to move between sections. Scrolling hides the keyboard so you can reach Save |
| **Channels** | Messaging integrations |
| **Credits** | The active organization's wallet and recent activity. Read-only; see [Plans and Marketplace](/docs/mobile/billing-and-marketplace) |
| **Connect apps** | Let Ryu use Calendar, Contacts, Location, and Alarms on this phone (iPhone and Android only, rolling out gradually); see [Phone Apps](/docs/mobile/phone-apps) |

## Going back

Going back from a detail screen, such as the agent editor, a Space document, or a device, returns
you to the screen that opened it. This also applies after you save an agent or choose **Done** in a
Space document.

A detail screen opened from a link returns to its parent instead:

- the agent editor returns to **Agents**;
- a Space document returns to **Spaces**;
- device pairing returns to **Devices**.

After you sign out and sign in again, Mobile opens at the home screen.

## App updates

**Automatic updates** is on by default. When the app opens, Mobile checks for Expo Application
Services (EAS) updates, downloads an available JavaScript or asset update, and reloads to use it.

Turn the setting off to stop automatic checks; **Check for updates** stays available in Settings.
Updates that change native code, permissions, or bundled modules still need a new App Store or Play
Store build.

## Lists, sheets, and screen readers

- Lists keep their current rows visible while they refresh, and retry when the connection returns.
  Switching nodes loads the new node's rows and never shows the earlier node's rows.
- If a list cannot load, it says so in one sentence with **Retry**. Choose **Show details** to read
  the error the node returned.
- The node selector, the Spaces **Create space** and **Search** sheets, and the drawer's
  conversation **Search** and **Actions** sheets have labeled Close buttons. On iOS you can also
  swipe down to close the drawer sheets.
- A row's own control, such as a Space's search button or **Delete job**, is a separate control
  that screen readers and keyboards can reach. Tapping a row's status icons opens the row.
- While the drawer is closed, its controls are hidden from screen readers and keyboard focus.
  Collapsible drawer sections tell screen readers whether they are expanded, and project rows
  whether they are open.
- On a device's screen, the mirrored face changes expression in place, so **Disconnect face** and
  the controls below it stay where you tap them.

## Recover from a screen error

If a screen cannot render, Mobile shows the error and a **Try again** action. Choose **Try again** to
load the screen again.
