This page covers the everyday workspace screens in Ryu Mobile: conversations, Spaces, and scheduled
jobs. Each screen works on the node you selected; see
[Connect and sign in](/docs/mobile/connect-and-sign-in).

## Conversations

Home groups a project's conversations under the project's name. Tap the project header to collapse
or expand the group.

Use **Search** in the drawer to filter loaded conversation titles. Choose a result to open that chat
and its saved transcript while the live conversation reconnects.

Chat actions on Mobile are rename, pin, archive, and delete. Pinning or archiving a chat updates the
drawer, Home, and Conversations lists at once.

## Spaces

Spaces are knowledge bases on the selected node.

1. Open the Spaces section of the drawer.
2. Tap **+** (**Add to Spaces**) to open the **Create space** sheet.
3. Enter a name. The create form stays disabled until the Space has a name.
4. Submit the form. A failed save keeps the draft so you can retry.

Use the search control beside a Space to search its documents. Search tells you when there are no
matches and keeps your query if a request must be retried.

## Scheduled jobs

The Schedules screen lists recurring jobs on the selected node. Use **Delete job** to remove one.
If deletion fails, the job stays in the list and its row shows a retry message.
