title: "Recaps"
description: "Record a conversation you choose from your phone or watch, and get a private summary with key points, action items, and a daily recap. Recaps are user-started, always visible, and deleted on a schedule."
---

A recap is a short, private summary of a conversation that you decide to record. You start it on
your phone or watch, Ryu shows that it is recording the whole time, and when you stop, your Ryu
node turns the audio into a title, a short summary, key points, action items, decisions, and
topics. Only you can see your recaps.

Recaps run on your own Ryu node through the [Meetings app](/docs/apps/meetings). The phone and
watch apps record and upload; the node transcribes and summarizes.

## Before you start

Recaps are off until they are turned on, and each step needs a deliberate choice:

1. **Your node has Meetings with Recaps.** Recaps need the Meetings app 0.2.0 or newer on your
   node. If your node does not have it, the Recaps screen says so.
2. **Recaps are turned on for the node.** Someone who can manage Meetings turns them on from the
   **Recaps** tab in Meetings. In an organization, members also need the **Can record recaps**
   permission, which is off unless an admin grants it.
3. **You agree to the recap notice.** Before your first recap, Ryu shows who processes your
   recaps (the transcription engine and the summary model your node uses, by name), the retention
   periods, and the minimum age. You are asked again if the summary model moves to a different
   provider or the notice changes.
4. **You allow the microphone.** Ryu asks for microphone access only after you agree to the notice.

You must be at least 16 to use Recaps. Recaps are not available for child or supervised accounts.

## Record a recap

Open **Recaps** in Ryu and tap **Start a recap**. Before recording starts, Ryu asks you to confirm
**Everyone here knows I'm recording**. Recording other people may require their consent where you
are, so tell everyone before you start.

While a recap records:

- **iPhone** shows a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island with the elapsed
  time, **Recording – let people know**, **Pause**, and **Stop**.
- **Android** shows an ongoing notification with **Pause** and **Stop**.
- **Apple Watch** and **Wear OS** show the recording on the watch screen.

A phone or video call pauses the recap. Tap **Resume** when the call ends. Recording also stops at
its time or storage limit, and the watch warns you 10 minutes before its limit and stops when its
battery is low.

### Starting from outside the app

You can also start a recap without opening the Recaps screen:

- **iPhone**: the Shortcuts action or Siri phrase "Start a recap in Ryu". On iOS 18 and later this
  also works from the Action Button and a Control Center or Lock Screen control.
- **Android**: the **Start a recap** Quick Settings tile or the launcher shortcut.
- **Watch**: the Ryu watch app, a watch complication, or a Wear OS tile.

A start from a button, control, tile, or complication opens a 10-second window. Tap **Confirm** to
keep recording. Without Confirm, the recap stops and its audio is deleted without being sent to
your node. Turn on Live Activities for Ryu to start a recap from outside the app on iPhone.

### Reminders

A reminder can nudge you to start a recap at a time of day or when you arrive at a place. A
reminder only sends a notification: recording starts when you tap **Start** (or **Start recap**)
and confirm **Everyone here knows I'm recording**, exactly like a normal start. Ryu never starts
recording on its own.

In Ryu, open the Recaps settings and turn on **Remind me to start a recap** under **Recap
reminders**; in Ryu Bot, open **Reminders**. Then add a time and the days it repeats, or a place.

- **Time reminders** use your phone's time zone.
- **Place reminders** use the place where your phone is when you add it. On iPhone, Ryu Bot can
  remind you when you arrive or leave and needs Precise Location; Ryu needs location access set to
  **Always** to remind you when you arrive. Ryu Bot on Android offers time reminders only.
- Ryu allows up to 8 reminders, and Ryu Bot on iPhone up to 10 (5 of them places).
- Reminders and places stay on your phone. They are never sent to your node.

Allow notifications for Ryu to receive reminders.

## Summaries while recording

You can see a short summary of what was said so far while a recap is still recording. It is off
by default: turn on **Show a summary while recording** (**Live summaries while recording** in Ryu
Bot) in the Recaps settings.

When it is on, the recording screen shows **So far** with a summary and key points once there is
enough speech. Your node makes a new summary at most once per 5 minutes of speech, using the same
summary model and name redaction as the finished recap. These summaries are not saved: they
disappear when the recap ends, and only the final recap is kept.

## Read your recaps

Each finished recap shows its title, summary, key points, action items (with an owner and due date
when one was said), decisions, and topics. You can show the transcript, search your recaps, and
open a recap in a new chat with **Discuss with Ryu**. If some audio never reached your node, the
recap is marked **Partial**.

The **daily recap** gathers the day's recaps. If you turn on the evening notification, it says only
that your daily recap is ready and how many conversations it covers, never what was said.
Notifications for a single recap say only that it is ready.

People's names are left out of recaps unless you turn them on, and they never appear in the daily
recap, search keywords, widgets, Siri, or Spotlight.

## Widgets, Siri, and Spotlight

- **Widgets and watch complications** show how many recaps you have today. Turn on **Show recap
  titles on widgets and the watch** to show titles as well; titles stay hidden while your iPhone is
  locked.
- **Siri and Spotlight** are off by default. Turn on **Show recaps in Siri & Spotlight** to let them
  find your recaps. Titles and topics, never names, are then shared with Siri, Spotlight, and Apple
  Intelligence on that iPhone, and opening a recap from them requires your iPhone to be unlocked.

## How long recaps are kept

| What | Kept for |
| --- | --- |
| Audio on your node | Deleted once each part is transcribed |
| Audio waiting on your phone or watch | Until it uploads, at most 7 days |
| Transcripts | 7 days after the recap finishes |
| Recaps | 30 days, unless you tap **Keep** |
| Daily recaps | 30 days |

The Recaps screen shows any audio still waiting to upload, with **Upload now** and **Delete**.
Waiting audio that cannot be sent within 7 days is deleted and the recap is marked partial.

## Export, delete, and withdraw

- **Delete** a recap to remove it from your node, your phone, and your watch. The daily recap for
  that day is rebuilt without it.
- **Export recaps** as JSON or Markdown.
- **Delete all recaps** removes every recap, transcript, and daily recap from your node and your
  phone.
- **Withdraw consent** turns Recaps off for you and offers to delete the recaps you still have.
- Signing out removes recap data, waiting audio, and widget data from the phone and watch.

## Apple Watch and Wear OS

On Apple Watch, the Ryu watch app records and passes the audio to Ryu on your iPhone, which
uploads it to your node. Keep your iPhone nearby and open Ryu on it now and then: audio waiting on
the watch shows as "waiting for iPhone" and is deleted after 7 days. If the watch runs out of room,
delete waiting audio first.

On Wear OS, Ryu Bot records from its tile or app and uploads to your node.

## Limits

- Recaps record only sessions you start. There is no always-on listening.
- A recap cannot start or resume in the background. After a call or another app takes the
  microphone, tap **Resume**.
- Recaps do not identify speakers.
- Transcripts and summaries are made by your node and the summary model it uses, which may be a
  cloud provider. The recap notice names it.
- Recaps cannot appear in the Siri app's own recap view.
