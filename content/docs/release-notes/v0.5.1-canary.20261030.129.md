## Highlights

### Home product discovery

Users can click anywhere on a home product card to open it, making product discovery more forgiving. Product scenes are centered, readable and consistent, with motion limited to transforms, and the animated scenes show what each product does directly in the web app.

### Eleven-locale localization

New strings, migrated sentences, and server and Core copy are translated into all 11 supported locales. Server-copy templates are matched at runtime, and Better Auth locales are mapped, enabling localized server copy and Better Auth locale handling.

### Stable, clear chat UI

During a live turn, the user message and typing indicator hold still while replies stream, streaming text stays visible as only the transcript updates, and multi-agent replies keep their parts. Long chat lists over 100 rows render only visible rows, and agent tool cards use Hugeicons with text status and solid tones. In narrow panes, composer placeholders stay on one line, bottom drawers remain solid, and read receipts stay reachable.
