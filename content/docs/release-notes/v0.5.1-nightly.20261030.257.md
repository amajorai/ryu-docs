## Highlights

### Chat streaming and overlay stability

During a live turn, the user's message and the typing indicator stay in place while the reply streams. Chat streaming now re-renders only the transcript, so the page, header, and composer are not restyled for each chunk; non-streaming multi-agent replies keep their parts while another reply streams. Sidebar chat lists longer than 100 rows use windowing, and the transcript's floating date label appears only after that day's divider has scrolled out of view. In narrow windows, the pinned summary opens on demand, and modal overlays cover the window navigation cluster.

### Visual consistency and interaction

Home page product scenes are centered, readable, and consistent, with transform-only motion, and the entire home product card is now clickable. Floating UI layers use solid surfaces and a single primitive shadow; the Sheet surface is solid with one shadow and a hairline edge. On narrow panes, the composer placeholder is a single line and the bottom drawer has a solid surface; agent panels use a solid muted surface with visible hovers, index chips, and aligned status labels. The terminal now takes its caret, sizes, dots, and icons from shared system styling instead of one-off values.

### Faster desktop loading

The desktop app now loads Yjs beside the entry and defers sidebar dialogs. It also splits on-demand panels, dialogs, and grammars out of the entry chunk.
