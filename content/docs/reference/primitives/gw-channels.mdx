## What it is

The Gateway channel-layer engine — the external messaging-surface adapters that turn "chat
with an agent" into something that works in your existing apps. It owns the **Telegram
long-poll, Slack Socket Mode, Discord poll, and WhatsApp webhook** adapters plus their
**shared inbound path** — allowlist gate, request-body build, reply extraction — over a
narrow `ChannelHost` seam.

## Swap seam

The pipeline call, `RequestContext` construction, control-plane store fetch, and channel
registration/spawn wiring stay in `apps/gateway` (they hold `SharedState` and drive the
pipeline; *"engine moves, wiring stays"*).

## Where it lives

`crates/gateway/channels`
