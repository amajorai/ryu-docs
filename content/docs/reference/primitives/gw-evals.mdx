## What it is

The Gateway evals stage — how provider quality stays measured. It owns:

- the **live `EvalsRunner`** — per-request sampling + provider-score **EMA**, as a
  swappable `EvalsBackend` trait + registry with an in-memory built-in, and
- the **pure dataset scorers** — `score_case`, `aggregate_scores`, assertions, and judge
  helpers.

The EMA score feeds the router's explore/exploit picker, so routing decisions are
data-driven rather than vibes.

## Swap seam

`EvalsBackend` + registry; the wiring stays in `apps/gateway`.

## Where it lives

`crates/gateway/evals`
