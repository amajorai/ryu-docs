## What it is

The Shadow capture + semantic-memory engine — screen, audio, and input capture with **OCR**
and **semantic search**. This is the primitive behind Shadow's *"what was on my screen when
that came up"* recall: capture continuously, OCR the frames, embed the text, and answer
queries about the past.
