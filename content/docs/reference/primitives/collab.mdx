## What it is

The authoritative CRDT document engine — Core's **durable server-side replica** of every
live collaborative Yjs document. It makes collaborative editing survivable: state is owned
and persisted server-side, not just passed through to clients.

- **`DocRegistry`** — lazily rehydrates per-doc `yrs::Doc` behind a single-writer mutex,
  with apply / state-vector / diff / snapshot / materialize / flush primitives.
- **`CollabStore`** — a rusqlite append-only `doc_updates` log plus compacted
  `doc_snapshots` projection at `~/.ryu/collab.db`.
- **`DocSyncMessage`** — the self-framed wire protocol.
- **`classify_doc_sync`** — the pure write-ACL gate.
- **Y.Doc → source projection** — lets the non-collaborative RAG/search readers consume
  live documents without joining the CRDT loop.

*Authoritative* means durable owner, not arbiter — Yjs converges arbiter-free.

## Swap seam

The single `~/.ryu` kernel coupling inverts through the narrow `CollabHost` trait. Consumed
as a non-optional in-process path dependency (the kernel's `ServerState` holds a
`DocRegistry` unconditionally; the `realtime_ws` transport drives it directly, never over
IPC).

## Where it lives

`crates/core/collab`
