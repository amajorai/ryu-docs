## What it is

The core automation primitives — **recipes** and their store — for the Ghost
desktop-automation MCP server. This is the "what to do on screen" half; the perception and
input halves live in [ghost-eyes](/docs/primitives/ghost-eyes) and
[ghost-hands](/docs/primitives/ghost-hands).
