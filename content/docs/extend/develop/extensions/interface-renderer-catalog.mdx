This page summarizes what each workspace renderer draws. Renderers not covered in
[Interface Renderer Params](/docs/extend/develop/extensions/interface-renderers) take no params
that change how they draw. A contribution can still give any renderer its own label and
description.

## Renderers by kind

### Inboxes, lists, and work queues

| Renderer | What it draws |
| --- | --- |
| `inbox` | Every conversation grouped by what needs the user, most urgent first, with the open conversation beside the list. |
| `focus` | One conversation at a time, most urgent first, ending on a caught-up card. |
| `desk` | A three-pane help-desk inbox with views by agent, team, and project, and a details sidebar. |
| `tickets` | A help-desk ticket workspace: views with counts, a ticket table with status, priority, SLA, bulk selection, filters, and grouping, and a tab per opened ticket. |
| `mail` | A mail client: folders by state, labels for teams and projects, a message list, and a reading pane. |
| `sheet` | Every conversation as a row in a spreadsheet grid the user can sort, resize, search, and filter by status. |
| `records` | Agents, teams, and projects as CRM records, with an object table or status board, sort, filter, and search, and a record page with an activity timeline and attributes. |
| `notebook` | Conversations as pages in a notebook, filed under their project, team, or lead agent. |
| `journal` | Conversations as dated journal entries with Day, Calendar, and Stats views. |
| `terminal` | A keyboard-first command line over agents and conversations. |
| `jobs` | Conversations as job postings and agents as freelancers, with filters, a job list, a job detail, and freelancer profiles. |

### Maps and canvases

| Renderer | What it draws |
| --- | --- |
| `graph` | A live map of who works with whom: the user at the center, agents around them, and teams and projects beside their members. |
| `mindmap` | Everything branching out from the user: projects, teams, and direct conversations, then agents, with conversations as the leaves. |
| `flow` | A minimal node canvas that starts as one card and grows into the user, agents, and their conversations, with a side panel of metrics, activity, and logs. |
| `moments` | An ambient timeline of flows (needs-you, each project and team, each agent) that zooms into one flow and one focused card. |
| `path` | Conversations as stops along a winding path, grouped into units by project, team, and agent. |

### Social and publishing

| Renderer | What it draws |
| --- | --- |
| `messenger` | Agents, and teams as group chats, as contacts in a desktop messaging app. |
| `stream` | One column of posts, one per conversation by its lead agent, with the replies below an open post. |
| `forum` | A discussion board: conversations are topics filed under project, team, and agent categories, with Latest, Top, and Unread lists. |
| `live` | Agents as live-streaming channels, with stream frames built from the running or latest conversation and the conversation as the stream chat. |
| `posts` | Async posts filed in spaces (a team or project), with comments, a details rail, and a Home of what needs the user. |
| `community` | Projects, teams, and agents as communities of posts, with scores from message counts, Hot, New, and Top sorting, and the replies as comments. |
| `wiki` | An article for every agent, team, project, and conversation, with the conversation as a Talk tab. |
| `qa` | Conversations as questions and agent replies as answers, tagged by project or team. |
| `newspaper` | A front page with the most urgent conversation as the lead and section fronts for projects and teams. |
| `gallery` | Every conversation as a card on a masonry board, with a cover built from what was said in its agent's color. |
| `cinema` | Rows of conversations to browse like a streaming catalog, with the live or latest one featured at the top. |
| `noticeboard` | Conversations pinned to a board as notes, index cards, or photos the user can drag, grouped by what they need from the user. |
| `library` | Agents as books bound in their avatar's color, projects and teams as collections, and conversations as chapters, with a reading view around the conversation. |

### Dashboards and ambient displays

| Renderer | What it draws |
| --- | --- |
| `arena` | A wall of live agent cards with live totals and filters. |
| `dashboard` | A grid of widgets the user arranges, remembered on their device: totals, status lists, agents, activity, upcoming runs, pinned, and recent. |
| `mission-control` | A live wall for a second screen: a clock and status banner, totals with 24-hour trends, agent stations, alerts, hourly activity, and scheduled runs. |
| `ticker` | Agents as a market: a watchlist of tickers, a price chart per agent, and a tape of the newest messages. |
| `status` | A status page: open incidents, each agent as a component with a 30-day uptime strip, and upcoming runs as scheduled maintenance. |
| `glance` | Minimal faces and widgets for a display across the room, with a distance control. |
| `hub` | An ambient clock that wakes into large glanceable cards. |
| `departures` | Conversations as flights on a split-flap board split into Departures and Arrivals (which also lists each routine's latest run), with flight details over the conversation. |
| `tracking` | Conversations as parcels listed by stage, with a route, a five-step progress bar, and a history for the selected one. |
| `bank` | The user's usage wallet as a banking app: balance, monthly spend over a 30-day chart, transactions, insights, and statements. Without a wallet (signed out), it shows how to connect one and each agent's activity. |
| `hologram` | A holographic HUD with a reactor core, rings of agent nodes with radial menus, floating status panels, and a command bar. |

### Personal assistants

| Renderer | What it draws |
| --- | --- |
| `persona` | A chat-first personal agent built around one chosen agent, with Chat, Feed, Ideas, Goals, and Activity, and its other conversations as side chats. |
| `panels` | A personal-assistant shell with Home (suggestions and tool panels over a time-of-day sky), one large Chat, and Projects. |

### Illustrated and 3D scenes

The 3D scenes (`aquarium`, `planets`, `kitchen`, and `town`) need WebGL. Each one keeps a plain
fallback when WebGL is unavailable, as its row says.

| Renderer | What it draws |
| --- | --- |
| `aquarium` | A 3D underwater reef in which each agent swims as its own sea creature, with its card and conversations beside the tank. Without WebGL, the agents are listed as buttons. |
| `planets` | A 3D solar system: the user is the star, each agent a planet, and each conversation a moon colored by status, with the agent's panel and conversations beside it. Without WebGL, the planets stay reachable from the system card's list. |
| `kitchen` | A 3D kitchen with agents as chefs at stations and conversations as order tickets and plated dishes. Without WebGL, the chefs are listed. |
| `town` | A 3D town on an island, with project and team districts in which each agent is a building that grows with its conversations. Without WebGL, the buildings are listed. |
| `market` | A shopping street with projects as shops, teams as stalls, agents as shopkeepers, and conversations waiting on the user as queueing customers. |
| `workers` | An office of desks, one per agent, whose monitors show each worker's current conversation, with a sheet for the worker's tasks. |
| `arcade` | Agents as slot machines on a casino floor whose reels are their latest conversations, with projects and teams as chips that narrow the floor. |
| `farm` | Agents' plots fenced into project fields, with each conversation drawn as a crop at the growth stage of its status. |
| `rides` | A city map with agents as drivers driving to each conversation's destination, and a sheet for booking, active, arrived, and past trips. |
| `passport` | A passport booklet with agents as countries and conversations as entry stamps, plus a plain Map of travels. |
| `warehouse` | An isometric warehouse floor with agents as pickers and conversations as order boxes moving through the stages, beside an orders list on a clipboard. |
| `lobby` | A game lobby with a four-agent squad on stage, quests from what waits on the user, a roster, and news. |
| `quiz` | A quiz board of six categories of conversations, valued by row, with the most pressing at the bottom of each column and contestant podiums on the floor. |

## Color themes

Every renderer except the two worlds draws its controls in the user's color theme. Some renderers
also tint their scenes:

- **Card Table** mixes its felt and chips from the theme's colors.
- **Aquarium** leans its water toward the theme, with a sunlit reef in light themes and a deep-sea
  night in dark ones.
- **Planets** uses deep space in dark themes and a pale sky in light ones, and colors its moons
  with the theme's status colors.
- **Illustrated renderers** such as `kitchen`, `arcade`, `farm`, `town`, and `seasonal` paint their
  scenes from a palette of their own, with a daytime look in light themes and a night look in dark
  ones.
