---
title: "Interface Renderer Params"
description: "The params each built-in interface renderer accepts in contributes.interfaces, with values, defaults, and effects."
---

These are the renderer params for [Publish an Interface](/docs/extend/develop/extensions/interfaces). Options you leave out use
their default, an unknown option is ignored, and an invalid value falls back to the default. For
renderers without params, see [What Each Interface Renderer Draws](/docs/extend/develop/extensions/interface-renderer-catalog).

## `channels`

A team-chat layout like a typical work chat app: agents are direct messages, teams are channels,
and conversations without an agent are in `#general`. It draws in the user's color theme from
Settings → Appearance, so it has no palette of its own to set.

| Param | Values | Default | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `groupBy` | `"agent"`, `"project"` | `"agent"` | `"project"` adds a channel per project and files project conversations there instead of in the agent's direct messages. Team conversations are always in the team's channel. |

## `feed`

A timeline where each conversation is a post and its messages are the replies. Pinned
conversations always stay first. Like `channels`, it follows the user's color theme.

| Param | Values | Default | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `order` | `"recent"`, `"active"` | `"recent"` | `"recent"` sorts by latest activity. `"active"` lifts running conversations above the rest. |

## `world-2d`

A top-down pixel-art farming village in the style of classic life-sim games, with a cottage for each agent, a hotbar of
agent portraits, a clock box, and a dialogue box for talking to an agent. The user walks up to an
agent and talks to it. The world keeps its own scenery colors whatever the user's color theme is.

| Param | Values | Default | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `theme` | `"meadow"`, `"night"`, `"office"` | `"meadow"` | `"meadow"` is the village by day, `"night"` the same village after dark, and `"office"` an indoor office floor with a desk for each agent. |
| `controls` | `"auto"`, `"shown"`, `"hidden"` | `"auto"` | The controls hint. `"auto"` shows it on a roomy window until the user first moves, and starts it closed on a narrow one; `"shown"` and `"hidden"` fix it. The **?** slot toggles it and saves the choice as this setting. |

## `world-3d`

The same world as a cel-shaded 3D scene in the style of cozy island life-sim games: a
curved-horizon island village with a round minimap, interaction prompts, and a dialogue band. It
uses the same layout as `world-2d`, so each agent stands in the same place in both.

| Param | Values | Default | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `theme` | `"meadow"`, `"night"`, `"office"` | `"meadow"` | Scenery and lighting, as in `world-2d`. |
| `camera` | `"orbit"`, `"isometric"` | `"orbit"` | `"orbit"` follows the player and lets the user drag to look around and scroll to zoom; `"isometric"` uses a fixed angled view from above. |
| `minimap` | `true`, `false` | `true` | Whether the round minimap shows in the top-left corner. |

## `board`

Conversations as cards in a Kanban board, or as a dense list grouped into sections. Opening a
conversation shows it in a side panel.

| Param | Values | Default | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `groupBy` | `"status"`, `"agent"`, `"project"` | `"status"` | `"status"` groups by status, most urgent first; `"agent"` gives each agent its own group; `"project"` gives each project its own group, plus **No project**. |
| `view` | `"board"`, `"list"` | `"board"` | `"board"` shows Kanban columns; `"list"` shows one row per conversation in collapsible sections. |

Both params are where the interface starts. When the user switches the grouping or the view from the
Board's **Display** menu, the choice is saved as their setting for this interface and kept the next
time they open it.

## `timeline`

Conversations laid out across days and weeks.

| Param | Values | Default | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `scale` | `"day"`, `"week"` | `"day"` | `"day"` is a day-by-day list, newest first; `"week"` is a seven-day grid placing each conversation at the time of its latest activity. |

Switching between Day and Week in the header saves the choice as the user's setting.

## `canvas`

Conversations as sticky notes on an infinite, zoomable board. They start out filed into labelled
frames, and the user can drag them anywhere from there; where they put each one is remembered on
their device, separately for each arrangement.

| Param | Values | Default | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `arrange` | `"status"`, `"agent"` | `"status"` | `"status"` files conversations into one frame per status, most urgent first; `"agent"` gives each agent a frame for the conversations it leads, plus **No agent**. |
| `minimap` | `"auto"`, `"shown"`, `"hidden"` | `"auto"` | The overview map in the corner. `"auto"` shows it when the board is wide enough; the map never shows on a narrow board. |

Switching the arrangement in the Canvas header, or showing or hiding the minimap from its toolbar,
saves the choice as the user's setting.

## `calendar`

Conversations at their latest activity, and the user's scheduled runs at their start time, with a
month picker, the upcoming runs, and status filters in a sidebar.

| Param | Values | Default | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `scale` | `"month"`, `"week"` | `"month"` | `"month"` is a month grid; `"week"` is seven day columns over the hours of the day. |
| `rail` | `true`, `false` | `true` | Whether the sidebar (month picker, upcoming runs, and filters) is open beside the calendar on a wide window. On a narrow window it opens over the calendar when asked. |

Like `board`, switching between Month and Week, or opening and closing the sidebar on a wide window,
saves the choice as the user's setting.

## `card-table`

Agents seated around a card table, with the conversations waiting on the user dealt to them as a
hand of cards, or the same conversations laid out as decks on the felt. The felt and chips are mixed
from the user's color theme.

| Param | Values | Default | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `layout` | `"poker"`, `"spread"` | `"poker"` | `"poker"` seats up to six agents around a felt table, with the user's hand, the pot, and the rail. `"spread"` puts the seats and the hand away: each agent is a deck of its conversations, most urgent on top, and conversations without an agent form an **Unassigned** deck. |
| `felt` | `"classic"`, `"blue"`, `"red"` | `"classic"` | The table's cloth. `"classic"` keeps the green felt mixed from the color theme. |
| `deck` | `"classic"`, `"red"`, `"black"` | `"classic"` | The card backs. `"classic"` keeps the stock backs. |

Switching between **Table** and **Cards** in the header saves the choice as the user's `layout`
setting.

## `seasonal`

A seasonal board of 24 doors, one conversation behind each. While the date is in the board's own
season, the most urgent conversation sits behind today's door; otherwise it sits behind door 1, and
the rest follow at the lowest free numbers. Conversations past the 24th are listed under the
board's "more" button.

| Param | Values | Default | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `season` | `"auto"`, `"advent"`, `"halloween"`, `"lunar-new-year"` | `"auto"` | The board shown first: `"advent"` is a printed calendar of 24 numbered doors, `"halloween"` a pumpkin patch, and `"lunar-new-year"` a board of hanging red envelopes. `"auto"` picks from today's date: Halloween in October, Advent from November 20 through December, Lunar New Year from January 15 through February, and Advent the rest of the year. |
| `weather` | `true`, `false` | `true` | Snow falling over the Advent square, and motes drifting over the pumpkin patch and the lantern street. |

Switching seasons from the header saves the choice as the user's `season` setting.

## `kitchen`, `workers`, `farm`, `town`, `aquarium`, and `path`

| Renderer | Param | Values | Default | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `kitchen` | `serviceClock` | `true`, `false` | `true` | Shows the service clock in the kitchen's corner. |
| `workers` | `screenWords` | `"step"`, `"title"` | `"step"` | What each monitor's plate says: the step its worker is on, or the conversation's title. |
| `farm` | `coins` | `true`, `false` | `true` | Shows the coins counter (one coin per message). |
| `town` | `speed` | `"auto"`, `"normal"`, `"fast"`, `"paused"` | `"auto"` | How fast the town's clock and traffic run. `"auto"` runs at normal speed, or starts paused when the user prefers reduced motion. The user's speed control saves its choice back to this setting. |
| `town` | `dataView` | `"none"`, `"activity"`, `"needs"` | `"none"` | Tints the districts by how busy they are or by what needs the user. The map's data view control saves its choice back. |
| `aquarium` | `wallpaper` | `"cream"`, `"sage"`, `"blue"` | `"cream"` | The wallpaper of the room the tank stands in. |
| `path` | `stats` | `true`, `false` | `true` | Shows the streak, XP, and daily goal in the top bar. |

## `arcade`

| Param | Values | Default | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `lights` | `"theme"`, `"day"`, `"night"` | `"theme"` | The hall by day, or at night with its neon lit. `"theme"` follows the user's color theme (dark is night). |
| `meters` | `true`, `false` | `true` | Whether the credits, running, and jackpot meters show in the bottom-left corner. |

## `lobby`

| Param | Values | Default | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `squadSize` | `1` to `4` | `4` | How many agents stand on the podium as the squad. |
| `openOn` | `"lobby"`, `"quests"`, `"squad"`, `"news"` | `"lobby"` | The menu tab the lobby opens on. Switching tabs saves the last one as this setting. |

## `quiz`

| Param | Values | Default | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `round` | `"auto"`, `"single"`, `"double"` | `"auto"` | The tile values: `"single"` is 100 to 500, `"double"` 200 to 1,000, and `"auto"` doubles once the board is busy. |
| `audience` | `true`, `false` | `true` | Whether the studio audience sits in its theatre rows. |

## `rides`

| Param | Values | Default | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `timeOfDay` | `"theme"`, `"day"`, `"night"` | `"theme"` | Day or night in the city. `"theme"` follows the user's color theme (dark is night). |

## `market`, `warehouse`, `passport`, and `planets`

| Renderer | Param | Values | Default | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `market` | `awnings` | `"mixed"`, `"harvest"`, `"seaside"` | `"mixed"` | The shops' awning colors: the mixed stock, warm harvest tones, or seaside blues. |
| `market` | `bunting` | `true`, `false` | `true` | Whether pennant strings hang over the square (lit with bulbs at night). |
| `warehouse` | `shift` | `"theme"`, `"day"`, `"night"` | `"theme"` | The floor by day or at night. `"theme"` follows the user's color theme (dark is night). |
| `warehouse` | `paused` | `true`, `false` | `false` | Whether the floor stands still. The pause button on the floor saves its choice back. |
| `passport` | `cover` | `"navy"`, `"burgundy"`, `"green"`, `"black"` | `"navy"` | The booklet's cover color, on the desk and in the booklet sheet. |
| `planets` | `orbitSpeed` | `"normal"`, `"slow"`, `"still"` | `"normal"` | How fast the planets travel their orbits and the moons circle them. |
| `planets` | `moonLabels` | `true`, `false` | `true` | Whether the moons of the visited planet show their names. Off, a moon's name shows on hover or focus. |

## 3D game params

`world-2d`, `world-3d`, and every 3D game take these two params as well as their own:

| Param | Values | Default | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `overlayDensity` | `"full"`, `"minimal"` | `"full"` | `"minimal"` keeps alarms, asks, and limits on each station and drops its progress, elapsed-time, and next-step lines. |
| `workBubbles` | `true`, `false` | `true` | Whether each agent shows what it is working on over its head. Asks, alarms, and other notices always show. |

## `knowledge`

A 3D knowledge graph of agents, teams, projects, conversations, and shared topics. Without WebGL,
the graph is drawn flat. Its params are the **Graph settings** panel, and a change there is saved as
the user's setting; **Restore default settings** puts them back.

| Param | Values | Default | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `mode` | `"global"`, `"local"` | `"global"` | `"local"` shows only the nodes around the selected one (**Local graph**). |
| `depth` | `1`, `2` | `1` | How many links out a local graph reaches. |
| `agents`, `groups`, `conversations`, `topics`, `orphans` | `true`, `false` | `true` | Which kinds of node show: agents, projects and teams, conversations, topics, and nodes with no links. |
| `messages` | `true`, `false` | `false` | Topics also link conversations by words in their latest message, not only their titles. |
| `textFade` | `0` to `1` | `0.5` | Higher keeps labels until the graph is zoomed further out. |
| `nodeSize` | `0.5` to `2` | `1` | Node size multiplier. |
| `linkWidth` | `0.5` to `4` | `1` | Link thickness in pixels. |
| `centerForce`, `repelForce`, `linkForce` | `0` to `1` | `0.5`, `0.5`, `0.6` | How strongly nodes pull to the centre, push apart, and follow their links. |
| `linkDistance` | `12` to `120` | `30` | How long links settle. |

A value outside its range falls back to the default.

## `os`

The OS desktop under your own label. It takes no params.
