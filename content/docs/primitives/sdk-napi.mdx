## What it is

The **Node-API (napi-rs)** binding exposing the [SDK core](/docs/primitives/sdk) to
TypeScript/JavaScript as a native addon — the high-performance path for Node and Bun
consumers (alongside the pure-TS [`@ryuhq/sdk`](/docs/develop/sdk)).

## Where it lives

`crates/sdk/napi`
