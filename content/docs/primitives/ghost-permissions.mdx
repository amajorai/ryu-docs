## What it is

The cross-platform check/request primitive for the OS capabilities Ghost needs — **UI
automation** and **screen capture** (e.g. macOS TCC). One seam so every Ghost surface asks
for permissions the same way and degrades gracefully when denied.

## Where it lives

`crates/ghost/permissions`
