## What it is

The Gateway passthrough wire-format redaction engine — what lets subscription CLIs (Claude
Code, Codex) run **through** the Gateway without the raw native wire leaking secrets. It
owns native-format (**Anthropic Messages / OpenAI Responses**) request-body + streaming-SSE
**DLP redaction** over a narrow `PassthroughFirewall` interface, plus the upstream URL/path
helpers and the `WireFormat` marker.

## Swap seam

The reverse-proxy `forward` orchestration, audit emission, and the `PassthroughBackend`
trait + `PassthroughRegistry` stay in `apps/gateway` (they hold `SharedState` and drive the
pipeline).

## Where it lives

`crates/gateway/passthrough`
