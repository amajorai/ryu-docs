## What it is

The **C-ABI surface** over the [SDK core](/docs/primitives/sdk) — the stable C interface
that the **Go (cgo) binding** and any other C-FFI client consume. It is how Ryu works from
languages with no first-party runtime.

## Where it lives

`crates/sdk/ffi`
