## What it is

The **UniFFI** binding surface over the [SDK core](/docs/primitives/sdk) — the
multi-language path for **Python, Swift, and Kotlin** (and optionally C#/Go). It exposes
the blocking surface; streaming is a deferred callback-interface slice.

## Where it lives

`crates/sdk/uniffi`
