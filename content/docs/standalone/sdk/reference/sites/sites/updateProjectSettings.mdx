    contents:
      - content: >-
          Owner only. Replaces every setting at once: send the Site's current
          `settings` with your changes and the `revision` you read as
          `expectedRevision` (the SDK's `projects.updateSettings()` does this
          for you). `database` and `extensions` have their own routes and must
          be sent unchanged (`400 database_managed_separately` /
          `extensions_managed_separately`). `audience: "public"` answers `403
          policy_denied` unless the operator allows public Sites
          (`RYU_SITES_PUBLIC=1`, or the runtime config's `publicEnabled` when
          that variable is unset; `GET /api/sites/status` reports
          `publicEnabled`). A non-null `workspaceId` must be a workspace the
          caller belongs to (`403 forbidden`); the standalone management caller
          belongs to none. Sharing, sign-in, shared data, AI and owner reset
          checks need services the process may not have connected, and answer
          `503 unavailable` without them. Emails are stored lower-cased.
