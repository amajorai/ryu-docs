## 1. Parties and when this applies

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") is between the customer that agrees to the [Terms of Service](/docs/legal/terms) ("Customer") and **A Major Pte. Ltd.** (UEN 202616096G), Singapore ("A Major").

It forms part of the Terms and applies when A Major processes personal data on the Customer's behalf through a Ryu-managed service, for example Ryu Cloud sync, managed nodes, managed agents, Ryu Mail, or an organization workspace.

It does not apply to:

- data on Core or Gateway nodes the Customer runs itself, which A Major does not receive;
- data the Customer sends directly to a model provider, app, channel, or plugin using its own account or key, which that service processes under its own terms; or
- account, billing, security, and website data that A Major processes as a controller under the [Privacy Notice](/docs/legal/privacy).

A separately signed DPA or order form takes precedence over this page.

## 2. Roles

The Customer is the controller (or a processor acting for its own controller) of the personal data in its content. A Major is the processor and processes that data only to provide the Services. Each party complies with the data protection laws that apply to it, including, where relevant, the EU and UK GDPR and Singapore's PDPA.

## 3. Details of processing

| Item | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Subject matter | Providing the Ryu managed services the Customer uses |
| Duration | For as long as the Customer uses those services, plus the deletion period in Section 9 |
| Nature and purpose | Storing, syncing, transmitting, and processing content to run agents, models, tools, mail, and workspaces at the Customer's direction; securing and supporting the service |
| Types of personal data | Whatever the Customer's users put in prompts, conversations, files, Spaces documents, mail, agent and workflow definitions, and tool inputs and outputs; user names, email addresses, and workspace membership |
| Data subjects | The Customer's users, and people whose data appears in the Customer's content |
| Special categories | None intended. The Customer decides whether to submit them and is responsible for doing so lawfully |

## 4. Instructions

A Major processes Customer personal data only on the Customer's documented instructions. These are the Terms, this DPA, and the Customer's configuration and use of the Services, including which models, tools, channels, and integrations it enables.

If A Major is required by law to process data otherwise, it will tell the Customer first unless the law forbids it. A Major will tell the Customer if it believes an instruction breaks data protection law.

A Major does not use Customer content to train Ryu models, and does not sell it.

## 5. Confidentiality

A Major limits access to Customer personal data to personnel who need it to run and support the Services and who are bound by confidentiality. Support staff can see a Customer's account data only after the Customer grants support access. That access is limited to the scopes the Customer chooses, expires within one hour, and is recorded in an audit log the Customer can read.

## 6. Security

A Major maintains these technical and organizational measures:

- encryption in transit (TLS) to Ryu's managed services;
- application-layer encryption (AES-256-GCM) of sensitive fields at rest, including synced conversation and document content, integration tokens, and single-sign-on secrets; synced content is not end-to-end encrypted;
- API keys stored as hashes;
- role-based access within organizations, single sign-on and SCIM for organization customers, and step-up verification for sensitive account actions;
- scoped, time-limited, audited support access, as described in Section 5;
- server request logs that exclude request bodies, query strings, and user content; and
- redaction of tool arguments and credentials in traces and audit records.

The Customer is responsible for securing nodes it operates, the credentials it issues, and the integrations it enables.

## 7. Subprocessors

The Customer authorizes A Major to use the subprocessors listed on the [Subprocessors](/docs/legal/data-processors) page. A Major imposes data protection obligations on each subprocessor that are no less protective than this DPA, and remains responsible for their performance.

A Major will update the Subprocessors page before adding or replacing a subprocessor that processes Customer personal data. Organization customers who want notice by email can ask at **privacy@ryuhq.com**. The Customer may object on reasonable data protection grounds within 30 days of the update. If the parties cannot resolve the objection, the Customer may stop using the affected service.

Model providers, apps, channels, and plugins that the Customer chooses with its own account or key are not A Major subprocessors. Payment providers (Polar, as merchant of record, and Stripe) also act as independent controllers for their own payment, tax, and fraud-prevention obligations.

## 8. Assistance

Taking into account the nature of the processing, A Major will help the Customer respond to data subject requests and meet its security, breach notification, and impact assessment obligations. In particular:

- users can delete their own Ryu account from account settings; this removes their personal data from Ryu's managed services as described in [Deleting your account](/docs/security/authentication-account#deleting-your-account);
- organization administrators can remove members and delete workspace content; and
- for other requests, including exports, A Major will assist on request to **privacy@ryuhq.com**.

If A Major receives a request directly from one of the Customer's users about Customer content, it will refer the person to the Customer where it can identify the Customer.

## 9. Personal data breaches

A Major will notify the Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting Customer personal data. The notice will describe, as far as then known, what happened, the data and people affected, the likely consequences, and the measures taken or proposed. A Major will update the Customer as more information becomes available.

## 10. Deletion and return

Customer personal data is deleted:

- when a user deletes their account, immediately for that user's personal data and for workspaces where they were the only member;
- when an organization deletes content or removes members, as that action describes; and
- when the Customer's use of a managed service ends, on the Customer's request, or after the retention periods in the [Privacy Notice](/docs/legal/privacy#13-retention).

Before deletion, the Customer can export content it holds on its own nodes and, on request, A Major will help return content held in managed services.

Deleted data can remain in database backups until those backups are removed under A Major's backup retention schedule; A Major does not restore deleted personal data from backups except to recover from an incident.

Records that the law requires A Major to keep, such as billing and tax records, are retained without the user's name or contact details.

## 11. Information and audits

A Major will make available the information reasonably needed to show compliance with this DPA, including the [Privacy and Telemetry](/docs/surfaces/desktop/transparency) and [Security](/docs/security) documentation and written answers to reasonable security questionnaires.

Where that is not enough, the Customer may audit A Major's compliance once a year, on at least 30 days' notice, at its own cost, and under confidentiality, or more often if a supervisory authority requires it.

## 12. International transfers

A Major is based in Singapore, and Ryu's control plane and database are hosted in Singapore (AWS Lightsail). Its other subprocessors process data in other countries, including the United States and the European Union.

Where the law requires a transfer mechanism, such as for personal data from the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, the parties rely on the applicable standard contractual clauses or another lawful mechanism, which are incorporated by reference to the extent required.

## 13. Liability and precedence

Each party's liability under this DPA is subject to the limitations in the Terms. If this DPA conflicts with the Terms on data protection, this DPA prevails.
