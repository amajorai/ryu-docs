**Last updated:** October 19, 2026

This page lists the third parties ("subprocessors") that **A Major Pte. Ltd.** uses to provide Ryu's managed services, and that may receive personal data when you use them. It forms part of the [Data Processing Agreement](/docs/legal/dpa), which explains how we choose subprocessors, how we tell you about changes, and how you can object.

Which subprocessors receive your data depends on the features you use. A Core or Gateway node you run yourself sends nothing to A Major or to these subprocessors unless you enable a feature that does, such as sync, a Ryu-managed model, or diagnostics export.

## Infrastructure

| Subprocessor | Purpose | Personal data | Location | Used when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Amazon Web Services, Inc.** (Lightsail) | Hosts Ryu's control plane: the API, website, hosted Gateway, managed agents, and the MongoDB database A Major runs itself | Account, workspace, billing-status, and support-access records; synced conversations, documents, and settings (encrypted at the application layer); data passing through those services | Singapore | Any Ryu account |
| **Cloudflare, Inc.** | DNS, the network proxy in front of every Ryu website and API, inbound email routing, and bot protection (Turnstile) | IP address and request data in transit; forwarded email; browser signals for the bot check | Global network (United States company) | Any visit to a Ryu website or API |

| **Amazon Web Services, Inc.** | Ryu Mail sending and receiving (SES) and stored mail (S3) | Mail messages, attachments, addresses, and delivery events | United States (N. Virginia) | You use Ryu Mail |
| **Hetzner Online GmbH** (Object Storage) | Storage for uploaded files such as avatars | Uploaded files | Germany (Nuremberg) | You upload a file, such as a profile picture |

## Email

Account, security, billing, and (if you opt in) promotional email is sent by a **useSend** instance that A Major runs itself on AWS Lightsail in Singapore, which also manages unsubscribes. It delivers mail through **Amazon Simple Email Service (Amazon Web Services, Inc.)** in the United States (N. Virginia), which receives the recipient address, name, and message content. No other email provider receives your data.

## AI models and media

When you use a Ryu-managed model, your request (prompts, conversation context, files, and tool results) goes to the provider that serves the model or feature you chose. Depending on that choice, this may be:

| Subprocessor | What it serves | Location |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **OpenRouter, Inc.**, and the model provider it routes to | Chat and embedding models from many vendors | United States, and the routed provider's location |
| **OpenAI, L.L.C.** | Chat, image, and audio models | United States |
| **Anthropic, PBC** | Chat models | United States |
| **Google LLC** | Gemini models (Gemini API and Vertex AI) | United States; Vertex AI in Google's default region |
| **Amazon Web Services, Inc.** | Models on Amazon Bedrock | United States (N. Virginia) |
| **Cloudflare, Inc.** | Models on Workers AI | Global network |
| **Replicate, Inc.**, **Features & Labels, Inc. (fal.ai)**, **Modal Labs, Inc.** | Image, video, and other generative media models | United States |
| **Deepgram, Inc.**, **ElevenLabs, Inc.** | Speech-to-text and text-to-speech | United States |
| **Cohere Inc.** | Embedding and reranking models | United States |

Requests Ryu sends to OpenRouter always require providers that do not collect prompts for training and that meet OpenRouter's Zero Data Retention policy. Ryu does not train models on your data, but each provider processes requests under its own terms.

On Ryu's hosted Gateway, OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter, Fireworks, and Vercel AI Gateway also receive an opaque, pseudonymous per-user identifier with each request so they can attribute abuse. Ryu derives it from the user, end user, or gateway key the request is attributed to. It contains no email address or name. A self-hosted Gateway adds none.

## Services you choose

When you connect a service yourself, such as a model provider with your own API key, Slack, Telegram, Discord, WhatsApp, GitHub, an MCP server, a plugin, or a Marketplace app, that service receives what your agents send it under its own terms. It is not an A Major subprocessor, and you are responsible for that relationship.

## Changes and questions

We update this page before a new subprocessor starts processing personal data. The DPA describes how to object. For questions or to ask for email notice of changes, contact **privacy@ryuhq.com**.
