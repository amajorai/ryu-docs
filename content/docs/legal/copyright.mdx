Send copyright concerns about content hosted by Ryu to **contact@amajor.ai**, addressed to **A Major Pte. Ltd.** Postal correspondence can be sent to **71 Ayer Rajah Cres., #02-18, Singapore 139951**.

This contact is a copyright-reporting contact. Publication of this page does not establish registration of a designated DMCA agent with the US Copyright Office or eligibility for a statutory safe harbor.

## Reporting infringement

Include:

1. Your physical or electronic signature, and your authority to act for the copyright owner.
2. Identification of the copyrighted work, or a representative list of works.
3. The exact public URL or other location of the allegedly infringing material, with enough information to locate it.
4. Your name, mailing address, telephone number, and email address.
5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorized by the owner, its agent, or the law.
6. A statement that the information is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are authorized to act for the owner.

We review reports and may remove or disable access to material when appropriate. Ryu may terminate accounts of repeat copyright infringers in appropriate circumstances. Do not submit a report about content merely because you disagree with it. Misrepresentations can have legal consequences.

## Counter-notices

If your material was removed following a copyright notice and you believe this was a mistake or misidentification, contact the same address. Include:

1. Your signature.
2. Identification of the material and its former location.
3. A statement under penalty of perjury that the removal was a mistake or misidentification.
4. Your name, address, and telephone number.
5. Your consent to the jurisdiction required by 17 USC 512(g). For a person outside the United States, that jurisdiction is a US federal district where the service provider may be found.
6. Your agreement to accept service of process from the complainant or its agent.

We may share a counter-notice with the complainant. Where the DMCA process applies, restoration generally occurs after 10 to 14 business days unless the complainant notifies us of a filed action seeking a court order to restrain the activity. Other lawful reasons for restricting content may still apply.

See the [US Copyright Office's Section 512 explanation](https://www.copyright.gov/512/) and [designated-agent directory](https://www.copyright.gov/dmca-directory/) for the statutory process and registration information.
