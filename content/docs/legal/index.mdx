## Product context

- **Ryu Console** can operate a local or self-hosted Core and Gateway and can use local or remote model providers selected by the customer.
- **Ryu Bot** is the fully managed consumer surface and does not expose node or provider configuration.
- **Ryu Platform** provides the SDK, Core, and Gateway contracts used to build and govern products.
- **Ryu Gateway** is the policy and routing boundary for models, tools, permissions, budgets, DLP, audit, and other shared controls.
- **Ryu Infra** includes Ryu Cloud managed nodes and customer-operated self-hosted nodes.

The [Privacy and Telemetry](/docs/surfaces/desktop/transparency) page explains the user-facing defaults for local traces, product analytics, crash reporting, diagnostics export, and support access.
