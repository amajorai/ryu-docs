These pages are the agreements and notices that govern Ryu, published by **A Major Pte. Ltd.** Start with the Terms of Service and the Privacy Notice; organizations that use Ryu-managed services also need the Data Processing Agreement and its subprocessor list.

- **Using Ryu at all:** the [Terms of Service](/docs/legal/terms) and the [Privacy Notice](/docs/legal/privacy).
- **Customer data in a Ryu-managed service:** the [Data Processing Agreement](/docs/legal/dpa), with the [Subprocessors](/docs/legal/data-processors) it relies on.
- **Reporting infringing content:** [Copyright Notices](/docs/legal/copyright).

For the product defaults behind these documents (local traces, analytics, crash reporting, diagnostics export, and support access), see [Privacy and Telemetry](/docs/surfaces/desktop/transparency).
