## 2. What Ryu is

Ryu is an open-core control layer around AI agents. It helps people and organizations compose, route, govern, observe, and operate agents, models, tools, workflows, plugins, apps, and channels.

- **Core** coordinates local execution and agent state.
- **Gateway** provides policy, routing, access, network, budget, DLP, audit, and other controls around model and tool calls.
- **Ryu Console** can operate Core and Gateway locally or against a self-hosted node.
- **Ryu Bot** provides the fully managed consumer surface with simple access controls.
- **Ryu Platform** provides the SDK, Core, and Gateway contracts for building products.
- **Ryu Infra** includes Ryu Cloud managed nodes and customer-operated self-hosted nodes.

The deployment mode matters. A local or self-hosted installation is operated by the customer or its administrator. A managed service is operated by A Major or its infrastructure providers. The applicable order form, plan, product documentation, and [Data Processors](/docs/legal/data-processors) page identify the service path that applies.
