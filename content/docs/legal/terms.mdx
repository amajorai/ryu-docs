## 2. What Ryu is

Ryu is an open-core control layer for AI agents. It helps people and organizations run, route, govern, and observe agents, models, tools, workflows, plugins, apps, and messaging channels.

- **Core** runs agents and stores local state on the node you choose, such as your own computer or a server.
- **Gateway** applies routing, permissions, budgets, data-loss-prevention (DLP), network, and audit controls to model and tool calls routed through it.
- **Ryu Desktop, web, mobile, and extension apps** are the interfaces for those nodes.
- **Ryu Cloud** provides accounts, organizations, billing, synchronization, managed model access, managed cloud nodes, and other hosted features.
- **The Marketplace** distributes agents, apps, plugins, templates, and other items published by A Major and by third parties.

How a feature works depends on the deployment. A local or self-hosted installation is operated by you or your administrator. A managed service is operated by A Major and its service providers. The [Privacy Notice](/docs/legal/privacy) explains which data leaves your node in each case.
