## What it does

Decks turns a USB control deck plugged into the computer that runs Ryu into a
set of Ryu keys. Open **Decks**, pick a key, and give it a label, a colour and
an action:

- **Raise the event only**: the press raises the `@ryu/decks#key.pressed` app
  event. Add an event trigger to a workflow (or a plugin hook) and filter on the
  deck's id and the key number to make that key do anything a workflow can do.
- **Ask an agent**: the press also sends a saved prompt to an agent as a new
  chat. Like every app-initiated message, it waits in the Approvals inbox unless
  you turned that off. A second press within a second and a half is ignored, so
  a double press does not send twice.

Every press raises the event, whatever the key's action is.

On a deck with dials (Stream Deck +, the Corsair Galleon 100 SD, Loupedeck, the Ajazz AKP03 and AKP05, X-keys and Contour
jog-shuttles, the Logitech MX Creative Dialpad, the Xencelabs Quick Keys'
wheel, the Cooler Master MasterHub's knobs, faders, rollers and 1xDial, a 3Dconnexion 3D mouse's puck, an Xbox, PlayStation or GameSir controller's sticks, a DJI remote's sticks and gimbal wheel, the Yamaha CC121MK2's encoders, jog wheel and fader, the TourBox's knob, scroll wheel and dial, the Blackmagic panels' jog
wheel and T-bar), pushing a dial raises the same event with `control` set to
`dial`, and turning a dial raises `@ryu/decks#dial.turned` with the number of
detents (a shuttle ring reports how far it moved; the Dialpad's roller and dial,
the Blackmagic jog wheel and T-bar, the MasterHub's faders and rollers, a
3Dconnexion puck, and a DJI remote's sticks and wheel only turn).

A tap on a touch strip or the Touch Bar raises the press event with `control` set to `strip`.

Some decks light their dials: the Stream Deck Studio's two dial rings and knobs,
the Corsair Galleon 100 SD's two dial rings,
the Ajazz AKP05 / Mirabox N4's four knobs, the Xencelabs Quick Keys' wheel ring,
the Mirabox Stream Dock XL's side lights (one colour for both), and the
Cooler Master MasterHub knob and fader lights. Pick a
colour for each under **Dial lights** in Decks, or Off. The deck's brightness
dims them too (on the Quick Keys it sets only the display, in four steps from
off to full). The Mirabox M18's 24 lights and the MasterHub base's light take one colour, under **Deck lights**.
On the DaVinci Resolve Replay Editor and the ATEM Micro Panel, **Deck lights**
turns the T-bar's light strip on (any colour except black or near-black) or off.

On the iCON P1-Nano and the Behringer X-Touch One, a light you have not set
is white, so the first choice reads **Default (white)** and a black swatch turns
the light off.

Use **Test** in the key editor to run a key without the deck. Recent presses,
and whether each one was sent, is waiting for approval or failed, show under
the deck.

## Close other deck software

Only one program can drive a deck at a time. Quit the Stream Deck app, Ulanzi
Studio, Work Louder Input, Loupedeck, Razer Synapse, Logi Options+, Mountain
Base Camp, the Xencelabs driver, TourBox Console, Cooler Master's MasterHub
software, 3DxWare, Yamaha's ControlCenter, DJI Assistant, serialosc or the Mirabox / Ajazz app while
Ryu uses the deck. Close the Vial app while Ryu uses a Vial keyboard, and
turn off Windows Dynamic Lighting (or OpenRGB on Linux) for a device whose
lamps Ryu lights.

## Linux setup

On Linux, add the Decks udev rule once before you use a deck. The steps are in
[Decks Linux setup](/docs/apps/decks-linux).

## What stays on this computer

Layouts and brightness are stored on the Ryu node the deck is plugged into. The
list of recent presses is kept in memory only. Nothing leaves the node unless a
key asks an agent: that prompt goes to the agent like any other chat message.

## UI it contributes

### Apps shelf

When enabled, this app appears as one icon tile in the desktop sidebar's Apps shelf. Use sidebar sections for the app's record lists; feature navigation stays inside the Companion.

### Hook events

- **Deck key pressed** - A key, a dial or a touch-strip segment was pressed on a connected control deck, or a key was run from the Decks test button. Fires once per press, never on release, whatever the key's own action is, so a workflow can bind to any key: filter on `device_id` and `key`, or on the `label` you gave it. `control` is `key`, `dial` or `strip` (a touch on a Stream Deck +, + XL, AKP05 or Loupedeck side-strip segment, or on the MacBook Touch Bar; a touch on the Loupedeck CT wheel's screen is a `key`); `source` is `device` or `test`; `action` is the key's own action (`none` or `ask_agent`).
- **Deck dial turned** - A deck dial turned (Stream Deck +, Loupedeck, AKP03, AKP05 and other decks with knobs, an X-keys or Contour jog wheel or shuttle ring, the Logitech MX Creative Dialpad's roller and dial, the Xencelabs Quick Keys wheel, a TourBox's knob, scroll wheel or dial, or a Blackmagic panel's jog wheel or T-bar: the shuttle reports how far its ring moved). `delta` is the number of detents since the last report: positive is clockwise, negative is anticlockwise.
- **Deck touch strip swiped** - A finger swiped along a Stream Deck + or + XL touch strip. `from_key` and `to_key` are the strip keys under the start and end of the swipe (equal for a short swipe); `direction` is `left` or `right`.
