## What it does

A local invoice register for tracking what was issued, what is overdue, and what has been paid. Invoices records status and cashflow without replacing a payment processor or accounting system.

## Permissions

**Grants:** `storage:kv`, `shell:integrate`, `ui:toast`

| Level | Description | Implies |
|---|---|---|
| **Can view** | See invoice status, due dates, and local cashflow totals. |  |
| **Can edit** | Create invoices and update their local status. | `invoices.view` |

## UI it contributes

### Apps shelf

When enabled, this app appears as one icon tile in the desktop sidebar's Apps shelf. Use sidebar sections for the app's record lists; feature navigation stays inside the Companion.
