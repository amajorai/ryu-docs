## What it does

A local invoice register for tracking what was issued, what is overdue, and what has been paid. Invoices records status and cashflow without replacing a payment processor or accounting system.

The register opens only after its saved records load successfully. If storage is unavailable or a saved record cannot be read, use **Retry** to load it again before editing.

Failed saves keep the last confirmed records and show an error. Creating an invoice or marking it paid is confirmed only after storage accepts the change. Standalone previews use separate browser storage and report when it cannot save.

## Invoice totals

Due, overdue, and collected amounts are grouped by invoice currency. Each group shows its currency code, so amounts with similar symbols remain identifiable. Draft invoices are excluded from those amounts. Invoices does not convert currencies or combine them into a single total.
