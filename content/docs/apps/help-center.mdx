## What it does

Help Center brings a Ryu support operation into one companion. Teams can triage
customer questions in an inbox, keep a complete ticket history, maintain a
searchable knowledge base, preview Ryu's customer-facing answers, and review
operational metrics.

The companion has six local views:

- **Overview** for the support workspace summary.
- **Inbox** for active operational work.
- **Tickets** for the complete ticket history, including resolved and reopened
  records.
- **Knowledge** for articles, drafts, published guidance, and unanswered
  questions.
- **Agent** for tone, instructions, escalation rules, selected knowledge, and a
  customer-question preview.
- **Insights** for queue, handoff, resolution, topic, and article-usage
  metrics.

## Tickets and knowledge in Ryu Spaces

Help Center keeps tickets and knowledge articles in a dedicated Ryu Space. Each
record is a Space document. Ryu Core and Spaces own the Space identity,
tenancy, access control, indexing, retrieval, and persistence. Help Center owns
the ticket and article records, filtering, and local views.

The companion remembers the Help Center Space id and local preferences through
namespaced storage. It does not put ticket or article bodies in that storage.
There is no separate Help Center sidecar or remote support service in this
release.

## AI assistance and human approval

Ryu's model bridge can help Help Center with ticket classification,
conversation summaries, suggested replies, related-article suggestions,
knowledge-article drafts, and the Agent preview. Help Center sends the selected
ticket, agent settings, and bounded relevant Space search matches for a request.

The result remains a suggestion. A human reviews and edits a reply before
adding it to the conversation, and a human explicitly reviews an article before
publishing it. A denied or failed model request stays an error. Help Center does
not replace a failed live request with demo output.

## Open and use Help Center

1. Enable Help Center from Ryu's app store when it is not already enabled, then
   open the Help Center companion.
2. On first open, Help Center creates or reuses its dedicated Space. If the
   Space is empty, choose **Load sample workspace** or **Create first ticket**.
3. Use **Inbox** for active work and **Tickets** for the full history. Select a
   ticket to review its conversation, requester context, resolution state, and
   knowledge suggestions.
4. Use **Knowledge** to search or edit articles. Use **Agent** to test a
   customer question against selected knowledge. Use **Insights** to review
   deterministic metrics from the records in the Space.

Loading the sample workspace is an explicit action. When the host bridge is not
available, Help Center keeps that sample state in memory and shows a visible
**Demo mode** marker.

## Local reply history is not external delivery

Help Center v1 has no inbound channel transport and no outbound channel
delivery. **Add reply** appends an agent message to the local conversation and
labels it as local history. It does not tell a customer that a message was
sent. Resolution, reopening, assignment, snoozing, and article publication are
also explicit actions inside Ryu.

## Permissions

**Grants:** `spaces:docs`, `storage:kv`, `hook:side-model`

**Dependency:** `@ryu/spaces`

| Capability | Use |
|---|---|
| `spaces:docs` | Store, read, update, delete, list, and semantically search Help Center documents. |
| `storage:kv` | Remember the Help Center Space id and local preferences. |
| `hook:side-model` | Request bounded model completions through Ryu's model bridge. |

## Current boundary

Help Center does not ingest email, WhatsApp, SMS, voice, Slack, or social
messages. It does not provide public webhook ingestion, an externally hosted
customer widget or help-center domain, autonomous outbound replies,
irreversible customer or account actions, billing, seats, SLA enforcement, or
cloud administration. These remain separate future seams because they require
external credentials, public authentication, delivery guarantees, and a
dedicated operational backend.

## Engine requirement

Requires Ryu >=0.1.0.
