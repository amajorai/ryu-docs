## What it does

Meetings records a meeting, shows a live transcript, and writes AI notes when it ends. Each meeting is saved into the Meetings Space, where it stays editable and searchable by RAG (retrieval-augmented generation).

Speaker labeling is optional and off by default. When you turn it on, an installed local runtime uses pyannote or NVIDIA Nemotron after the meeting ends to find who spoke when. Audio stays on the Ryu node for this step.

When enabled, this app appears as one icon tile in the desktop sidebar's Apps shelf. Use sidebar sections for the app's record lists; feature navigation stays inside the Companion.

If a Desktop Meetings route opens while the app is turned off on the current node, choose **Enable** to continue; existing meeting data stays untouched. ### Hook events

- **Meeting detected** - A meeting app started using the microphone. Debounced: the same app does not re-fire within two minutes, so one call raises it once. No meeting record exists yet - it fires before the user (or auto-record) starts one.
- **Meeting started** - A meeting record was created and audio ingest began. Fires once per meeting on both paths: a live recording (Shadow capture is starting) and a file import, where it is followed within the same request by the transcription pass and meeting.ended - `app` is "import" in that case.
- **Meeting ended** - A meeting was finalized: capture stopped, notes were generated from the transcript, and the auto-title was applied - so the title here is the one the user sees. Fires again if the same meeting is finalized a second time (finalizing is not one-shot), and not at all when note generation failed. The transcript is NOT in the payload; read it from GET /api/meetings/&#123;id&#125;/transcript.
- **Meeting notes filed** - The generated notes landed in the Meetings Space as an editable, RAG-searchable document, immediately after meeting.ended. Filing is best-effort, so this does NOT fire when the Space save failed - the notes still exist on the meeting record, they are just not mirrored. Subscribe here (not to meeting.ended) when you need a document to link to.

## Data at rest

Meetings encrypts transcripts, speaker labels, meeting titles, participants, generated notes, and the recorded meeting audio on disk with a key only this app receives from Ryu. Ids and timestamps stay unencrypted. Recordings and transcripts saved by an earlier version are encrypted the first time the updated app starts; older audio files are encrypted in the background. If the app is updated before Ryu itself, it keeps working unencrypted until Ryu delivers its key, and its health status says it is waiting for a Ryu update.
