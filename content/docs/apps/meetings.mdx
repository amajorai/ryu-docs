## What it does

Meetings records audio, produces a live transcript, and generates AI notes. Notes are saved to the Meetings Space so they remain editable and searchable with RAG.

### Hook events

- **Meeting started** - A meeting record was created and audio ingest began. Fires once per meeting on both paths: a live recording (Shadow capture is starting) and a file import, where it is followed within the same request by the transcription pass and meeting.ended - `app` is "import" in that case.
- **Meeting ended** - A meeting was finalized: capture stopped, notes were generated, and the auto-title was applied. Fires again if the same meeting is finalized a second time, and does not fire when note generation fails. The transcript is not in the payload; read it from `GET /api/meetings/&#123;id&#125;/transcript`.
- **Meeting notes filed** - Generated notes were saved to the Meetings Space as an editable, RAG-searchable document immediately after `meeting.ended`. Filing is best-effort, so this event does not fire when the Space save fails. The notes remain on the meeting record but are not mirrored. Subscribe to this event when you need a document link.
