## What it does

Meeting notes: record → live transcript → AI notes, auto-saved into the Meetings Space so they stay editable and RAG-searchable.

### Hook events

- **Meeting started** - A meeting record was created and audio ingest began. Fires once per meeting on both paths: a live recording (Shadow capture is starting) and a file import, where it is followed within the same request by the transcription pass and meeting.ended - `app` is "import" in that case.
- **Meeting ended** - A meeting was finalized: capture stopped, notes were generated from the transcript, and the auto-title was applied - so the title here is the one the user sees. Fires again if the same meeting is finalized a second time (finalizing is not one-shot), and not at all when note generation failed. The transcript is NOT in the payload; read it from GET /api/meetings/&#123;id&#125;/transcript.
- **Meeting notes filed** - The generated notes landed in the Meetings Space as an editable, RAG-searchable document, immediately after meeting.ended. Filing is best-effort, so this does NOT fire when the Space save failed - the notes still exist on the meeting record, they are just not mirrored. Subscribe here (not to meeting.ended) when you need a document to link to.
