## What it does

Connect WhatsApp Personal or WhatsApp Business (Cloud API) to a Ryu agent or team from a native workspace tab.

## Ryu-managed shared front door

Ryu Cloud can provide one official WhatsApp Business number for all managed
organizations. This is one shared Ryu front door, not a number purchased for
each organization or member. You do not connect your own Meta account or paste
WhatsApp credentials into Ryu.

An organization admin grants a member's WhatsApp phone identity and chooses the
agent or team it may reach. Once granted, the member simply messages the shared
Ryu number. Ryu routes the message to that organization's managed node; an
unknown phone is shown onboarding information and is never guessed into a
tenant. Managed model usage is charged to the organization's existing Ryu
credit wallet.

The shared sender still requires Ryu to maintain a verified WhatsApp Business
Account and production phone number with Meta. Meta supplies the Cloud API, not
the production telephone number. Self-hosted deployments can instead use the
manual Cloud API or Personal setup below.
