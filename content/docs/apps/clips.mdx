## What it does

Clips opens into a local-first video repurposing workspace. Import a local video for preview, review transcript-led clip suggestions, shape a vertical canvas with captions, and prepare an export. The Companion includes an inlined demo video so its editor workflow is reviewable without a running capture sidecar.

The **Inspiration** lane is a fast review queue for short-form formats. Review one bundled example at a time, press **←** or swipe left to pass, and press **→** or drag right to copy its hook and format into the current project. Copied ideas appear in **Your picks** and can be opened in the editor. The bundled examples are local demo data; Clips does not fetch or republish third-party videos from this lane.

The out-of-process `ryu-clips` sidecar remains the owner of screen/timeline capture and URL or local-file ingest. It depends on the Shadow capture app for recordings; Core proxies the `/api/clips/*` surface to it. When the Companion is mounted in Ryu, its own-sidecar requests use the generic `app:http` bridge, so the frame does not receive a node token or reach the network directly.

| Level | Description | Implies |
|---|---|---|
| **Can view** | Browse and play back recorded clips, including whatever was on screen when they were captured. |  |
| **Can capture** | Record new clips and ingest external ones, which starts screen capture on this machine. | `clips.view` |

### Hook events

- **Clip recording started** - Fires when POST /api/clips/start has actually begun a capture on Shadow and a clip id exists. A start Shadow refused, or one that never reached it (the fail-soft `&#123; available: false &#125;` body), does not fire. The clip has no video and no duration yet - those arrive with `clip.ready`; this is the moment to react to recording having begun (announce it, arm a timer, dim a surface).
- **Clip ready** - Fires once a finished clip bundle exists - from BOTH paths that produce one: stopping a recording (POST /api/clips/&#123;id&#125;/stop) and completing an ingest of a watched URL or a local video file (POST /api/clips/ingest). `source` is how a consumer tells them apart; the bundle itself does not record which. Fires once per clip, alongside (not after) the auto-file into the Clips Space, so it still fires if that filing fails. `framesEndpoint` is the Core-served path, already rewritten off Shadow's internal one, so a consumer can fetch frames from it directly; the mp4 is at /api/clips/&#123;id&#125;/file.
