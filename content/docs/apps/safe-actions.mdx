## What it does

Review and govern typed tool plans before they can touch the world. Safe Actions shows the exact sequence, parallel branches, conditions, effects, and resources a verified agent proposed; explains every deterministic policy finding and counterexample; lets an authorized reviewer approve or deny plans that require judgment; and preserves hash-bound execution receipts without exposing raw secrets. It is the control surface for Core's generic fail-closed verified-plan gate: approval resumes the persisted plan, while the Companion itself never invokes a tool or executes an action.

## Who can review

Every call the Companion makes needs two things at once: the app's approved `safe-actions:manage` grant, and your own role in the organization that owns the node. On an organization node, Viewers can read plans, findings and receipts; changing a policy or contract, or approving or denying a plan, needs an Admin. Approvals, denials and contract attestations are recorded under your signed-in identity, never a name the app supplies, so the grant alone never makes an app a reviewer. On a node that is not bound to an organization (for example your own machine), its operator can do all of this.
