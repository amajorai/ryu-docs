## What it does

Timeline replays captured activity with a CapCut-style scrubber over Shadow's screen, input, window, and audio lanes. It includes keyframe previews, a Dayflow work journal, and jump-to-moment focus.

### Companion surfaces

- **Timeline** (html)

### Views

The Timeline companion opens in **Replay**, the existing scrubber view with
capture lanes, a keyframe preview, playback controls, and a work-journal strip.
Use the **Timeline** toggle to switch to a chronological History view grouped by
day. When a work journal is available, it shows readable focus blocks; otherwise
it lists the captured events directly. Select a block or event to open that
moment in Replay.

History cards can also show the installed operating-system icons for the apps
involved in that activity, such as WhatsApp and Chrome. On desktop, icon lookup
stays local to the device and uses the operating system's app identity; if an
app is no longer installed or its icon cannot be resolved, the card keeps the
app name and an initial instead. Web and other non-desktop surfaces retain the
name-based fallback.
