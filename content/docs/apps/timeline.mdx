## What it does

The activity replay timeline: a CapCut-style scrubber over Shadow's captured screen/input/window/audio lanes with a keyframe preview, a Dayflow work journal, and jump-to-moment focus.

## What it exposes

### Companion surfaces

- **Timeline** (html)

### Views

The Timeline companion opens in **Replay**, the existing scrubber view with
capture lanes, a keyframe preview, playback controls, and a work-journal strip.
Use the **Timeline** toggle to switch to a chronological History view grouped by
day. When a work journal is available, it shows readable focus blocks; otherwise
it lists the captured events directly. Select a block or event to open that
moment in Replay.

## Permissions

**Grants:** `timeline:read`, `shell:integrate`

| Level | Description | Implies |
|---|---|---|
| **Can view** | Replay Shadow's captured screen, window, and audio lanes and the work journal built from them, which exposes everything recorded on this machine. |  |
