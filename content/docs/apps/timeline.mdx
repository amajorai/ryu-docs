## What it does

The activity timeline with Replay lanes and a chronological History view over Shadow's captured screen/input/window/audio lanes, plus a keyframe preview, Dayflow work journal, and jump-to-moment focus.

## Permissions

**Grants:** `timeline:read`, `shell:integrate`

| Level | Description | Implies |
|---|---|---|
| **Can view** | Replay Shadow's captured screen, window, and audio lanes and the work journal built from them, which exposes everything recorded on this machine. |  |
