## What it does

Approval inbox: a human-in-the-loop queue where agent-proposed actions, edits, and fixes wait for your accept or reject before they take effect.

## Permissions

**Grants:** `approvals:crud`, `notifications:send-to-user`, `quests:crud`, `ui:toast`, `shell:integrate`

| Level | Description | Implies |
|---|---|---|
| **Can view** | Read the approval inbox and see what an agent is waiting to do, without accepting or rejecting anything. |  |
| **Can decide** | Accept or reject queued items, which is what lets an agent's proposed action, edit, or fix actually take effect. | `approvals.view` |

## Refreshing decisions

Approval and task-suggestion lists pause automatic refreshes in hidden browser
companion tabs. Returning to the tab refreshes them. After an approval or task
decision, the action waits for a fresh list before completing its busy state;
a slower earlier poll cannot replace the new result.
