## What it does

The **Inbox**: a unified decision + notification surface. A human-in-the-loop queue where agent-proposed actions, edits, and fixes wait for your accept or reject before they take effect — plus your per-user app-inbox notification feed, so a monitor alert, an incoming reply, a finished fine-tune, or any app notification lands beside the approvals it pairs with.

Each notification row shows the **sending app's icon** (resolved from the app's manifest), and the Notifications section offers a Live / Archived view: open a row to mark it read, archive it to tidy the inbox, or restore it later from the archive.

## Permissions

**Grants:** `approvals:crud`, `quests:crud`, `shell:integrate`

| Level | Description | Implies |
|---|---|---|
| **Can view** | Read the approval inbox and see what an agent is waiting to do, without accepting or rejecting anything. |  |
| **Can decide** | Accept or reject queued items, which is what lets an agent's proposed action, edit, or fix actually take effect. | `approvals.view` |
