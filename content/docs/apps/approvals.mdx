## What it does

The **Inbox** combines approvals and notifications. Agent-proposed actions, edits, and fixes wait for you to accept or reject them before they take effect. The per-user app-inbox feed puts monitor alerts, incoming replies, finished fine-tunes, and other app notifications beside the approvals they relate to.

Each notification row shows the **sending app's icon**, resolved from the app's manifest. The Notifications section has Live and Archived views. Open a row to mark it read, archive it, or restore it later.

## Permissions

**Grants:** `approvals:crud`, `quests:crud`, `shell:integrate`

| Level | Description | Implies |
|---|---|---|
| **Can view** | Read the approval inbox and see what an agent is waiting to do, without accepting or rejecting anything. |  |
| **Can decide** | Accept or reject queued items, which is what lets an agent's proposed action, edit, or fix actually take effect. | `approvals.view` |
