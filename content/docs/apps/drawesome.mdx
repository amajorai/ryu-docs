## What it does

A lightweight sketching studio for Ryu with seven natural-feeling pens, area erasing, and SVG or PNG export. Sketches persist in app storage and stay local to the node.

When Drawesome is enabled, the chat composer’s **Add** menu also includes **Sketch**. It opens
Drawesome in a focused dialog, keeps the app’s drawing toolbar, and lets you attach the resulting
PNG to the next chat message. Existing uploaded or generated images can also open the same dialog
from their lightbox, with the image held beneath the drawing surface for circles, arrows, marks,
and erasing. Canceling the dialog leaves the composer unchanged.
