## What it does

Tuition is a personal tutor. Point it at a syllabus, chapter, or set of notes and it builds a readable
mastery model. Each skill has a Bayesian Knowledge Tracing posterior, prerequisite edges, and a
spaced-repetition schedule calculated in whole days, so a review due tomorrow stays due tomorrow even
if you studied at 23:58.

The next-study recommendation is the weakest skill whose prerequisites you have mastered. Tuition can project how many more correct answers at a given difficulty would raise a posterior, such as `0.62` to `0.9`. Multiple-choice, cloze, numeric-with-tolerance, and exact answers use direct comparison. A model marks free-response answers and always shows the rubric used.

With an exam date, Tuition estimates whether the syllabus will be covered in time from the observed rate over the last ten sessions. It returns `unknown` with too little data instead of extrapolating from two points. Models run at two edges only: proposing skills from a document and writing practice items and rubric marks. A person reviews both before they count.

### MCP server

- **`tuition`** - Expose the mastery model to agents and workflows. `due` lists skills whose reviews are owed and why; `quiz` returns stored practice items; `grade` marks an objective or rubric-graded answer; `log` records an attempt and updates the posterior and schedule; and `mastery` reports each skill's current posterior and the projected work needed to reach the target. `due`, `quiz`, and `mastery` are pure reads of computed state.

## UI it contributes

- **Study mode** - When an answer teaches you something, queue it as a review candidate for the active subject. Candidates are held for you to accept or reject - nothing enters the deck on its own. (type: toggle)

### Settings tabs

### Turn hooks

- **tuition.study** (on: `post_assistant_turn`)

### Hook events

- **Reviews are due** - The scheduler found at least one skill whose `due_at` has passed since the last emit. Fires once per tick per subject with every newly due skill. It does not fire again for a skill until that skill is reviewed and rescheduled. A weak skill is not necessarily due; the SM-2 interval controls due status. Pending Study mode candidates are not in the deck and do not fire this event.
- **A skill fell back below mastered** - A graded attempt moved a skill's Bayesian Knowledge Tracing posterior from at or above the ready threshold to below it. A decrease from `0.55` to `0.48` does not fire if the skill was never above the threshold, and a wrong answer that leaves the posterior above it does not fire either. A degenerate BKT denominator leaves the posterior unchanged. Free-response answers fire this only after the rubric grade lands.
- **The exam date is at risk** - The trajectory projection needs more than 15% more sessions than the days remaining before the exam. It divides remaining mastery work by the observed gain per session over the last ten sessions and is deterministic from stored state. It does not fire with fewer than three sessions of history or without an exam date. It fires at most once per day per subject.
