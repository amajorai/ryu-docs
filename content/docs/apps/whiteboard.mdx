## What it does

An Excalidraw whiteboard shipped as a Ryu app: draw, diagram, and rearrange freely, insert attributed Openverse or node-configured Unsplash images, and use Mermaid-to-Excalidraw to import a diagram straight from text. Each board persists as a Space document, so it stays editable, shareable, and searchable.
