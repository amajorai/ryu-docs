## What it does

Wire is a personal newsroom that runs on your node. It imports RSS, Atom, and JSON Feed sources on a schedule. It uses conditional GET and per-source backoff, and marks a source `failing` after three consecutive misses.

Wire canonicalizes URLs by following redirects, removing `utm_*` parameters and similar tracking fields, and sorting query pairs. It fingerprints content with a banded 64-bit SimHash over word shingles, then clusters matching stories across outlets by shingle similarity, entity overlap, and title overlap. Each story shows its source count and the different framings instead of repeating the same paragraph.

Watches use boolean queries with `AND`, `OR`, `NOT`, phrases, `title:`, and `source:` scopes. Wire parses each query into an AST and reports the column when it cannot parse one. A breaking story is a topic whose hourly volume exceeds a same-hour baseline from the previous seven days; the result includes the z-score and the articles that caused it. Ranking uses recency half-life, source count, topic match, and unread state.

The model has two jobs. It writes two sentences per cluster in the brief and one neutral headline for
each story. The raw cluster remains available below both. Apart from fetching the sources you add,
Wire keeps data on the node.

### Capabilities

- **news.crud** - capability `news.crud`
  - Grant: `news:crud`

### MCP server

- **`news`** - Your reviewed feed as tools. `search` queries the local corpus of sources you added, after deduplication and clustering, without making a web call. `brief` fetches a generated digest, `story` opens a cluster with every outlet covering it, and `topics` lists saved watches and their current matches.

### Hook events

- **The daily brief finished generating** - A brief was generated and stored, either on the schedule in Settings → Wire or after someone requested it. Fires once per brief after the prose and source links are stored, so a workflow can read the finished document. A poll cycle that ingested articles without producing a brief does not fire this event, and a failed brief does not fire it either.
- **A watch is bursting** - A saved topic's article rate crossed its burst threshold. The z-score against the same-hour baseline from the previous seven days must reach the threshold, and the absolute count must clear the floor. The count floor prevents a topic with no usual hourly traffic from firing on one article. Fires at most once per topic per cooldown window. A topic matching articles at its usual rate does not fire this event.
- **A followed story picked up more outlets** - A followed story gained source articles since the last poll. Fires once per poll for each story that grew and includes the outlets that joined. An unchanged story and a newly opened cluster do not fire this event. A cluster you do not follow never fires it.
