## What it does

Inline ghost-text autocomplete in any text field on your machine, accepted with Tab. A small companion reads the caret context and asks Core for a completion, and every prediction is routed through the Gateway - so no model id, Gateway URL, or policy ever lives in the companion process. Windows-first.

## What it exposes

### Policies

- **predict** - On/off switch for system-wide inline autocomplete: the /api/predict/* brain used by the apps-store/predict overlay and any predict client. Enabling this plugin is the single switch - there is no separate settings toggle. Each suggestion still routes through the Gateway. Core-local - no gateway restart.
