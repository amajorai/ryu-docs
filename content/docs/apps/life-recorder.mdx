## Remember a conversation

Life Recorder collects speech and short notes into a private library. Open a
moment to read its transcript, create a recap with your configured Ryu model,
search for a detail, or copy the text into another app.

### On iPhone

Choose **Record conversation** and allow microphone access. Recording continues
while the phone is locked. Ryu writes minute-long audio chunks on the phone;
they remain queued until you return and choose **Stop and transcribe**.
Transcription retries keep unprocessed audio. Successfully saved chunks are
acknowledged before their temporary audio is deleted. The library groups a
recording into fifteen-minute moments.

The native **Microphone recording** strip lets you stop capture from any Ryu
screen, including when your node is offline. Stopping preserves queued audio.

**Rewind 15 seconds** and **Last 5 minutes** transcribe a recent portion of the
recording without stopping the session. You can save the preview if it matters.
Rewind cannot recover speech from before you started recording.

Capture never restarts itself after an app termination or phone restart. Open
Life Recorder again to process queued audio and start another session. Audio
interruptions can stop capture; check the microphone status before continuing.
The offline queue holds up to three days of minute-long chunks, then stops
capture until you process or discard audio.

### On desktop and Island

Shadow supplies desktop speech history. Enable its audio capture and
transcription before using rewind. Life Recorder reads existing transcript
windows; it does not turn on screen capture. A Shadow transcript window may
include speech slightly outside the selected interval, and the app labels these
as capture windows.

Desktop also offers a microphone recording of up to five minutes. Keep Ryu open
until transcription succeeds. Island uses the local computer's Shadow history
and its context-reading consent. It never treats a remote node's microphone as
the microphone on your device.

### Import and save

On desktop and iPhone, **Import audio** accepts browser-decodable WAV, MP3, M4A,
AAC, OGG, WebM, and FLAC files, up to 20 MB and ten minutes. Ryu converts imports
to the WAV format its transcription engines use. Codec support depends on the
device. Imported source files are not deleted or retained by Life Recorder.

**Add a note** works without audio. **Create recap** uses your configured Ryu
model, so its provider and routing settings determine where text is processed.
Recaps are memory aids; consult the transcript when a detail matters.

Recent moments expire seven days after creation and are removed when the
library next opens. Choose **Save moment** to keep one until you delete it.
Saved transcripts and recaps are sealed with Ryu's app-specific encryption key
on the selected node. Temporary microphone audio is stored separately on the
capturing device. Deleting a moment does not delete an imported original or
Shadow's source history.

The web surface supports notes, saved moments, search, and recaps. Android
supports the shared library and recording imports; continuous microphone
capture currently requires the iPhone native build.

## Inspiration

The workflow draws on [Life Recorder](https://github.com/browser-use/life-recorder)
and Apple's [Live Rewind and Siri Recap](https://support.apple.com/en-us/148354).
Ryu uses its own capture, storage, and model services. It does not use Apple's
Secure Enclave audio buffer or Private Cloud Compute.
