## What it does

Pick a video on this machine, transcribe it locally, translate the transcript into the language you want, and get a timed .srt or .vtt written beside the file so your player picks it up. Both model hops stay on the device - local whisper for the transcription, the local gateway's on-device model for the translation - so nothing about the video leaves the disk it is already on. Long jobs are queued and polled, so a feature film keeps working while you do something else.

### Hook events

- **Subtitle job queued** - A video was accepted for subtitling and is waiting for the worker. Fires once per job, before any decoding starts - the transcript does not exist yet, so subscribe to `job.completed` if you need the cues.
- **Subtitles ready** - A subtitle file was written. `output_path` is the copy written beside the source video, and is absent when that could not be written (a read-only volume, or the node setting turned off) - the downloadable copy always exists either way. The cues are NOT in the payload; read them from GET /api/subtitles/jobs/&#123;id&#125;/cues.
- **Subtitle job failed** - A job stopped before producing a file, and `error` says why - an unsupported audio codec, a file that moved while queued, or a transcription engine that could not be reached. Does NOT fire when a job merely finished with some lines untranslated; that job completed, and its `translated_count` is lower than its `cue_count`.
