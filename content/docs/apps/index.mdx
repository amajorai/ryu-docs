---
title: "Apps"
description: "Ryu Apps pair a focused user-facing workflow with an out-of-process sidecar through generic platform seams."
tags: ["Apps"]
---

Ryu Apps give a team a focused work surface for a repeatable process. Start with
an app, then change its workflow, views, rules, and integrations to match your
operation.

At runtime, each store app pairs an out-of-process sidecar with a user-facing
surface. Apps connect to the platform through generic ext-proxy,
capability-broker, and companion seams. App-specific behavior stays app-owned;
Core and Gateway continue to own the shared boundaries around identity,
permissions, budgets, approvals, and audit history.

If an app accepts conversational changes, represent each one as a reviewable
proposal. The app can describe the requested adaptation, but publishing a
production change must remain an explicit, authorized action. The app owns the
change; Core and Gateway continue to enforce the shared boundaries.
