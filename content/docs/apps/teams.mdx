## What it does

Teams are named groups of agents you can address as one. The Teams Companion owns creation,
editing, membership, coordination strategy, and lead-agent selection. Its contributed sidebar
section shows the current roster and opens the Companion for management. Core keeps the
in-crate teams store and the sidecar owns the app surface behind `/api/teams/*`.

## UI it contributes

### Companion surfaces

- **Teams** - Browse, create, edit, and delete teams; add or remove agents; choose coordination
  and lead-agent settings.

The Companion uses `app:http` for its own sidecar, `core:list_agents` for the member picker,
`ui:toast` for short result feedback, and `ui:declarative-http` for the live sidebar roster.

### Sidebar sections

- **Teams** - A live list of teams. Selecting a row opens the Teams Companion.

### Hook events

- **Team created** - A new team was persisted, from the desktop, the API, or Core's own agent-builder. Fires exactly once per team, on the insert that mints its id - a failed create does not fire. The payload is the team record as `GET /api/teams/&#123;id&#125;` returns it, so a brand-new team may already carry members (they can be supplied at create time) and its `coordination` defaults to `broadcast`.
- **Team changed** - A team's configuration changed: its name, description, coordination strategy, lead agent, or roster. Fires for both the full patch (`PATCH /api/teams/&#123;id&#125;`) and the incremental member endpoints used by the desktop drag gesture. Both member endpoints are idempotent, so re-adding an existing member or removing an absent member emits nothing. The payload is the team after the change, not a diff; compare `members` with your last-seen copy to find changes.
- **Team deleted** - A team was removed. Fires only when a row was deleted, so deleting an unknown id is silent. The payload is the team as it stood immediately before deletion, which lets a consumer clean up per-team or per-member state.
 - **Team changed** - A team's configuration changed - renamed, re-described, its coordination strategy or lead agent swapped, or an agent added to or removed from its roster. Fires for both the full patch (`PATCH /api/teams/&#123;id&#125;`) and the incremental member endpoints the Companion uses. Both member endpoints are idempotent, and a re-add of an existing member (or a remove of one who was never on the team) changes nothing and does not fire. The payload is the team after the change, not a diff; compare `members` against your own last-seen copy to see who moved.
 - **Team deleted** - A team was removed. Fires only when a row was actually deleted, so deleting an id that does not exist is silent. The payload is the team as it stood immediately before deletion, which lets a consumer clean up per-team or per-member state.
