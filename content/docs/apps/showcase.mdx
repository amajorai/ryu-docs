The [Ryu Apps showcase](https://ryuhq.com/products/apps) is the public visual index for the official app surfaces Ryu ships. Each listing has a lightweight skeleton preview and its own detail page, so you can understand the shape of the work before opening the full app.

The showcase currently highlights:

- [Sites](https://ryuhq.com/products/apps/sites) for creating, refining, and publishing websites.
- [Dashboards](https://ryuhq.com/products/apps/dashboards) for live widget boards over a Ryu node.
- [Video Studio](https://ryuhq.com/products/apps/video-studio) for layered timelines, captions, and rendering.
- [Slides](https://ryuhq.com/products/apps/slides) for creating and exporting carousel frames.
- [Spaces](https://ryuhq.com/products/apps/spaces) for notes, documents, files, and retrieval context.
- [Calendar](https://ryuhq.com/products/apps/calendar) for scheduled agent and workflow runs.
- [Canvas](https://ryuhq.com/products/apps/canvas) for media and agent graphs saved in Spaces.

These previews are illustrations of the user-facing surface. They are not live account data and do not replace the [Marketplace](/docs/extend/develop/extensions/marketplace), which remains the source for published installable listings.

For the runtime contract behind each app, continue to the [Apps catalog](/docs/apps), [app manifest lifecycle](/docs/core/app-manifest-lifecycle), and [app reuse standard](/docs/extend/develop/extensions/app-reuse-standard).
