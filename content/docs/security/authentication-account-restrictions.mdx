---
title: "Restricted Accounts and Appeals"
description: "What happens when Ryu restricts an account, and how to appeal an account restriction or an automatic Gateway step."
---

Part of the [Authentication and pairing](/docs/security/authentication-and-pairing) guide.

Ryu staff can restrict an account that breaks Ryu's usage policies. Restricting an account is always
a person's decision, never an automatic one. While an account is restricted:

- Signing in on the website with a password, a passkey, an email code or Google shows **Your account
  is restricted** and an **Appeal this decision** button instead of a sign-in error. Password, passkey
  and email code sign-ins also show the reason in plain words.
- Gateway keys and API keys the account owned are revoked. Lifting the restriction later restores
  sign-in, not those keys, so create new ones.

## Appeal a restriction

When the restriction is applied, Ryu emails an appeal link to the address the account had at that
moment. The link opens the appeal page without signing in. It shows what was restricted and why, and
lets you send one appeal of up to 4,000 characters, with an optional contact address for questions.

- The link lasts 30 days. If it expired or you cannot find the email, open the appeal page, enter the
  address the account had when it was restricted, and ask for a new link. A new link goes only to that
  address, never to one you changed to later. Earlier links keep working until they expire, so asking
  for a new link never cuts off the link you already have open. The page answers the same way whether
  or not the address matches, so it cannot be used to check whether an account is restricted.
- You can appeal each decision once. Ryu reviews appeals within five business days and emails the
  outcome to the same address.
- An upheld appeal lifts the restriction. Sometimes lifting it needs one more confirmation from Ryu
  first. The outcome email then says you cannot sign in yet, and Ryu emails you again when access is
  restored. If that confirmation does not happen, your appeal goes back under review, Ryu tells you so,
  and you get a new decision. You do not need to appeal again.
- When the decision rested on a model provider's block, Ryu clears only its own side: the provider's
  block stays in place, and Ryu does not route you to another provider to get around it. The appeal
  page says so when it applies.
- The appeal link only shows and appeals that one decision. It never signs you in. Each link can make
  a limited number of appeal requests per day, and so can each network address Ryu can identify.
- Ryu removes the text of a decided appeal and its contact address 90 days after the decision. The
  address the account had when it was restricted is removed 90 days after the restriction ends, unless
  an appeal against it is still open. Deleting your account removes both at once.

## Appeal an automatic step

When the hosted Gateway throttles, restricts or suspends a gateway key or an organization after
repeated violations, the email to the organization's owners and admins carries an **Appeal this
decision** link. It works the same way as an account appeal link, with these differences:

- The appeal is filed for the organization. Anyone who received the email can send it, once per
  link. A later step on the same key or organization comes with a new link that can be appealed
  again. When one review covers several keys, each key's link appeals that key alone, and the
  outcome for one key never changes another.
- A link appeals only the step it announced. Once the key or organization moves to a stronger step,
  the older link can no longer be used. Appeal from the link in the later step's email instead.
- When an automatic step makes a suspension that a person at Ryu set last until review, the email
  carries no appeal link. Contact Ryu support to appeal that suspension.
- The page shows what the step applies to (a gateway key or the organization), what it does, the
  policy areas, and how long it lasts.
- A throttle or restriction that already ended can still be appealed while the violations it was based
  on still count toward the next step.
- An upheld appeal clears those violations and lifts the step. Lifting some organization suspensions
  needs one more confirmation from Ryu first, and the outcome says so. If that confirmation does not
  happen, the appeal goes back under review and you get a new decision.
- If the organization is also suspended for a separate reason, an upheld appeal against a key or
  organization step lifts that step only. The outcome says the suspension stays in place.
- If a stronger step replaced the appealed one before Ryu decided, the appeal changes nothing, and the
  outcome tells you to appeal the later step.
- Ryu sends the outcome to the organization's owners and admins in the inbox and by email.
- There is no new-link form for these steps. If the link expired, contact Ryu support.
- Child safety actions never carry an appeal link.
