The [Challenges page](https://ryuhq.com/challenges) contains example briefs for building Ryu apps and automating recurring tasks.

**Challenges are in preview.** The briefs, dates, prize amounts, and archive projects are examples. Public submissions and credit awards are not open. Saving an entry does not send it to judges or add credits to a balance.

## Browse the briefs

Choose **All**, **Apps**, **Automation**, or **Community** to filter the examples. Each brief lists the task, requirements, example dates and prize pool, and review criteria. Expand an item in **Example archive** to see its sample projects.

## Try the entry form

1. Choose **Try the entry form** on a brief.
2. Enter your name, project name, demo or repository URL, and a description of what it does. Your handle is optional.
3. Choose **Save preview entry**.

The confirmation says that the entry stays in this browser. Browser storage must be available to keep it across visits; clearing site data removes it. Preview entries are not shared with other users.

## Prepare a project

Include a working demo or repository, setup instructions, and a walkthrough of the complete workflow. Describe the tools it uses and any actions that require human approval. Remove credentials and private customer data before sharing a project.
