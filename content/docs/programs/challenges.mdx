This program connects to the [Programs catalog](/docs/programs), [builder path](/docs/learn/academy/builder), [public Apps catalog](/docs/apps), and [Marketplace](/docs/extend/develop/extensions/marketplace) so participants can verify the product and publishing boundaries behind a submission.

The [Ryu Challenges page](https://ryuhq.com/challenges) lists current and past build challenges, hackathons, and community events. Each published listing has its own detail page with the schedule, format, host, brief, rules, judging criteria, and prizes.

## Find an opportunity

Use the type filters to browse challenges, hackathons, and events. Search by title, host, or category, then switch between open and past opportunities. Dates are shown in the time zone published with the listing. Participant and submission counts come from registrations saved by the service.

The public list contains published listings only. Drafts and archived listings are available to platform administrators in the Challenges console.

## Register and submit

Sign in with your Ryu account to register. Registration is saved to your account and appears under [My entries](https://ryuhq.com/challenges/my-entries).

For a challenge or hackathon:

1. Join while registration is open. You can join before the build starts.
2. When the listing is live, add a project name, demo or repository URL, and a short description.
3. Choose whether your name and project may appear on the public results page if selected.
4. Update your submission before the closing time if your project changes.

An event registration does not require a project submission. A challenge or hackathon entry must include an HTTP or HTTPS project link and a description before the deadline. Each Ryu account has one entry per listing. Entries are private to the participant and platform administrators until results are published.

## Review and Ryu Points

Administrators review submitted projects, shortlist or decline entries, and select winners from the published prize places after the listing closes. A delivered award is added to the participant's Ryu Points balance and recorded once, even if the award request is retried. The award amount and place remain attached to the entry.

The public results page shows winners after the listing closes. If a participant did not opt in to public display, the result hides their name and project details.

## Manage a listing

Platform administrators can create drafts, edit listing details, publish or archive a listing, review registrations, and award Ryu Points from [/admin/challenges](https://ryuhq.com/admin/challenges). Start and closing times use the selected IANA time zone. Once someone registers, the schedule is locked so a public deadline cannot change underneath participants.

Archiving removes a listing from public discovery. A closed, published listing remains available with its results.
