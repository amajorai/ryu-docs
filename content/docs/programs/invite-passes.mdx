title: "Invite and Milestone Passes"
description: "Share weekly invite passes by plan, redeem a pass, earn a multi-seat milestone pass from agent hours, and export a pass card to share"
---

Invite passes and milestone passes give new people access to Ryu. They are separate from the
credit and cash rewards on [Referrals and Affiliates](/docs/programs/referrals). Open the live
[Referrals page](https://ryuhq.com/referrals) to see and share your passes.

Pass codes and links are masked by default. The reveal and hide controls are described in
[Share your referral link](/docs/programs/referrals#share-your-referral-link).

## Weekly invite passes

Once your account has access, open [Referrals](https://ryuhq.com/referrals) to
share invite passes each week. The number depends on your plan.

| Plan | Invite passes per week |
|---|---:|
| Free, Ryu Desktop licence, or another one-time purchase | 3 |
| Ryu Plus | 5 |
| Ryu Pro | 6 |
| Ryu Max | 7 |
| Ryu Teams Lite | 7 |
| Ryu Teams | 8 |
| Ryu Business | 10 |

Each pass uses Ryu's interactive 3D pass card,
with its access code, redemption status, and expiry. Copy an invite below its card
and send it to a friend, or choose **Send by email** to enter one recipient and
an optional personal note. Ryu emails that pass's card link and access code.
Each pass can be emailed once; you can still copy its link afterward.

The link opens the same 3D card on its own invite page, including whether it is
available, redeemed, or expired. Recipients sign in or create an account, return
to that card, and choose **Redeem my pass**. The bottom ticket stub tears away
after redemption succeeds, and the sender sees the same effect when the pass
status changes to **Used**. They do not need to retype the code.
The six-character code also works in the waitlist's access-code form.
When entering it there, you can paste spaces or hyphens with the code; the form removes them and
normalizes the six-character code to uppercase before redemption.
Emailing or viewing a pass does not consume it; redeeming it does.

Each member pass grants access to one account. Your plan's fresh passes are
available every Monday at 00:00 UTC. Upgrading mid-week adds the extra passes right away.
Passes already issued that week stay available after a downgrade. Unused passes expire at the
reset and do not roll over.
The page shows the next reset in your local time and lets you refresh redemption
status. The **Invite history** tab also lists each pass, the address it was emailed
to when applicable, and whether it is still available, failed delivery, expired,
or redeemed. A copied pass link has no identifiable recipient until an account
redeems it. Accounts still on the waitlist cannot issue passes. Once an invited
account has access, it can share its own weekly passes.

Invite passes grant account access. Referral credits and rewards follow the
[referral program rules](/docs/programs/referrals#credit-referral-campaign). Admin-issued access codes
can optionally expire at a chosen date and time, have a maximum number of
redemptions, or use both limits. A code stops working when either limit is
reached.

## Milestone passes

Using Ryu also earns a milestone pass: one code that several people can share. Open
[Referrals](https://ryuhq.com/referrals) to see your progress in agent hours.
Once you reach the milestone, the page shows your milestone pass: one access
code, the number of seats it holds, and how many are still open. Copy the invite
or choose **Give it away on X** to post it with your referral link.

Seats are first come, first served, and a claimed seat never frees up. Each
person who signs up through your link, or redeems the code on the waitlist
screen, takes one seat and counts as your referral. While Ryu has a waitlist,
a seat also lets that person skip the queue. After the waitlist ends, the same
code keeps working as a referral code until its seats run out. The code itself
does not expire, and you cannot redeem your own pass.

Milestone passes are separate from your weekly invite passes and do not use up
any of them.

## Share a pass card

The back of each waitlist and referral pass has a QR code that opens your referral link
(`/r/CODE`).

From **Share your pass**, choose a still image, a ten-second looping video, or a looping GIF in
portrait (9:16), feed (4:5), post (3:4), square (1:1), or landscape (16:9). Copy or download the
image, or download the video or GIF. The GIF is smaller (480 pixels on its short edge) and plays on
its own in chat apps such as Slack, Discord and iMessage. Video export can take longer on a slower
device. Keep the window in the foreground until the file is ready. Closing the chooser stops an
active export and resets the next share to the default image format.

Milestone passes and unlocked battle-pass tiers have a **Share card** action too. It opens the same
chooser with a card that shows the milestone or tier you reached and the counts the Referrals page
already shows for it, such as agent hours and seats, or quest progress, reward and points. The back
of the card carries a QR code to your invite link, and the X and LinkedIn actions share that same
link.
