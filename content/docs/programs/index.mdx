## Lifetime updates reward

Ryu Lifetime owners can get one more year of updates by posting publicly about Ryu with `#Ryu`.

1. Post about Ryu on a public social account and include `#Ryu`. Set the post to Public.
2. Sign in on the [redemption page](https://ryuhq.com/redeem) and paste the post's `https://` link.
3. Ryu reviews the post, then adds the year to your lifetime licence.

The current offer and terms are on the [redemption page](https://ryuhq.com/redeem).
