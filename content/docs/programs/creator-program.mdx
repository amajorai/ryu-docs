---

The Ryu Creator Program is for people who teach, review, or recommend tools for AI-agent work.
Approved creators can join campaigns, claim a custom handle for each campaign, and share a link
that sends supporters to eligible Marketplace items.

Apply from the live [Creator Program page](https://ryuhq.com/creator). Approval is separate from
Marketplace publishing: it does not grant `marketplace.publish`, seller status, or a Stripe payout
account.

## Campaign links

A campaign link looks like:

```text
https://ryuhq.com/creator/c/spring-launch/maria_studio
```

The handle belongs to that campaign. The same creator may use a different handle for another
campaign, while Ryu keeps the internal participant and attribution records stable if a public handle
changes.

When a supporter opens the link and browses Marketplace, the campaign context stays attached to the
web checkout. A qualifying item must be live, paid, and eligible for that campaign. The
server reads the price, seller, and reward terms itself. It does not trust the URL or browser.

## Support reset and bonuses

Each campaign defines its support window. The default is 14 days, but a campaign can use another
bounded duration. When the window ends, the supporter is not silently reassigned. They can choose a
creator again at a later checkout.

Most campaigns include a standard buyer credit bonus, but some have none. The
campaign page shows the reward terms. A campaign may also carry a campaign- or
participant-specific creator commission rule. Ryu snapshots the rule at checkout, so changing a
campaign later does not rewrite a completed purchase.

Buyer credit bonuses are Ryu Credits, not cash. They are pool-restricted and may expire according to
the campaign terms. They cannot be used to buy third-party Marketplace items.

## Payouts and refunds

Creator earnings use the same review and Stripe Connect payout process as the
[affiliate program](/docs/programs/referrals#affiliate-program). A commission is pending until it
passes review. Subscription campaigns can pay recurring commissions up to the campaign's duration
limit.

- A full refund or dispute reverses an unpaid commission. A commission that was already paid may
  need an operator clawback.
- Refunding or disputing one subscription renewal reverses that renewal's commission only.
- The buyer's credit bonus is reversed only when the first payment, the one that earned it, is
  refunded or disputed.
- A payment refunded or disputed before Ryu records it earns neither reward.

If a creator's approval is suspended or rejected, their campaign links stop working and they leave
every campaign they had joined. Purchases attributed to them that settle afterwards, including
subscription renewals, earn no further creator commission or buyer credit bonus. A creator who is
approved again must rejoin each campaign.

Campaign attribution takes precedence over the legacy account referral for that Marketplace
purchase, so one purchase does not pay two competing creators. Legacy referral links continue to
work for ordinary signups and purchases outside a creator campaign.
