The Ryu Partner Program lets organizations offer Ryu as a member benefit. Incubators, accelerators, universities, student programs, VC portfolios, community programs, and agencies can list Ryu on their resource or perks page.

See the live [Partner Program page](https://ryuhq.com/partners) to start a conversation.

## Who it is for

- **Incubators and accelerators** that want to give founders a governed agent workspace.
- **Universities and student programs** that help students learn to build with agents.
- **VC and community portfolios** that maintain a shared resource or perks page.
- **Agencies and resellers** that build and run agents for clients.

## What partners get

- A hosted perk that members can redeem.
- A simple way to verify and onboard members.
- Co-marketing and a place in the partner directory.
- A direct support channel with the Ryu team.

The program is free for partners. Ryu hosts the member perk. The partner brings the community and a place to list it.

## What members get

Members can receive the full Ryu desktop Pro app for the window attached to their program. The experience supports local models, user-provided keys, swappable providers, and Gateway policy for model calls.

The current public offer gives founders in a partner incubator or accelerator six months. Students and early-stage startups have a separate offer with a three-month window. See [Startups & Students](/docs/programs/startups-and-students) for the eligibility split.

<Callout type="warn">
  Block71 / NUS Enterprise is identified as Ryu's first incubator partner. A six-month window is for verified founders in a partner program, not an automatic benefit for every incubator.
</Callout>

## How it works

1. Tell Ryu who your members are and how you want to offer the perk.
2. Ryu sets up a redemption path and a lightweight membership check.
3. List Ryu on your resource or perks page. Members apply, get verified, and unlock the offer.

## Community partners and product partners

These are two different ways to work with Ryu.

| Partner type | Contribution | Member or user outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Community partner | Brings Ryu to a defined community | Members can redeem Ryu access through the program |
| Product partner | Offers a deal on its own product | Ryu builders can find and redeem the deal in [Perks](/docs/programs/perks) |

If you run a product that helps with inference, fine-tuning, browsers, sandboxes, or agent infrastructure, [list a product perk](https://ryuhq.com/perks/submit).
