Ryu for Students and Ryu for Startups give eligible people a free, time-limited Ryu Desktop licence. You use the full desktop app with your own provider API keys or local models.

<Callout type="warn">
  Each program opens in stages and is off until Ryu opens it. While a program is open, its page is live at [ryuhq.com/students](https://ryuhq.com/students) or [ryuhq.com/startups](https://ryuhq.com/startups). If the page is not found, that program is not open yet.
</Callout>

## The offers

| Program | Free licence | How you qualify |
|---|---:|---|
| Students | 1 year | Confirm a school-issued email address with a one-time code |
| Startups with a partner code | 12 months | Enter a code from a partner incubator, accelerator, or investor. Approval is instant. |
| Startups | 6 months | Apply. A person reviews every application. |

Each offer can be used once per Ryu account. A program licence never replaces a plan or licence you already hold.

## What is included

- The Ryu Desktop licence for the approved window, with chat, agents, workflows, Spaces, and every local desktop feature.
- Your own provider API keys (BYOK) or local models. You pay your provider directly.
- The local Gateway for routing, firewall rules, budgets, and audit.

## What is not included

The program licence is the desktop app with your own keys. It does not include:

- Ryu Cloud nodes or hosted bots.
- Managed inference or included usage credits.
- Paid Marketplace apps.
- Team seats or organization billing.

These stay on paid plans. See [Pricing](https://ryuhq.com/pricing).

## Students

1. Sign in to Ryu with any account, including a personal email.
2. On the Students page, enter the email address your school gave you.
3. Type in the 6-digit code sent to that address. The code works for 15 minutes. Your year starts immediately.
4. Sign in to Ryu Desktop with the same account.

Ryu accepts common academic domains such as `.edu`, `.edu.<country>`, and `.ac.<country>`, plus a reviewed list of universities that use other domains. Personal mailboxes such as Gmail, Outlook, or iCloud cannot confirm enrolment. Alumni forwarding addresses are not accepted. Each school email can be linked to one Ryu account.

If your school uses a different domain and is not recognized, contact support from your school address.

## Startups

Ryu for Startups is for companies founded in the last five years that have raised less than $5M in total.

1. Sign in to Ryu and open the Startups page.
2. Enter your company, website, founding year, team size, and total funding raised.
3. If your incubator, accelerator, or investor gave you a partner code, enter it. A valid code approves you instantly for 12 months.
4. Without a code, a person reviews the application and approves 6 months. The decision appears on the Startups page.

Ryu issues partner codes to its partner communities. A code can have a usage limit, an expiry date, or both. If your program is not a partner yet, it can join the [Partner Program](/docs/programs/partner-program), or you can apply without a code.

The licence attaches to the account that applies. Each co-founder can apply from their own account with the same company details.

## After the window

Your data stays on your device. The licence does not renew automatically. You can buy the desktop licence or move to a plan to keep the licensed features.

If you run an incubator, accelerator, university, or student program, see the [Partner Program](/docs/programs/partner-program) to offer Ryu to your members.
