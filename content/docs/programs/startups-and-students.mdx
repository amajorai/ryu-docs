Ryu offers the full desktop Pro app to verified students and early-stage founders. You can run local models, bring your own keys, or evaluate Ryu against a managed setup during the free window.

Apply through the live [Startups & Students offer](https://ryuhq.com/startups).

## Current eligibility

| Group | Public offer | Who it is for |
|---|---:|---|
| Students | 3 months free | Verified students with a school email or current enrollment |
| Incubated startups | 6 months free | Founders in a partner incubator or accelerator |
| Startups | 3 months free | Early-stage companies, with or without a backer |

Block71 / NUS Enterprise is Ryu's first incubator partner. If you are in another program, name it in your application so Ryu can verify the program and the correct tier.

## What is included

- The full Ryu desktop Pro app for the approved window.
- Chat, agents, workflows, Spaces, and the Marketplace.
- Local models and user-provided API keys.
- Managed cloud or local execution, depending on the setup you choose.
- Swappable models, agents, and providers.

The offer does not attach a separate pool of usage credits. You run local inference, bring your own provider keys, or use the managed billing path for cloud inference.

## Apply

1. Open the [startup and student application](https://ryuhq.com/startups).
2. Tell Ryu who you are, what you are building, and which group fits.
3. Share the evidence needed for verification, such as a school email, current enrollment, or your incubator program.
4. Download Ryu and use Pro during the approved window.

There is no equity requirement and no card is needed to apply.

## After the free window

The local app keeps working on your device. You can move to a paid managed plan for cloud features, or continue with local models and your own provider keys.

If you run an incubator, accelerator, university, or student program, see the [Partner Program](/docs/programs/partner-program) to offer Ryu to your members.
