title: "Ryu Discord"
description: "Get a personal, single-use invite to the Ryu community Discord from your account"
---

The Ryu community Discord has no public invite link. Each Ryu account gets its own invite.

## Get your invite

1. Sign in to Ryu. A waitlisted account works too.
2. Open the user menu on the website or in the desktop app and choose **Join Discord**. This opens
   the [Discord invite page](https://ryuhq.com/discord).
3. Select **Generate my invite**, then **Open in Discord**.

## How invites work

- **One use.** Your invite admits exactly one person. Once you join with it, it stops working.
- **Personal.** The invite is tied to your Ryu account, so do not post or share it. A friend
  who wants to join can sign up for Ryu and get their own invite. Use your
  [referral link](/docs/programs/referrals) to invite them.
- **Expires after 24 hours.** If you don't use it in time, come back to the page for a new one.
- **Stable until used.** Reopening the page shows the same invite while it is still unused, so
  refreshing does not create a new one.
- **Limited per day.** You can generate a few new invites per day. If you reach the limit, the
  page tells you to try again later.
