The desktop app is the primary surface for Ryu and the most complete one. This page is the full
feature reference: everything you do in the app. The [CLI](/docs/surfaces/cli), [Island](/docs/surfaces/island),
and [Raycast](/docs/surfaces/raycast) pages cover those surfaces and how they differ.

Every surface talks to the **same Core** (the local backend on `:7980`), so a chat you start here is
the same conversation you open in the CLI, the Island, or on your phone.
