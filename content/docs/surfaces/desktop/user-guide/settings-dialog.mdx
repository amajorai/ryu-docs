title: "The Settings Dialog"
description: "How the desktop Settings dialog is laid out, what General, Appearance, and Sounds hold, and where the longer explanations live."
---

Press **Ctrl+.** (or **Cmd+.** on macOS), or choose **Settings** from the account menu, to open
Settings. Gateway settings (**Ctrl+,** or **Cmd+,**) use the same frame.

## Layout

- **Navigation** runs down the left on the sidebar tone. Each section has a plain icon and its
  name. The search box sits at the top; click it or press Tab to search every setting.
- **The pane** on the right shows the section title and its settings as one list of rows, with a
  thin line between rows. A row's label is on the left with at most one short line of help
  beneath it, and its control is on the right. Changes apply at once.
- The edge between the two columns can be dragged to resize the navigation. The handle appears
  when you point at it.
- **Device settings** (or **Gateway settings**) and **Send feedback** sit at the foot of the
  navigation. The first closes Settings and opens the node's own settings.
- Under **Services**, the workspace switcher names the organization that Billing, Referrals,
  Teams, Credits and Usage apply to. Open it to switch; each workspace's plan is shown in that
  menu.

## General

General opens on **On startup**: the projectless task folder, what the window opens with, the
startup realm, and which account and node to use. If the startup realm can't be saved, the reason
appears under the row, next to a **Retry saving startup realm** button.

Below it, **We never train on your data** is a single row. **Learn more** opens the
[training commitment](/docs/surfaces/desktop/transparency#training-commitment) in full. Terms and
Privacy are in the account menu.

The rest of General is split into pages: **Tabs & panes**, **Chats**, **Language**, **Terminal**,
**Files**, **System & tray** and **Setup & recovery**. Each row's help line is short; open a page to
see its settings. A **Keyboard shortcuts** row at the end of General and Voice opens the shortcut
editor.

## Appearance

Appearance opens on **Theme** (Light, Dark or System) and **Color theme**, where you pick a preset
for each mode or adjust its colors. The selected theme and primary color are marked with a ring.
The remaining options are on their own pages: **Language & vibe**, **Layout & text**,
**Motion & effects**, **Interface**, **Chat view**, **Usage meter**, **Diff viewer**,
**File tree** and **Reset**.

**Seasonal effects**, under **Motion & effects**, is off by default. Turn it on to show holiday
particles in the title bar; it also needs **Enable animations**, and it never runs while your
system asks for reduced motion. **Preview** shows the selected season for a few seconds even
while the switch is off.

## Sounds

**Sounds** is its own section, below Appearance. It holds the **Play sounds** switch, **Volume**,
**Sound pack**, **Quiet while Do Not Disturb**, and a switch and **Preview** button for each event.
See [Sounds](/docs/surfaces/desktop/user-guide/sounds) for what each sound means.
