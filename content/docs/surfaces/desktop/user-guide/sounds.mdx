title: "Sounds"
description: "Which short sounds the Ryu desktop app plays for messages, runs, approvals, calls, and rewards, and how to change the volume, pick a sound pack, turn single sounds off, or silence them all."
---

The desktop app plays short sounds when something happens that you may want to notice: a message
arrives, an agent run finishes or fails, an agent needs your approval, or a call connects. Every
sound in the default pack is built from the same rising open fifth, so a Ryu cue is easy to tell
apart from other apps. It is part of the
[Desktop User Guide](/docs/surfaces/desktop/user-guide).

All sound controls are in **Settings → Sounds**. Press **Ctrl+.** (or **Cmd+.** on macOS) to open
Settings, or search for "sounds" in the settings search box.

## What plays and when

Each event has its own row in **Settings → Sounds**, grouped under **Activity**, **Agents**,
**Calls**, and **Rewards**, and can be turned on or off on its own.

| Event | Plays when | On by default |
|---|---|---|
| **Message received** | A notification arrives for you | Yes |
| **Message sent** | You send a message to an agent | No |
| **Run complete** | An agent run finishes, in the open chat or in the background | Yes |
| **Run failed** | An agent run ends with an error | Yes |
| **Approval needed** | An agent asks for your permission or approval | Yes |
| **Alerts** | A monitor alert, or a warning or error notification, arrives | Yes |
| **Credit alert** | Your credit balance crosses an alert threshold | Yes |
| **Quest suggestions** | Ryu suggests a quest | Yes |
| **Quest completed** | A quest is completed | Yes |
| **Achievement unlocked** | You unlock a feature with points | Yes |
| **Calling** | A voice call is connecting (a repeating ring) | Yes |
| **Join and leave** | A voice call connects, ends, or fails to connect | Yes |
| **Mute and unmute** | You mute or unmute your microphone in a voice call | Yes |
| **Meeting detected** | Ryu detects that a meeting has started | Yes |

Choosing **Stop** on a run does not play **Run complete** or **Run failed** for it, in any Ryu
window. The next message you send in that chat plays its sounds as usual. When the open chat and a
background notification report the same finished run, the sound plays once, and it plays once
even when several Ryu windows are open.

## Turn all sounds off

Turn off **Play sounds** at the top of **Settings → Sounds** to silence every sound. The other
controls (**Volume**, **Sound pack**, **Quiet while Do Not Disturb**, and each event's switch and
**Preview** button) stay visible but are unavailable until you turn sounds back on, because nothing
can play while sounds are off.

## Volume

**Volume** sets how loud sounds are, from 0 to 100%. When the slider stops moving for a moment, a
short sample plays at the new level. Setting it to 0% silences every sound, the same as turning
**Play sounds** off.

Volume is saved on this device only, so a laptop and a desktop can use different levels.

## Sound packs

**Sound pack** changes the style of every sound at once. Each pack keeps the same short melodies,
so a sound means the same thing in any pack.

- **Ryu** (default) is bright and bell-like.
- **Soft** is gentler and quieter, with rounded, muffled tones.
- **Retro** is an 8-bit style with square-wave tones and quick octave arpeggios. Sounds for bad
  news, such as **Run failed**, still fall in pitch.

Choosing a pack plays a sample of **Run complete** in that style.

## Per-event toggles and previews

Each event row has a switch and a **Preview** button. Turn the switch off to stop that one sound;
the others keep playing. **Preview** plays the sound in the selected pack and at the current
volume, even if that event is turned off (but not while **Play sounds** is off).

Some rows cover related sounds: **Join and leave** includes the sound for a call that fails to
connect, and **Mute and unmute** includes both directions.

## Do Not Disturb and calls

**Quiet while Do Not Disturb** is on by default. While your
[availability](/docs/surfaces/desktop/user-guide/availability-and-prompts) is **Do not disturb**,
notification and reward sounds (messages, runs, approvals, alerts, quests, achievements, and
meeting detection) stay silent. Sounds that answer something you did, such as **Message sent**,
**Mute and unmute**, and the call sounds, still play.

During a voice call, notification sounds play at half their usual volume so they do not cover the
conversation.

## Calls

Voice calls use three kinds of sound:

- **Calling** rings while the call connects, and stops as soon as the call is ready, fails, or you
  hang up.
- **Join and leave** plays when the call connects, when it ends after connecting, and when it fails
  to connect.
- **Mute and unmute** plays each time you toggle your microphone.

## Sync

Your sound choices (**Play sounds**, **Sound pack**, **Quiet while Do Not Disturb**, and the event
switches) follow your account through
[Settings Sync](/docs/surfaces/desktop/user-guide/cross-device-sync#desktop-settings-sync) when
it is on. **Volume** stays on each device.

If your system or browser cannot play these sounds, **Settings → Sounds** says so and its controls
are turned off.
