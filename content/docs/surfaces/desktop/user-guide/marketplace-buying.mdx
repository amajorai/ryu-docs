This page explains how to buy paid Marketplace items, manage their licenses, and add organization
credits. To browse
listings, see the [Marketplace guide](/docs/surfaces/desktop/user-guide/marketplace).

<Callout type="info">
Purchases, licenses, seller accounts, and credits belong to the active organization. Select or create
the organization you want to use before you browse or buy. The web and desktop Marketplace share
these account and purchase rules.
</Callout>

## The three tabs

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Browse** | Paid and free listings, with a price and Buy action for items your organization does not own. The web Marketplace also lets you filter by followed publishers. |
| **My licenses** | Paid items owned by your organization, with their current status. A subscription whose renewal is cancelled shows **Renewal off** until it ends. On the web, subscription licenses also have **Manage**. |
| **Sell** | Payout setup and publisher totals for your organization. |

Free items do not create a paid license. Use the listing's **Open in Ryu** or install action to add one. For installation steps and downloads, see the [Store installation guide](/docs/surfaces/desktop/user-guide/store-installation).

The web Marketplace remembers which publishers you follow on your account. That preference does not change the catalogs or install permissions on your nodes.

## Buy a paid item

1. Open **Browse** and choose a kind: Skills, Plugins, MCP, or Models.
2. Review the listing price and license terms, then click **Buy**.
3. Complete checkout in the browser. The checkout page shows the total, including applicable sales tax.
4. After payment clears, the item appears as **Owned** in Browse and under **My licenses**. Marketplace refreshes the license list when you return to the page.

If your organization already has an active license, Marketplace keeps that license and does not charge again. A bounded-update license describes the purchase terms. Installing a signed release still follows the same publisher, permission, and node-access checks, and the update allowance is not a runtime paywall.

For recurring items, only a paid invoice extends the license. A failed renewal or a subscription
status update does not restore a refunded or disputed license.

<Callout type="info">
  One-time, bounded-update, or recurring terms are shown with the listing. The price you pay is the
  stored price plus tax, which Stripe Tax calculates and collects. Checkout shows the final amount
  before you confirm payment. The platform takes a commission per sale (20% by default), and Stripe
  sends the rest to the seller's connected account.
</Callout>

### How checkout works

1. **Buy** calls `POST /api/marketplace/purchase` with the item `kind` and `id`.
2. The server reads the price, cadence, platform fee, and seller from the stored pricing, never
   from the client. It creates a Stripe Checkout session for a one-time, bounded-update, or
   recurring Connect charge.
3. [Ryu's checkout page](/docs/billing/checkout) opens in your browser.
4. A server webhook grants the license after payment clears. The app fetches your licenses again
   when its window regains focus.

If the organization already holds an active license, the route returns `alreadyLicensed: true` and
the existing license, with no new charge.

The server decides whether a GitHub download is a first install or an update from the requested
build. The `purpose` query parameter is only a client hint. Signed downloads use `private, no-store`
caching so a shared cache never serves one handoff to someone else.

### How install trust is enforced

Before installation, Ryu checks the publisher signature, package contents, dependencies, declared permissions, and your access to the selected node. A price does not change those checks. Ryu manages the release download as part of the install flow; you do not need to fetch release files yourself.

For publishing and signing details, see [Develop: Marketplace](/docs/extend/develop/extensions/marketplace).

### Refunds, disputes, and enabling paid items

When you enable a paid Marketplace item, Ryu re-checks your organization's license with your signed-in account. If the purchase was refunded, is under a payment dispute, or the license has expired, Ryu refuses to enable the item and says why. Buy or renew the item to enable it again. **My licenses** shows the same status.

If Ryu cannot reach the Marketplace or you are signed out, it uses the last license status it confirmed. A paid item it last confirmed as licensed can be enabled for up to 7 days after that check; after that, reconnect and sign in to enable it.

Ryu also re-checks the licenses of paid items that are already enabled, every few hours and again shortly after it reconnects. If a purchase is refunded or disputed, or a license has expired, Ryu disables the item the same way **Disable** does: its background services stop and its hooks and tools are removed. The reason appears in your activity feed and as a notification. A lost connection alone never disables an item: the same 7-day window from the last confirmed check applies, and an item is disabled only once that window has passed without a successful check. Buy, renew, or reconnect, then enable the item again.

## Manage a subscription license

On the web, an organization's licenses are also listed under **Organization Settings → Marketplace & payouts**. Choose **Manage** on a subscription row in **My licenses** to open the license's page in the organization console. It shows the item, its plan, and its renewal: the next renewal date, or the date it ends.

- **Cancel renewal** asks you to confirm, then stops future charges. The license keeps working until the end of the paid period, then stops. **Keep renewing** leaves it unchanged.
- **Resume renewal** appears while a cancelled renewal has not ended yet, and turns renewal back on.

Licenses belong to the organization that bought them, so switch to that organization first. The member who bought a license can cancel or resume its renewal, as can anyone with billing permission in that organization; either way Ryu asks you to confirm it's you. A renewal cancelled in Stripe directly also shows as cancelled. One-time licenses don't renew and have no renewal to manage.

## Buy through a creator campaign

Creators can share a campaign link that opens the web Marketplace with creator context attached. A campaign may add a buyer credit bonus after payment and may record a creator commission. Any support window and its duration are shown with the campaign.

Campaign attribution does not change the listing price, seller, license, or payout terms. One purchase is attributed to one campaign creator.

## Read the health report before installing

Listing details show a **Health** score when the listing provides enough evidence to review. The report may check the declared UI against Ryu's design system, including shared controls, semantic colors, typography, accessibility states, and reduced motion. Missing evidence is shown as unavailable and does not count as a pass.

The score is a review aid, not a safety guarantee. Installation still checks the publisher signature and permissions.

Choose **Audit with agent** for an optional assessment of the listing. It returns evidence, confidence, recommendations, and limitations. It does not install the item or make repairs. No audit runs automatically; use **Open audit conversation** to review or continue it, and **Audit again** to start a fresh assessment.

## Node lifecycle access

Installing, updating, enabling, disabling, and uninstalling are separate permissions on each node. Organization owners and administrators have the default lifecycle access on organization and team nodes. A personal node is managed by its owner. Other members may need a team, role, or individual grant.

An individual deny takes priority over a team grant. Organization administration does not override ownership of a personal node. If an action is unavailable, ask an organization administrator to review access for that node.

## The credits wallet

Credits are an organization balance for first-party managed inference. The **Credits** and **Usage** surfaces show the active organization beside its plan, balance, recent transactions, and usage summaries. Switching organizations reloads the balance, grant pools, and usage statement. They are never personal balances.

Ryu-managed models can use the organization's unrestricted prepaid credits without a managed plan.
Provider-restricted grants apply only to their matching credit pools. Use **Add prepaid credits**
in the provider settings to open the organization's Credits view.

Top-up presets are $100, $250, and $1,000, or a custom amount from $5 to $1,000. You pay the face
value plus a deposit fee, and the wallet receives the face value. The desktop shows the fee
breakdown before checkout. Payment runs through Polar. A server webhook credits the wallet, so
refresh the wallet or return to the page to see the new balance. The wallet is credited only after
the paid order covers the full quoted charge, including the deposit fee. A discount or reduced
checkout amount below that total adds no credits.

<Callout type="info">
  Only organization owners and administrators can add credits, and the server enforces this. Other members can view the balance. Paid third-party Marketplace items are bought with a card through their listing checkout, not with Ryu credits.
</Callout>

Provider balances for keys such as OpenRouter, DeepSeek, or Moonshot are held by those providers on the current node and are separate from the Ryu credits wallet (see [Gateway usage and cost](/docs/surfaces/desktop/user-guide/gateway-usage-cost)). The Usage surface charts credit spend by usage type, model, and provider; top-ups and grants appear in the transaction list but are not counted as spend.

<Callout type="warn">
  Gateway debits managed-inference usage from the wallet only on installs configured for metered
  billing. Top-ups, paid Marketplace purchases, and licenses are separate.
</Callout>

## Listing details and reviews

Opening a screenshot keeps the listing underneath it. Press **Escape** to close the screenshot
viewer and return to the expanded listing; a second **Escape** closes the listing.

**More details** keeps reviews available even when a listing has no optional setup or publisher
metadata. The **Reviews** tab shows ratings and reviews from the review service. If it cannot load
them, it shows an error with **Try again** instead of claiming the item has no reviews. If loading
another page fails, the reviews already loaded stay visible; use **Load more reviews** to retry.
Review authoring waits for that read to finish. Rejected writes keep your star rating and text
available for retry. Signed-out visitors can read reviews, and paid-item review eligibility is
checked by the Marketplace service.
