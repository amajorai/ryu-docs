Your profile and stats live in desktop **Settings**. Your public profile page is covered in
[Public Profiles and Agents](/docs/surfaces/public-profiles).

## Profile

**Settings → Profile** holds your profile photo, display name, and an **About you** block (nickname,
occupation, and notes about you and your organization). About you is saved on the current node and added
to agent context there; it is not shared with your organization.

When you select another node, the fields show that node's own details. A node whose saved details
cannot be read starts with empty fields rather than the previous node's.

About you is part of every thread's system prompt, so saving a change first asks you to confirm.
Each thread's next message is sent once at the full input price instead of from the prompt cache.
Saving unchanged details does not ask.

## Stats

**Settings → Stats** shows your level and streak, an activity heatmap, lifetime totals, the features you
can unlock with points, and a per-agent view of your team's usage.

**Share your Ryu Wrapped** opens your
public Wrapped card in the browser, where **Video or GIF** exports the card as a still image, a
ten-second looping video, or a looping GIF.

**Share as clip** turns the activity heatmap into an eight-second animation. Your last 12 months fill
in day by day, then your total tokens, active days, streak, and level appear beneath it.

1. Pick a square (1:1), story (9:16), or wide (16:9) frame and preview it.
2. Choose **Download video** (MP4, or WebM where MP4 recording isn't available) or **Download GIF**.

The clip is rendered on your device, so keep the window in the foreground until the download
finishes. Closing the dialog cancels it.

Your streak counts consecutive UTC days on which you or your agents used Ryu. See
[Streaks](/docs/surfaces/public-profiles#streaks) for what counts. Hover or focus the streak badge
to see this definition.

To stop chats, agent runs, and usage on a device from counting, turn off **Sync activity on this
device to your profile** in **Settings → Privacy**. [Privacy and Telemetry](/docs/surfaces/desktop/transparency#profile-activity-streak)
explains what this signal sends and how points are counted.

## Emails Ryu sent you

On the website, **Settings → Emails** lists the official emails Ryu sent to your account: receipts,
account notices, announcements, and marketing messages. Search by subject, filter by type, and select
a row to read the message as it was sent. Emails stay in this list for 12 months.

Emails that carried a one-time code or a sign-in, verification, reset, or invitation link show only
their subject. Ryu does not keep those codes and links, so use the copy in your inbox while it is still
valid. To stop announcement emails, turn off **Announcements** under email in **Settings →
Notifications**; marketing emails include an unsubscribe link.
