Pages in the desktop app open as tabs. This page shows how to arrange them. To change how tabs
look, see [Appearance](/docs/surfaces/desktop/user-guide/appearance) and
[Tab appearance](#tab-appearance).

## Keep pages open

Desktop keeps open pages mounted, so returning to a tab keeps its working state and scroll
position. Pages that need Core, a Companion UI, or a remote service can show a loading state while
that service responds. Background polling pauses while the window is unfocused or hidden. Links to
websites open in your browser, and detached windows stay within the desktop app.

## Split view

Tabs can be shown side by side. A **split view** places two or more tabs in the main content area at once, as either **columns** (side by side) or **rows** (stacked).

- Press **Ctrl+Alt+S** (or **Cmd+Alt+S**) to toggle a split view on the active tab.
- Tab context menus also create, drop a tab from, or dissolve a split.
- Drag a tab onto a pane edge to preview and create a split there. Dropping in the
  center of an existing split swaps the two panes.
- Drag the gutter between two panes to resize them.

Open **Split view → Save layout as preset** from a split's context menu to save its shape. Turn on
**Remember what each pane was showing** if the preset should reopen the same pages; leave it off to
save only the arrangement. **Apply layout preset** re-tiles the current panes when the pane counts
match, or opens a new set of panes when they do not. **Equalize panes** evens the sizes of an uneven
split.

Manage saved presets in **Settings → General → Tabs → Pane layout presets**. Use the rename or
delete controls on a saved row; double-clicking its name also opens the inline editor. Confirm a
rename with **Enter** or cancel it with **Escape**. A preset name must contain at least one
non-whitespace character; a blank rename stays in the editor so you can correct it or cancel.
Deleting a preset does not change panes already open.

You can save up to 60 pane layout presets. Saving with an existing name replaces that preset, including when all 60 slots are used. Names match without regard to case. Names are trimmed and limited to 64 characters when saved or renamed, so they stay consistent after reopening the workspace.

If storage refuses a save, rename, or deletion, Ryu shows an error and keeps your draft or deletion confirmation open. The saved collection remains unchanged until the write succeeds. Restore available storage and retry. A new name at the 60-layout limit keeps the save dialog open; replace an existing name or delete a saved layout first.

<Callout type="info">
  Panes are positioned independently, so switching focus between panes never reloads a page.
</Callout>

### Right and Bottom panes

Besides the main **Center** area, a page tab can live in a **Right** or **Bottom** pane. Right-click
a tab and choose **Move to pane**, then **Right** or **Bottom**. Each pane has its own tab strip,
where each tab shows the same icon as its page or tool, and a working chat shows a spinner. Its
**…** menu moves the active tab to another pane, hides the pane, or resets the layout. Moving a
tab never reloads it.

- In the Right or Bottom pane, **+** opens a new chat or a tool from any page: **Terminal**,
  **Files**, **Changes**, **Git graph**, and the panels your enabled apps add. The command palette
  offers the same tools as **Open Terminal**, **Open Files**, and so on. Opening a tool that is
  already open focuses it instead of adding a copy. Project tools belong to the project that was
  open when you added them, and show a notice instead of another project's files or terminal.
  When the focused Center tab is a saved chat, **+** also offers that chat's **Context**,
  **Sources**, and **Subagents**. Each of these tabs is named after its chat, keeps showing that
  chat's data wherever you move it, and says the chat is closed (instead of showing another chat)
  once you close the chat.
- Drag a tab onto another pane's tab strip to place it at that position, or onto the pane itself to
  add it at the end. While you drag, a hidden or empty **Right** or **Bottom** pane appears as a
  target at the window edge. Dropping a Right or Bottom tab in the middle of a Center pane moves it
  into that pane.
- Right-click a tab in a pane's strip to move it to another pane, split it **Right** or **Down**,
  close it, close the other tabs, or close the tabs to its right.
- Use the **Left** and **Right** arrow keys to move between tabs in a strip, and **Home** or **End**
  to jump to the first or last tab. Middle-click a tab to close it. If a strip has more tabs than
  fit, scroll it sideways; the active tab always scrolls into view.
- A hidden pane keeps its tabs. Use the **Toggle Bottom Panel** and **Toggle Right Panel**
  shortcuts, or the command palette's **Layout** actions, to show or hide it from any page,
  including a chat.
- In the Bottom pane's **…** menu, **Align Bottom** sets how wide it is: under **Center** only,
  **Full width**, or extended under the **Left** or **Right** side.
- Drag the edge of a pane to resize it, or focus the edge and use the arrow keys. Double-click the
  edge to restore the default size.
- When a split pane in Center has no tabs left, it offers **Add a view** and **Close pane**. When
  Center has more than one pane, its **…** menu also has **Close pane**. Closing a pane moves its tabs
  into the pane beside it, which also takes over its space.
- **Reset layout** returns every tab to its default place, joins Center back into one pane, and
  restores the default pane sizes. It does not close any tabs.

#### Chat panels in the panes

A chat uses the same Right and Bottom panes for its panels instead of docks of its own:

- The chat header's right-panel and bottom-panel buttons show or hide the Right and Bottom panes,
  and stay highlighted while the pane is showing. Showing an empty pane opens a tool there: a
  **Terminal** below, and **Files** at the right, or the chat's **Context** when no project is
  open.
- When a chat opens one of its panels, such as **Files** for a file search, **Changes** for a turn's
  edits, **Context**, **Sources**, **Subagents**, an artifact, the message inspector, or **Agent
  activity**, it opens as a tab in the Right pane, or focuses the tab that is already open. Focus
  stays in the chat, so you can keep typing.
- A chat's panels are named after the chat and keep showing that chat's data wherever you move
  them. Project tools such as **Terminal** and **Files** are shared by every chat in the project.
- A page or app panel opened in the side panel from elsewhere, such as a command or a link, also
  opens as a Right pane tab. A terminal that runs a command opens as a new **Terminal** tab in the
  pane chosen by **Settings → General → Default terminal location**.

Workspace tabs saved by earlier versions move into the panes once, without showing them. The
scrolling and **Infinite canvas** tab layouts have no panes, so there each chat keeps its own
bottom and right docks.

The command palette's **Layout** group runs the same actions by name: show or hide the Right or
Bottom pane, move the focused tab to another pane, align Bottom, **Equalize Center Panes**,
**Join Center Panes**, **Focus Next Pane** or **Focus Previous Pane**, and **Reset layout**. The
pane actions are also listed under **Layout** in **Settings → Keyboard Shortcuts**. They have no
shortcut by default, so assign the ones you use.

To keep navigation on the right, like moving an editor's side bar, run **Move Navigation to
Right** from the command palette. Navigation then becomes the first tab of the Right pane and the
left sidebar steps aside, so Center uses the full width. The sidebar shortcut (**Ctrl+B** or
**Cmd+B**) and the title bar's sidebar button show or hide that pane instead. Run **Move Navigation
to Left**, choose **Move to Left** from the Navigation tab's menu, or use **Reset layout** to bring
it back. Navigation cannot be closed. Phone-width windows always show navigation as the usual
slide-in sidebar.

When the window is too narrow to keep Center usable beside the Right pane, Right and Bottom open
as a sheet over Center instead, one at a time. A pane's sheet opens while one of its tabs has
focus: run **Focus Next Pane** or **Focus Previous Pane** to reach it, or use **Toggle Right
Panel** or **Toggle Bottom Panel** to open or close that pane's sheet, and click outside the sheet
to return to Center. Split Center panes show only the
focused pane until there is room again. Widening the window brings back your docked layout and
sizes unchanged.

Pane placement, sizes, and alignment are remembered when you reopen Ryu.

## Tab appearance

By default, open tabs live in a small searchable list in the title bar. Turn off
**Settings → Appearance → Interface → Show tabs as a dropdown** to show every tab in the title bar.
In that view, tabs use floating pills by default. Turn off **Settings → General → Tabs → Floating
tabs** to make the active tab use the page color instead. Pinned tabs, groups, drag-and-drop, and
tab actions still work.

In dropdown mode, click the active-tab trigger to open **Search open tabs**. Type a
title or route to narrow the list, select a result to switch to it, or use its close
button to close it. In full-strip mode, use the chevron beside the **+** button for
the same searchable list. Right-click the chevron to hide the button; restore it from
**Settings → Appearance → Interface → Show tab search button**.

The main tab and sidebar settings are also available in place. Right-click empty
sidebar space to change the sidebar mode or style, list grouping, project and Space
navigation, chat previews, section overflow, or agent-row presentation.

Choose **Customize sidebar** to show or hide individual buttons and sections,
including contributions from enabled apps. **All on** and **All off** apply to
every listed row; the group controls affect only top buttons, sections, or bottom
buttons. Drag the top-button and section rows to reorder each list; the other rows
slide aside to show where the dragged row will land. Changes apply
immediately and remain saved when you close the dialog. **Reset to default**
restores the default order, visibility, section page sizes, and sorts.
Right-click the title-bar tab area to toggle the tab dropdown, tab search button,
floating tab pills, or other title-bar behavior. These context-menu changes update the
same saved Appearance preferences as Settings.

### Move a tab to its own window

In Desktop, drag a tab from the title bar or vertical tab list beyond the edge of
its window and release. Ryu moves that tab into a separate window, with the sidebar
closed and the other tabs left in the original window. A tab in a group or split
moves on its own; the remaining group or panes stay together.

You can also right-click a tab and choose **Move tab to new window**. **Open in new
window** keeps the original tab open as a separate copy. Moving waits for the new
window to become ready; if it cannot open, your source tab stays in place. Dropping
inside the original window keeps the existing reorder and split gestures. Drag
along the tab strip to reorder: the neighbouring tabs slide aside to open a gap
where the tab will land. Move into the content area and drop at a pane edge to
create a split. The content drop targets leave the tab strip available throughout
the drag.

The moved tab keeps its selected node, unsent chat text and attachments, model and
agent selections, workspace dock state, and workspace artifact content. Save a
temporary chat with existing messages before moving it, since its history belongs
to its current window. Native tab tear-out is available in Desktop.

### Center-pane tab views

Right-click any tab and choose **Tab view** to switch between four ways of working:

- **Horizontal tabs** keeps the compact title-bar strip.
- **Vertical tabs** moves the open-tab list into the sidebar.
- **Scrollable tabs** turns the center pane into a long horizontal track of live
  tab cards. Scroll or use the arrow keys to move between focused and unfocused
  tabs. Focus a card itself before using Left or Right; arrow keys inside page
  inputs retain their normal caret behavior. Right-clicking a card opens its menu
  without activating it; choose **Focus tab** to switch to it and dismiss the menu.
- **Infinite canvas** turns every open tab into a live, draggable canvas pane.
  Existing tab groups and split groups appear as colored regions around their
  member panes; pan, zoom, resize, and use **Fit all** to arrange the view.

The tab and split groups stay the same when you change views. An unloaded tab keeps
its memory-saving state and reloads when you focus it. On a narrow window, Ryu keeps
the normal horizontal tab strip so the active page remains easy to reach.

Desktop unloads inactive tab trees after **10 minutes** by default to keep long-lived workspaces
responsive. Change this in **Settings → General → Tabs → Unload inactive tabs**; **Never** keeps
every tab mounted. This does not delete a conversation or its transcript. It only releases the
inactive tab's in-memory state, and focusing the tab loads the durable transcript again. Active,
pinned, split-visible, and running tabs are never unloaded.

In the normal title-bar strip, double-click a colored tab-group pill to rename the
group inline. Double-click a saved pane-layout preset's name in **Settings → General
→ Tabs** to edit that name inline. Press **Enter** to commit either rename or
**Esc** to cancel.

For a more focused browsing mode, turn on **Settings → Appearance → Interface → Blur
popup backgrounds**. While enabled, dropdowns, selects, popovers, context menus, and
navigation menus softly dim and blur the app behind the active surface. It is off by
default and does not change full dialogs; **Blur dialog backgrounds** remains the
separate control for dialogs, action dialogs, sheets, and drawers.

## Renaming tabs

Double-click a tab's title to rename it inline. In the normal title-bar strip, the
same gesture also renames a colored tab-group pill. The rename applies where the tab
is named for something you own:

- **Chats** rename the conversation (also shown in the sidebar).
- **Space pages** - documents, databases, and app-owned pages - rename the page itself.

Tabs whose title comes from a route (Settings, Library, the store) have nothing to
rename, so a double-click just activates the tab. Commit with **Enter**, cancel with **Esc**.
