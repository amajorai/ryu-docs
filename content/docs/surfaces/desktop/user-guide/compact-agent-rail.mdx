---
title: "Agent Rail in Narrow Windows"
description: "In narrow windows and on phones, the collapsed sidebar becomes a column of agent avatars."
---

When the window is narrower than 1024 pixels, a collapsed sidebar becomes a slim
column of agent avatars along the left edge, called the agent rail. This works the way
a messaging app keeps your contacts in reach. It applies to a narrow desktop window and
to Ryu in a phone or tablet browser. The chat keeps most of the width, and each agent is
still one tap away. For the full sidebar, see the
[Desktop user guide](/docs/surfaces/desktop/user-guide).

## What the rail shows

- **Pinned agents** come first, in the order you pinned them. A divider separates them
  from your other agents. Pin or unpin an agent from its right-click menu in the rail or
  in the sidebar.
- **Your other agents** follow in the same order as in the sidebar. The rail scrolls
  when you have more agents than fit.
- A marker at the window edge and a highlighted tile show the agent of the chat you have
  open.
- A dot on an avatar means that agent has an unread direct chat.

Select an avatar to open that agent's conversation. Select it again to return to the
same tab instead of starting another chat. Hover an avatar to see the agent's name.
Right-click it, or touch and hold on a touch screen, to choose **Edit agent**, **Pin
agent**, or **Unpin agent**.

At the bottom of the rail:

- **+** opens **New agent**.
- **Search** opens the command palette, as the title bar's search button does. It is not
  shown in [Bot](/docs/surfaces/desktop/bot).
- Your **account picture** opens the full sidebar, where you'll find the account menu,
  every section, and the rest of navigation.

## Opening and closing the full sidebar

The sidebar shortcut (**Ctrl+B** or **Cmd+B**) and your account picture open the full
sidebar. The rail hides while the sidebar is open and comes back when you close it.

If the sidebar is docked when the window narrows past 1024 pixels, Ryu collapses it
into the rail. If you widen the window again without having reopened the sidebar, Ryu
docks it again. A sidebar you closed yourself in a wide window stays closed. Wide
windows never show the rail.

The rail does not appear in a tab you moved into its own window, or in the browser
extension's side panel.

## Turning the rail off

Right-click empty space in the rail and clear **Agent rail in narrow windows**. A
collapsed sidebar in a narrow window then hides completely, as it does in a wide window,
and the navigation buttons, including the sidebar button, return to the top-left corner.
Right-click those buttons and select **Agent rail in narrow windows** to turn the rail
back on. Outside Bot, the same item is also in the menu you get when you right-click
empty sidebar space. This setting is saved on this device.
