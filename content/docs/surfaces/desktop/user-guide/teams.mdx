title: "Agent Groups"
description: "Address several agents as one unit with a coordination strategy."
---

A **group** lets several agents work on one turn. Ryu sends your message to the members and combines
their replies into one answer.

## Create a group

Groups live in their own **Groups** section in the sidebar.

1. Open **Groups** from the sidebar.
2. Select **New group** in the Groups Companion and give it a name.
3. Pick the **coordination strategy** (see below).
4. Add members from the Companion's agent picker. For router or debate/synthesis, choose a lead
   agent as well.

The sidebar shows a live roster. You create groups and change members in the Companion UI.

At the top of Group settings, **Group at a glance** shows the roster around a table. Each seat
shows a member's name and engine, the lead is highlighted, and the center shows the coordination
strategy. The table shows the first six seats. The full ordered list is in **Members** below.

## Talk to a group

There are two ways to direct a turn to a group:

- **Composer target picker** - click the agent picker in the composer and select a group. Groups
  appear alongside agents. The group stays the target for the conversation.
- **`@mention`** - type `@groupname` in the composer to mention a group once in a conversation
  with a single agent.

The members' replies arrive as one streamed answer. Each block starts with the member's name, so you
can see who said what. The combined reply is attributed to the group. For the request fields and
routing, see [Agent groups](/docs/core/agent-teams).

## Coordination strategies

Four strategies are built in:

- **Broadcast** - every member answers the turn independently. No member sees another's reply.
  This is the default.
- **Round-robin** - each member answers in order, seeing the prior members' replies folded into
  its prompt.
- **Debate / synthesis** - members answer independently (round one), then the lead agent reads
  all replies and produces one merged answer.
- **Router** - the lead agent classifies the turn and routes it to a single best-suited member.

For debate/synthesis and router, the lead is the agent you chose as lead. Without one, the first
member leads.

<Callout type="info">
  Council chat (`@mention` of individual agents in one thread) and groups are related but distinct.
  Council chat targets agents one at a time, while a group is a named unit with a fixed roster and a
  strategy.
</Callout>

## Expose a group via a channel bot

A group can answer a channel bot (Telegram, Slack, WhatsApp, Discord) configured in **Gateway
settings**. In the channel dialog, the **Routes to** picker lists agents and groups. When a channel
routes to a group, each inbound message goes to the group's members with the same coordination
strategy, and the bot sends one combined reply. Each sender gets a persistent conversation, so
history, memory, and tools apply as they do in the desktop.

See [Channel Bots](/docs/gateway/channels) for full setup instructions.
