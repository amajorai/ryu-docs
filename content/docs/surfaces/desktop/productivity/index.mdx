---
title: "Desktop Productivity"
description: "Overview of the desktop features that work in the background - meetings, monitors, recorded automations, predictive typing, and the Timeline activity history."
---

These Desktop features work in the background. They take meeting notes, watch websites for
changes, and replay recorded desktop actions as workflow steps.

The [Timeline](/docs/apps/timeline) app adds a visual history of your screen and activity, with
the nearest screenshot shown at the playhead.

<Callout type="info">
  Meeting capture relies on the Shadow sensor and desktop recording relies on Ghost, so both run on
  the device, not on a remote node. Platform support for meeting capture differs. See the caveat on
  [Meetings](/docs/surfaces/desktop/productivity/meetings#automatic-detection). Notes, monitors, and
  workflows work anywhere Core runs.
</Callout>
