---
title: "Recorded Automations"
description: "Record a desktop task once and replay it as steps inside a workflow."
---

Ryu can record a desktop task once (your clicks, typing, and scrolls) and turn it into workflow
steps that replay the same actions each time.

## Record a task

Recording lives in the **Workflows** surface.

1. Open **Workflows** and click **Record a task**.
2. Type what you are doing under **What are you doing?**, then click **Start recording** and perform
   the task on screen.
3. Click **Stop & build workflow**. Ryu turns the captured actions into a workflow you can edit: an
   `Input` node, one `ghost_action` node per action, and an `Output` node.

Each recorded action becomes a step node in the workflow's DAG, so a recorded automation is driven
by upstream data like any other workflow step. See [Workflows](/docs/core/workflows) for how to wire
nodes together.

<Callout type="warn">
  Recording and replay need the Ghost desktop-automation sidecar installed, and replay needs a
  display. Ghost captures input on Windows, macOS (an event tap), and Linux (evdev). On Linux,
  capture needs read access to `/dev/input/event*` (root or the `input` group), and pointer
  positions resolve only under X11 or XWayland (`crates/ghost/eyes/src/input.rs`). Without the
  sidecar, recording and replay report an error.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  Recording uses Ghost's recipe engine. A saved recipe can also replay as a single `Recipe` node,
  in addition to the per-action step nodes that an in-canvas recording produces.
</Callout>

When a recording drives the active node's desktop, the Virtual Desktop live-media card shows that
same screen. Use **Open PiP** to keep the card on top of other windows while you record, or
**Expand** to view the current frame full screen. The frame returns to the small card when you close
the full-screen view.
