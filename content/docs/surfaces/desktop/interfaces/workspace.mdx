## More layouts

These built-in layouts also use your color theme from **Settings → Appearance**:

| Interface | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Board** | Conversations as Kanban cards or a dense list, grouped by status, agent, or project, with filters and a command menu. See [Board](#board) below. |
| **Inbox** | Every conversation grouped by what needs you, most urgent first, in a keyboard-first list. On a wide window the top conversation opens in the reading pane when Inbox loads; close it and the pane previews the selected conversation instead. |
| **Graph** | A live map of who works with whom: you at the center, your agents around you, and teams and projects beside their members. |
| **Timeline** | Conversations across days and weeks: a day-by-day list, or a seven-day week grid placed at each conversation's latest activity. |
| **Messenger** | Agents, and teams as group chats, as contacts in a desktop messaging app, one chat each. In a wide window it opens your most recent conversation. |
| **Terminal** | A keyboard-first command line over your agents and conversations, with the open conversation beside the scrollback. |
| **Arena** | A wall of live agent cards, working agents first, with live totals and filters. |
| **Desk** | A help-desk inbox: views by agent, team, and project, the view's conversations, and the open conversation with a details sidebar. On a wide window the view's first conversation opens when Desk loads; with nothing open, the pane shows your queue: counts by status and the next conversation that needs you. |

Board, Inbox, and Arena sort conversations by the same statuses: **Needs attention** (the last run
failed or was interrupted), **Your turn** (an agent replied), **Working**, **Waiting on agent** (you
wrote last), and **Not started**.

## Board

Board shows every conversation in groups. Open **Display** in the header to change how:

- **Grouping** by **Status**, **Agent**, or **Project** picks the groups. By status, the most urgent group comes
  first. By agent or project, the **+** on a group starts a new conversation with that agent or in
  that project. Conversations without one land in **Unassigned** or **No project**.
- **List** and **Board** switch between a dense list, one row per conversation in
  collapsible sections, and Kanban columns of cards. Cards and rows show the latest message,
  the agents involved, the message count, and how long ago the conversation changed. Select a
  section heading in the list to collapse or expand it.
- **Filter** narrows the board by status, agent, or team or project. Active filters appear as chips
  under the header; remove one with its **×**, or choose **Clear filters**.

Open a conversation and it appears in a panel beside the board.

| Action | Keys |
| --- | --- |
| Open the command menu: jump to a conversation, start one, switch the view or grouping, filter | `Cmd+Shift+K` on macOS, `Ctrl+Shift+K` elsewhere |
| Start a new conversation | `C` |
| Move between rows in the list | `J` / `K` or `↑` / `↓`; `Home` and `End` jump to the ends |
| Move between cards on the board | Arrow keys |
| Open the selected conversation | `Enter` |
| Close the open conversation | `Esc` |

`Cmd+Shift+K` (`Ctrl+Shift+K`) opens the Board's command menu while you are on the board, not
inside an open conversation. `Cmd+K` (`Ctrl+K`) opens the app's command palette, on the board too.

In **Inbox** and **Desk**, press `J` / `K` (or the arrow keys) to move through conversations,
`Enter` to open one, and `Esc` to close it.

**Terminal** starts with a summary of what needs you and your agents, with the prompt at the bottom
and a status line under it. It understands these commands:

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `ls [filter]` | List conversations, numbered. Filters: `all`, `working`, `your-turn`, `waiting`, `attention`. |
| `open <n>` | Open a conversation by its list number or id. |
| `new [@agent] <message>` | Start a conversation and send the message, optionally to one agent. |
| `@agent <message>` | Shorthand for `new`. |
| `agents` | List agents and what they are doing. |
| `close` | Close the open conversation. |
| `clear` | Clear the screen (or press `Ctrl+L`). |
| `help` | Show the commands. |

Use `↑` / `↓` for command history and `Tab` to complete commands, `@` names, and conversations.
`Ctrl+U` clears the line, `Ctrl+C` cancels it, and `Esc` closes the open conversation.

## More interfaces

These interfaces also follow your color theme. The Card Table's felt and chips are mixed from its
colors. The Aquarium is a sunlit room in light themes and a dim room lit by the glowing tank in dark
ones, with the water leaning toward your theme's colors. Planets floats in deep space in dark themes and a pale,
day-side sky in light ones, and its moons glow in your theme's status colors.

| Interface | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Card Table** | Your agents seated around a card table, with the conversations that need you dealt to you as a hand of cards. |
| **Sheet** | Every conversation as a row in a spreadsheet grid you can sort, resize, search, and filter by status. |
| **Canvas** | Conversations as sticky notes on an infinite, zoomable board that you arrange yourself. |
| **Calendar** | Conversations and scheduled runs on a month or a week. |
| **Notebook** | Conversations as pages in a notebook, filed under their project, team, or agent. |
| **Mission Control** | A live wall for a second screen: status, totals, alerts, hourly activity, and scheduled runs. |
| **Ticker** | Your agents as a market: a watchlist of tickers, a price chart per agent, and a tape of the newest messages. |
| **Focus** | One conversation at a time, most urgent first, until you are caught up. |
| **Aquarium** | A big glass aquarium in a cosy room, in which each agent swims as its own sea creature. |
| **Planets** | A toon solar system: you are the star, each agent is a little planet with its cottage and villager on top, and its conversations are moons. |

Each of them opens a conversation in the normal chat view without leaving the interface.
**Card Table**, **Ticker**, **Aquarium**, and **Planets** are described in
[Playful Views](/docs/surfaces/desktop/interfaces/playful).

**Calendar** and **Mission Control** also show scheduled runs from your
[routines](/docs/surfaces/desktop/user-guide/agents#set-up-agent-routines) and scheduled workflows:
runs that already happened, with whether they succeeded or failed, and runs still to come, within
about six weeks of today. The list refreshes every minute. A few others show the runs still to come
as well: the **Next deal** on the Card Table's felt, the **Ticker**'s upcoming runs and **Next run**
figure, and the **Upcoming runs** card on the **Notebook**'s Home page. Many other interfaces use
the same runs too, such as the **Dashboard**'s
upcoming-runs widget, the **Status** page's scheduled maintenance, and the schedules in
**Departures**, **Rides**, **Hologram**, **Journal**, **Passport**, and **Town**.

## Sheet

Every conversation is a row, with **Title**, **Status**, **Agents**, **Team**, **Project**,
**Messages**, **Last message**, **Updated**, and **Pinned** columns. The row numbers and the Title
column stay in place while the rest scrolls sideways.

- Select a column header to sort by it: once ascending, again descending, a third time off. A chip
  in the toolbar names the sort and its direction.
- Drag the edge of a column header to resize the column.
- **Search threads** keeps the rows whose title, last message, team, project, or agent names
  contain every word you type, and highlights the matches.
- The tabs along the bottom narrow the rows to one status, with a count for each. The footer also
  totals the messages and pinned conversations.
- **New thread** starts a blank conversation; its menu starts one with an agent, in a team, or in a
  project.

Double-click a row, or press `Enter`, to open its conversation beside the grid. While one is open,
selecting another row, with a click or with the keyboard, opens that row instead, like flipping
through records.

| Action | Keys |
| --- | --- |
| Move between cells | Arrow keys; `Home` / `End` jump to the ends of the row |
| Jump to the first or last cell | `Ctrl+Home` / `Ctrl+End` (`Cmd` on macOS) |
| Move a page of rows | `Page Up` / `Page Down` |
| Open the row's conversation, or sort by a header | `Enter` or `Space` |
| Resize the column under the cursor (on a header) | `Alt+←` / `Alt+→` |
| Search | `/`; then `↓` or `Enter` returns to the grid and `Esc` clears the search |
| Previous or next conversation (from the open conversation's header) | `Alt+↑` / `Alt+↓` |
| Close the open conversation | `Esc` |

## Canvas

Conversations start out filed into labelled frames, one per status or one per agent; switch with
**Arrange by** **Status** or **Agent** at the top. From there, drag the stickies wherever you like.
The canvas remembers where you put them on this device, separately for each arrangement, and
**Reset layout** puts them back in their frames.

- Pan by dragging empty space, holding `Space` and dragging, using the **Hand** tool, or scrolling.
  Zoom with `Ctrl` (`Cmd` on macOS) and the scroll wheel, a pinch, the dock's buttons, or the zoom
  menu.
- The minimap in the corner shows the whole board. Click or drag in it to move the view, or hide it.
- Double-click a sticky, or select it and press `Enter`, to open its conversation beside the canvas.
- Double-click empty space to write a new conversation right there: choose an agent if you want
  one (in the Agent arrangement, double-clicking inside an agent's frame picks that agent), type,
  and press `Enter` to send. The new sticky lands where you double-clicked.

| Action | Keys |
| --- | --- |
| Select tool / Hand tool | `V` / `H` |
| New conversation in the middle of the view | `N` |
| Pan | `Space` and drag, or the arrow keys when nothing is selected |
| Move the selected sticky | Arrow keys (`Shift` for bigger steps) |
| Open the selected sticky | `Enter` |
| Zoom in / out | `+` / `-` |
| Zoom to 100% | `Shift+0`, or `Ctrl+0` (`Cmd+0` on macOS) |
| Zoom to fit / to the selection | `Shift+1` / `Shift+2` |
| Close the composer, then the open conversation, then the selection | `Esc` |

## Calendar

Conversations sit on the day (and, in the week, the hour) of their latest activity, and scheduled
runs at their start time, both placed in the display time zone you choose in **Settings**. Switch between **Month** and **Week** in the header, step with the arrows
beside them, and choose **Today** to come back.

- In the month, a busy day ends with **+N more**, which lists everything on that day. Select a
  date number to see that week.
- In the week, items that overlap share their day side by side, or stack when the column is too
  narrow. A line marks the current time, and a day with items above or below the visible hours shows
  how many are **earlier** or **later**. A schedule that fires many times a day shows as a single
  marker with its count.
- The sidebar has a month picker, the **Upcoming runs**, and a **Show** list to hide or show each
  status and the scheduled runs. Hide it with the sidebar button; on narrow windows it slides over
  the calendar.
- Opening a conversation shows it beside the calendar, with a button to show its day. Opening a run
  shows its schedule, time, agent, and outcome (for example **Succeeded**, **Failed**, or
  **Scheduled**), and, when the run belongs to an agent, a **Message** button that starts a
  conversation with that agent.

| Action | Keys |
| --- | --- |
| Move between days | `←` / `→`; `↑` / `↓` move a week |
| Start or end of the week | `Home` / `End` |
| Previous or next month (or week) | `Page Up` / `Page Down` |
| Open the first item on the selected day | `Enter` |
| Go to today | `T` |
| Switch to Month / Week | `M` / `W` |
| Close the open conversation or the sidebar overlay | `Esc` |

## Notebook

A notebook workspace in which every conversation is a page. The sidebar files each page under its
project, else its team, else its lead agent, else **Unfiled**, and pinned pages also appear under
**Favorites**. Every agent has a row, even before it has pages, so its **+** can start one with
that agent.

With no page open, **Home** greets you with your **Recently edited** pages and the pages that
**Needs you**. A page shows its properties (status, agents, team, project, messages, the last run's
outcome, and when it was last edited) above the conversation, which is the page's body. Select a
crumb in the breadcrumb to find that parent in the sidebar. **Search** in the sidebar narrows it to
matching pages. **Close page** or **Home** returns to Home.

| Action | Keys |
| --- | --- |
| Move through the sidebar | `↑` / `↓`; `Home` and `End` jump to the ends |
| Expand or collapse a row | `→` / `←` |
| Open a page, or fold a parent | `Enter` or `Space` |
| New page under the selected parent | `N` |
| Show or hide the sidebar | `Cmd+\` on macOS, `Ctrl+\` elsewhere |
| Jump to Search | `Cmd+Shift+F` on macOS, `Ctrl+Shift+F` elsewhere |

## Mission Control

A wall meant to stay open on a second screen:

- A clock with the date and your time zone, and a status banner. It reads **All systems nominal**,
  or how many failures need attention, with **Review alerts** to jump to them.
- Six tiles, **Working**, **Needs attention**, **Your turn**, **Waiting on agent**, **Agents
  active**, and **Updated today**, each with its trend over the last 24 hours.
- **Agent status**: one station per agent with what it is doing (**Working**, **Your turn**,
  **Queued**, **Failed**, or **Idle**) and its current conversation. Select a station to open
  that conversation, or its message button to start a new one with the agent.
- **Alerts**: failed or interrupted runs and failed scheduled runs, then the conversations waiting on
  your reply.
- **Activity**: conversations updated per hour over the last 24 hours, with each hour's scheduled
  runs marked as succeeded or failed. Select an hour to open its newest conversation.
- **Scheduled runs**: the next scheduled runs under **Upcoming**, and the recent ones with how they
  went under **Recent**.
- A tape of the latest updates.

Any conversation opens in a panel that slides over the wall; `Esc` closes it. The **New thread**
button in the header starts a conversation.

## Focus

One conversation at a time, in a single card: its status, title, agents, and latest message. The
queue runs from the most urgent (failed runs, then replies waiting on you) through running
conversations, those waiting on an agent, and those not started; pinned ones lead their group.
**Up next** previews the following card (select it to step forward), and the strip at the top shows
the whole queue and how many are left. The keys are listed under the card.

**Open thread** shows the conversation in the normal chat view; stepping forward or back from there opens
the next one directly, and **Back to queue** returns to the card. At the end of the queue, a
**You're caught up** card has a box to start something new.

| Action | Keys |
| --- | --- |
| Next / previous conversation | `J` or `→` / `K` or `←` |
| First / last conversation | `Home` / `End` |
| Open the current conversation | `Enter` or `O` |
| Back to the queue | `Esc` |
| Move along the queue strip (when it has focus) | `←` / `→`; `Home` and `End` jump to the ends |
