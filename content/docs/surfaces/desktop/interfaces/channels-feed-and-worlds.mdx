---

These built-in interfaces show your agents and conversations as chat channels, a feed, or a world.

## Channels

A team-chat workspace, laid out like a typical work chat app. Each agent is a **direct message**, each team is a
**#channel**, and a project gets a #channel once it has conversations. Conversations without a
team, project, or agent land in **#general**. Channels draws in your color theme from
**Settings → Appearance**, light or dark, so it matches the rest of the app.

The sidebar groups channels under **Channels** and **Direct messages**; select a section heading to
collapse it. A collapsed section still shows the channel you are in and any channel with a
conversation running. Running work shows as a count beside the channel, and a working agent's
avatar shows a green presence dot. **Home** in the left rail expands every section again.

A channel lists its conversations as messages, newest at the bottom, each with its reply count and
when it last changed. Select the reply count, or **Reply in thread** on the message, to open the
conversation in the **Thread** pane on the right, in the normal chat view where you read and reply;
close the pane with its close button. Switching channels closes the open thread. The **Pinned** tab
next to **Messages** lists only the channel's pinned conversations, and the member avatars in a
channel's header list its agents with a button to message each one.

Type in the box at the bottom of a channel to start a new conversation there: in a direct message
it goes to that agent, in a team channel to the team, and in #general or a project channel you pick
the agent first. The compose button next to the workspace name starts a new conversation without
picking an agent.

The **Search Ryu** box at the top narrows the sidebar to channels whose name or messages match and
the open channel to its matching messages. When the open channel has no matches, it lists the
channels that do.

## Feed

A social timeline in which every conversation is a **post** and its messages are the **replies**.
A post shows its title, the newest message, the reply count, and a **Live** marker while the
conversation is running. Pinned conversations stay at the top. Like Channels, the Feed follows your
color theme from **Settings → Appearance**.

- **Home** has two tabs: **For you** shows every post, and **Needs you** shows only the
  conversations waiting on your reply or needing your attention: an agent's reply, or a failed or
  interrupted run.
- The navigation rail also has **Working** (with a count of running conversations), **Pinned**,
  and **Agents**, the list of your agents with a **Message** button for each. On narrower windows
  **Search** moves into the rail, and on the narrowest the rail becomes a bar at the bottom.
- Type in **What should your agents do?** and choose **Post** to start a new conversation; you can
  pick which agent receives it. The **Post** button in the rail jumps to that box.
- The **Search** box filters the timeline by title and message text as you type.
- **What's happening** lists the conversations running right now, and the **Agents** card lets you
  message an agent directly.

Open a post to see it on its own page with its replies below it, where you read and reply as in
the normal chat view. The back arrow (**Back to feed**) returns to the timeline where you left it.
On the timeline, `J` and `K` move between posts.

## World 2D

A cozy pixel-art farming village, drawn like a classic top-down life-sim game. Every agent has a cottage and stands at
its door, and agents that are working show a speech bubble. The village name sits in a wooden
plaque in the top-left corner, and the clock box in the top-right shows your local day and time
with a count of working agents.

Along the bottom, the **hotbar** has a portrait slot for each agent. Select a slot, or press its
number key (`1` to `9`, then `0`), to walk straight to that agent and talk. When you have more
agents than fit, the hotbar's arrow buttons (or `[` and `]`) turn the page. The `?` slot
shows or hides the controls, and the map slot (or `M`) opens a small village map that marks you and
the agents who are working.

Walk up to an agent and talk to it: a dialogue box opens beside the village with the agent's
portrait, a greeting, its description and status, its past conversations as dialogue choices, and
a reply box. Type in the reply box and choose **Say it** (or press `Enter`) to start a new
conversation. Open a past conversation to read and reply to it inside the dialogue box;
**Back to conversations** returns to the list, and **Walk away** closes it.

An agent that asks you something walks to the queue by your doorstep, on the free tiles nearest
it (down your column on a narrow window), and waits there; next to it, `E` opens its ask card, and its hotbar slot comes
first and answers the ask. The rest of what agents do is drawn over them, as described in
[How the games show what your agents are doing](/docs/surfaces/desktop/interfaces/games#how-the-games-show-what-your-agents-are-doing).

## World 3D

The same village as a cel-shaded 3D scene in the style of cozy island life-sim games: cottages,
paths, and trees on an island whose ground curves away toward the horizon, with outlined,
anime-style villagers. It uses the same layout as World 2D, so an agent's cottage stands in the
same place in both worlds.

- A round **minimap** in the top-left corner is centered on you, with a compass, the cottages in
  their roof colors, working agents ringed in green, and an arrow showing which way you face.
- Under it, a tracker names the agent whose ask needs you first and what it asks. Select it to
  open that ask's card. When no agent's ask is waiting, it says nothing needs you, or points
  you to the queue for asks no agent owns.
- Name plates float over the agents, and a working agent shows a thought bubble. The signs at
  an agent's station hide while they would sit under the corner panels or behind a roof. When you are close
  enough, a **Talk to** prompt appears, or **Answer** when the agent is asking you something.
- An agent that asks you something walks to your doorstep and queues there beside a lantern
  that dims the longer it waits.
- Talking frames you and the agent together. The agent's name and its latest line appear along the
  bottom, and the choices on the right are **New conversation** (opens a box to start a new
  conversation), the agent's **Past conversations**, and **Walk away**. An open conversation
  appears in a panel on the right with **Back to conversations**.

The camera follows you. In the default view you can drag to look around and scroll to zoom; the
isometric variant keeps a fixed, angled view from above. If this device does not support
WebGL, the village is shown as a list of your agents, with what each is doing and the same ask
cards. Motion (wind, clouds, smoke, and camera
moves) is reduced when your system asks for reduced motion.

## World controls

| Action | Keys |
| --- | --- |
| Walk | Arrow keys or `W` `A` `S` `D` |
| Talk to the agent next to you | `E`, `Enter`, or `Space` |
| Walk away from the conversation | `Esc` |
| Talk to any agent directly | Click the agent (2D) or its name plate (3D) |
| Visit an agent from the hotbar (2D) | `1` to `9`, `0`, or click its slot |
| Turn the hotbar page (2D) | `[` and `]` |
| Show or hide the village map (2D) | `M` |
| Show or hide the controls (2D) | `?` |
| Look around (3D) | Drag the scene |

Click or tab into the world first so it has keyboard focus. Cottages and agents block the way, so
you walk up to an agent rather than through it. Talking to a different agent closes the
conversation that was open and shows the new agent's conversations.
