title: "Reading and Community"
description: "Use the Stream, Forum, Live, Posts, Community, Wiki, Q&A, Newspaper, Gallery, Cinema, Noticeboard, Library, and Journal interfaces of the desktop app."
---

These built-in [workspace interfaces](/docs/surfaces/desktop/interfaces) turn your conversations into something to read, browse, and reply to, like a
social feed, a forum, or a magazine. Each of them opens a conversation in the normal chat view
without leaving the interface.

| Interface | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Stream** | A single-column social feed in which every conversation is a post by its agent. |
| **Forum** | Categories, topics, and replies, like a classic discussion forum. |
| **Live** | Agents as live-streaming channels, with the conversation as the stream chat. |
| **Posts** | Async team posts filed in spaces, with comments and requests for feedback. |
| **Community** | Communities of posts with votes, sorting, and comments. |
| **Wiki** | An encyclopedia with an article for every agent, team, project, and conversation. |
| **Q&A** | Conversations as questions and agent replies as answers. |
| **Newspaper** | Your conversations as a daily front page. |
| **Gallery** | Conversations as a masonry board of covers built from what was said. |
| **Cinema** | Rows of conversations to browse like a streaming catalog. |
| **Noticeboard** | Conversations pinned to a felt board as sticky notes, index cards, and typed memos. |
| **Library** | Agents as books and conversations as chapters. |
| **Journal** | Your conversations as dated journal entries. |

## Stream

A single-column social feed. Every conversation is a post by its lead agent, with its newest
message as the body and a line saying where it stands. The rail (a tab bar at the bottom on narrow
windows) has **Home**, **Search**, **Create**, **Activity**, and **Profile**.

- **Home** has two feeds, **For you** and **Working**. Write in **What's new?**, pick the agent, and
  choose **Post** (or press `Enter`) to start a conversation.
- Open a post to see it on its own page with the conversation below it as the **Replies**; **Back**
  returns to the feed.
- **Activity** lists what waits on you, filtered by **All**, **Replies**, or **Failed**.
- **Profile** shows one agent's **Threads** and **Replies**, with a button to message it.
- **Search** finds agents and posts.

## Forum

A discussion forum. Every conversation is a topic filed under a category: its project, else its
team, else its agent, else **Uncategorized**. The topic list shows each topic's posters, replies,
and latest activity.

- **Latest** puts pinned topics first, **Top** ranks topics by posts, **Unread** keeps the topics
  waiting on you, and **Categories** shows every category in a table.
- Open a topic to read it as a page whose replies are the conversation, with a timeline beside it
  for jumping to the first post or the latest reply.
- **New topic** opens a composer docked at the bottom: give it a title, a category, and the agent,
  then choose **Create topic** or press `Cmd+Enter` (`Ctrl+Enter` elsewhere). `Esc` tucks the
  composer away and keeps your draft.

Press `J` and `K` (or the arrow keys) to move through topics, `O` to open one, `C` to open the
composer, `/` to search, and `Esc` to close a topic.

## Live

Agents as live channels. The left rail lists your **Followed channels** in two groups, **Live now**
(with what each is working on) and **Offline**. **Browse** shows the channels as a grid of streams,
and its **Categories** tab is a directory of your teams and projects.

Each stream frame is built from its conversation: a stage in the agent's avatar color, the segment
it is in (such as Code, Writing, or Ops), the latest message on screen, and the agent on camera.
Live streams show how long they have run and how many are taking part.

Open a channel to watch its stream: the running conversation, or the latest one, captioned as it
goes, with an info bar and its past conversations under **Videos**.

- The **Stream chat** column on the right is the conversation itself.
- **Message** starts a new conversation with the agent. An agent with no conversations yet gets an
  empty chat room with a box to send the first message.
- Press `/` to search channels and categories, and `Esc` to go back to Browse.
- On narrow windows, the rail shrinks to faces and the chat column can collapse.

## Posts

Async team posts. Every conversation is a post filed in a space (its project or team, else
**General**), and your agents are the members. Each post carries a status mark such as **In
progress**, **Feedback requested**, or **Needs attention**.

- **Home** collects the posts that need you, **Needs attention** first, then **Feedback
  requested**. **Posts** shows everything, pinned first. Select a space or a member to narrow the
  feed.
- Open a post to read it as a page whose comments are the conversation, with a details rail beside
  it (press `I` to show or hide it).
- **New post** (or `C`) asks for a title and a body and files the post in the space or with the
  member you are looking at. Post with `Cmd+Enter` (`Ctrl+Enter` elsewhere); `Esc` discards it.

Press `J` and `K` to move between posts, and `Esc` to leave an open post.

## Community

A front page of communities. Your projects, teams, and agents are communities, and each
conversation is a post filed in one. A post's score comes from its messages, and its replies are
the comments. Your up and down votes are kept on screen only: they never reorder the feed or reach
your agents.

- **Home** shows every post and **Popular** those active in the last day. Sort by **Hot**, **New**,
  or **Top**, and switch between the card and compact views.
- Open a community for its banner, an **About** card, and **Message**, which starts a conversation
  there.
- Open a post to read it as a page whose comments are the conversation.
- **Create post** needs a title and a community and files the post there.

Press `/` to search posts and communities, `J` and `K` to walk the feed, and `Esc` to leave an open
post.

## Wiki

Your workspace as an encyclopedia. Every agent, team, project, and conversation has an article with
an infobox, a lead written from its live counts, a table of contents, and sections that link to
related articles.

- The **Main page** gathers highlights from across the workspace.
- **Recent changes** lists activity by day.
- **Contents** lists the **Agents**, **Projects**, **Teams**, and **Threads** (a sortable table of
  every conversation).

A conversation's article has **Read**, **Talk**, and **View history** tabs; **Talk** is the
conversation itself. On an agent's or a team's talk page, **Add a topic** starts a new conversation
there. Press `/` to search: suggestions appear as you type, and `Enter` shows a results page. The
main menu can move to the sidebar or hide.

## Q&A

A question-and-answer site. Every conversation is a question, the agent's replies are its answers,
and its project or team is the topic tag.

- The tabs are **Top** (pinned first, then the newest), **Unanswered**, **Answered**, and **Needs
  attention**.
- The rail ranks **Top answerers** and lists **Topics**. Select a tag or an answerer to narrow the
  questions, along with search.
- Open a question to read it as a page whose answers are the conversation, with related questions
  beside it.
- **Ask question** takes a question, details, a topic, and who answers. Send it with `Cmd+Enter`
  (`Ctrl+Enter` elsewhere); `Esc` discards the form.

Press `J` and `K` to move between questions, `/` to search, and `Esc` to leave an open question.

## Newspaper

Your conversations as a front page. The most urgent conversation is the lead story, the next ones
run under smaller headlines, the rest are **In brief**, and your projects and teams get section
fronts. The sidebar has **At a glance** totals and the **Staff**, your agents.

Select a headline to read the story as an article page whose body is the conversation; `Esc`
returns you to the headline. To start a conversation, write in **Submit a story**, pick who it is
assigned to, and choose **File story** (or press `Enter`).

## Gallery

Every conversation as a card on a masonry board, with a cover built from the conversation and a
byline. Covers take the color of the agent's avatar.

- Most covers quote the latest message, framed to its length.
- Running and failed runs show their latest output.
- The newest conversation in each project or team carries that group's poster.

Search, or use the chips (**All**, statuses, then one per agent), to narrow the board. Hovering
over a card shows **Open** and **Message**.

Opening a card shows its page: the cover, title, owner, status, project or team, message count,
and **More like this**, beside the conversation. **Back to gallery** (or `Esc`) closes it, and `/`
jumps to search. **New conversation** starts one.

## Cinema

Your conversations as a streaming catalog, always shown as a dark screening room whatever your
theme. Every title's key art is lit in its agent's color around that agent's initial, so an agent's
conversations look like one series.

A billboard features the running conversation (or the one waiting on you, or the newest), its
title set large as the poster, with **Open** and **More info**. Rows below shelve **Continue**
(your turn), **Live now**, **Needs attention**, **Recently updated**, **Most active**, **Pinned**,
and one row per agent.

- Rest on a poster to grow a preview with **Open**. Click it for **More info**.
- Select a row's title to see the whole collection, and search to see matching titles in a grid.
- **Open** shows the conversation in a full-height panel. `Esc` closes it.
- `/` jumps to search, and **New thread** in the top bar starts one.

## Noticeboard

Every conversation pinned to a felt board as a sticky note, an index card, or a typed memo,
grouped into areas by status. Drag a note anywhere: the board remembers where you put it on this
device, and **Tidy up** puts everything back. Focus a note and use the arrow keys to nudge it
(`Shift` for bigger steps).

Click a note to lift it and open its conversation beside the board; `Esc` closes it. **Pin a note**
drops a blank card where you write what an agent should pick up. `Enter` pins it, and `Esc` puts it
away.

## Library

A library of books. Every agent is a book bound in the color of its avatar, every project and team
a collection, and every conversation a chapter.

Covers are printed in a series per kind:

- agents with a title band and their initial as a colophon;
- projects in a stamped frame;
- teams in bold stacked type;
- unfiled conversations on plain paper.

**Home** shows **Continue Reading** (books with a chapter waiting on you, failures first, each with
**Resume**), the **Library** shelves with search and sort, and **Collections**. The sidebar also has
**All Books** and **Finished**.

Open a book for its contents, and a chapter to read the conversation in a reading view. In the
reading view, `←` and `→` turn to the previous and next chapter and `Esc` returns to the book.
**Write a chapter** starts a new conversation with the book's agent, or in the collection's project
or team. Press `/` to search.

## Journal

Your conversations as journal entries, each filed on the day of its latest activity. Pick a day in
the timeline, or switch the page between **Day**, **Calendar** (a month), and **Stats** (entries,
messages, days journaled, streaks, and a year of activity). **On this day** looks back to the same
date in earlier weeks, months, and years, and your day streak sits in the header.

Open an entry to read the conversation in its body. **New entry** asks what today's entry should be
about and which agent writes it; `Enter` starts it. Search narrows the timeline, and **Serif
titles** switches the title style.

| Action | Keys |
| --- | --- |
| Newer / older entry (while one is open) | `Alt+↑` / `Alt+↓` |
| Previous / next day (on the Day page) | `Alt+←` / `Alt+→` |
| Move through the Calendar month or the Stats heatmap | Arrow keys |

The `Alt` shortcuts work outside text fields.
