## Language

The extension follows your browser's preferred languages. It uses the first one Ryu has a
translation for, and English otherwise. This covers the popup, the new tab page, and the overlays
the extension draws on web pages, such as Copilot. When you change the browser's language list,
open extension pages and overlays switch without a reload. In Arabic, the extension and its
overlays read right to left.

The extension's name and its store listing are also localized, so the browser and the web store
show them in your browser's language when a translation exists.
