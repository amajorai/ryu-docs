## Language

Ryu Mobile shows its interface in the language you pick under **Settings**. With **Automatic**, it
uses the first of your phone's preferred languages that Ryu has a translation for, and English
otherwise. The app also tells your node which language it uses, so notifications such as monitor
alerts and "agent finished" arrive in that language. When you change the language, the app sends
the new one to the node.

On Android 13 and later, you can also set Ryu's language in the system settings (**Settings > Apps >
Ryu > Language**). While Ryu's own setting is **Automatic**, the app uses the language you set
there. With no language set there, it checks every language in your phone's list, not just the
first.

On iPhone, the permission prompts for the camera, microphone, Bluetooth, photos, contacts,
calendars, and reminders are shown by iOS. They follow the language iOS uses for Ryu: your iPhone's
language, or the one set for Ryu in the iOS Settings app (**Settings > Apps > Ryu > Language**).
Ryu's language setting does not change them. When Ryu has no translation for that language, the
prompts appear in English.

Arabic uses a right-to-left layout. If you choose Arabic in Ryu while your phone uses a
left-to-right language, or the other way round, Ryu asks to restart so the layout can switch
direction. Choose **Later** to switch the next time you open the app. In the right-to-left layout,
back buttons, list disclosure arrows, and expand arrows point along the reading direction.
