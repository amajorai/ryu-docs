The Telegram Mini App is the same shared app shell as the [Browser app](/docs/surfaces/webapp),
opened inside Telegram. Chats, agents, and settings are the ones you see on Desktop and the web when
you use the same Ryu account.

## Open Ryu in Telegram

Open the Ryu bot in Telegram and choose its **Open** button (or the menu button next to the message
field). The first time, link your Telegram account to Ryu:

1. Review the Telegram account shown and choose **Continue with Telegram**. A first launch or a
   different Telegram identity requires this confirmation before it changes your active Ryu account.
2. When the Mini App says **Connect Telegram to Ryu**, choose **Connect Telegram**. Telegram opens
   the Ryu connect page in your browser.
3. Sign in to Ryu there and approve **Telegram Login**. This is the same link the hosted Ryu bot
   uses for direct messages.
4. Return to Telegram and choose **I've connected it**.

After that, opening with the same remembered Telegram identity resumes your active session.
Choose **Keep current account** to leave an existing Ryu account active, or **Sign in another way**
to use normal Ryu sign-in when signed out.

Accounts with two-factor authentication turned on always use the normal sign-in flow, so opening
Telegram alone never skips your second factor. Waitlist approval and plan limits apply exactly as
they do in the browser app.

## How it fits into Telegram

| Telegram behavior | What Ryu does |
|---|---|
| Header **Back** button | Goes back through Ryu's in-app navigation and hides itself when there is nothing to go back to |
| Light and dark themes | Follows Telegram's theme until you pick a theme in Ryu's Appearance settings |
| Viewport and safe areas | Opens at full height, keeps content clear of notches and Telegram's own controls, and keeps chat scrolling from closing the Mini App |
| External links | Open in your browser |

The Mini App is a browser host, so it follows the same boundaries as the browser app: Desktop-only
controls such as native windows, tray settings, the app updater, and local file launchers are hidden.

## Privacy and security

Telegram signs the launch data it gives the Mini App. Ryu's server verifies that signature before it
trusts the Telegram account, and it only signs in a Telegram account you already linked yourself.
Opening a link never silently activates a different account: a new Telegram identity requires your
confirmation. The Mini App never creates a Ryu account or links a Telegram account on its own. The Ryu
session is stored in the Mini App's local account vault, like the browser app, and signing out
removes it.
