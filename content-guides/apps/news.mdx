## Data at rest

Wire encrypts what you chose and wrote with a key only this app receives from Ryu: source titles and feed links (a private feed link can carry a token), your watches, story titles, summaries and entities, and generated briefs. Articles are copies of public feed items and stay readable, as do read, saved and archived marks, story counts and settings. Data saved by an earlier version is encrypted when the updated app starts. If the app is updated before Ryu itself, it keeps working unencrypted until Ryu delivers its key, and its health status says it is waiting for a Ryu update. The `ryu-news mcp` agent tools reach encrypted data through the running app: when Wire is stopped they refuse sealed data, and opening Wire in Ryu starts it again.
