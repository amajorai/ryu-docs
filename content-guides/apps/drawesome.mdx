A focused sketch surface for Ryu with a pen, highlighter, area erasing, SVG or PNG export, plus chat dialogs for sketching or annotating visual references.

## Sketch surface

Drawesome opens directly to a dark, edge-to-edge canvas. A compact floating tray keeps the pen, highlighter, eraser, ink, size, and undo controls close to the drawing. The sketch title, save state, new sketch action, and exports stay in a small control group at the top edge.

In chat, the same canvas opens in a compact dialog with undo and redo at the upper-right, plus close and attach controls. When annotating an image, the image stays beneath the marks and is included in the attached PNG.
