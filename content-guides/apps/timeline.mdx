## Using Timeline

### Replay

Timeline opens in **Replay**, where the ruler and capture lanes move the playhead through the selected range.

- **Range.** Choose the last 15 minutes, hour, 6 hours, or 24 hours.
- **Transport controls.** Play or pause, step backward or forward, jump to now, zoom the lanes, or cycle playback speed.
- **Preview size.** Drag the divider below the preview to resize it.
- **Hover.** The preview follows the pointer across the lanes. Turn it off to move the playhead only by clicking. That preference is saved for your next visit.
- **Keyboard.** Event marks can be reached with Tab and opened with Enter or Space.

### History and journal

Switch to **Timeline** to browse a chronological History. When journal data is available, History groups work into focus blocks; otherwise it lists captured events.

- Select a block or event to return to Replay at that moment. From Replay, journal cards also jump to their start time.
- **Polish with AI** requests a narrated journal from the configured gateway.

### Weekly review

**Weekly review** opens the review page. It has no separate chat panel. To ask about your week, open the **Ask Ryu** assistant from that page.

The assistant receives the review as removable context, offers starter prompts, and can search your on-device timeline, subject to your tool permission settings. Your questions and the review summary go to the model the assistant uses, which may be a cloud model depending on your setup.

### When data is missing

- If activity recording is off, Timeline offers a direct link to Settings.
- If recent activity cannot be loaded, use **Try again**.
- A missing keyframe leaves the event timeline usable and shows the **No frame recorded** preview.
