When opened without a Ryu host bridge, Help Center shows a visible **Demo mode**
banner and uses sample records kept in memory. A connected desktop host loads
the selected Ryu Space instead; sample records are never inserted automatically.

If the Space cannot be opened, the activation screen keeps the error visible and
offers **Retry connection**. Failed ticket and article writes remain visible as
errors so an operator can retry; an unsaved reply stays in its composer. The
Agent preview also shows the model's uncertainty reason when it recommends a
human handoff.

In a connected desktop host, tickets and knowledge articles are stored as
Help Center records in that Space. Draft replies, notes, triage, resolution,
and reopening update those records locally. The app does not send customer
messages or claim outbound delivery.

Inbox assignment filters show active work; resolved tickets remain in the
Tickets history and appear in Inbox only when **Resolved** is selected.
Ticket rows keep the subject and requester above a wrapping row of status,
priority, channel and reply indicators, so those details stay readable in the
narrow Inbox sidebar.
