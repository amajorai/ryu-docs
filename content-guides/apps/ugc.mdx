Automated metric refresh uses the node's managed Composio provider. Configure its credential in **Gateway → Keys**; UGC does not store a separate Composio key. If a platform account is not connected, a refresh reports that account and offers the connection link returned by Composio. You can still record metric snapshots by hand. A refresh whose response has no valid view count is reported as a failed refresh and records nothing, so it never lowers a payout; enter the numbers by hand if the platform keeps returning incomplete data.

A submission labelled with one of the platforms UGC refreshes automatically (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, X or LinkedIn) must link to that platform's own site. A link on any other site is refused for that label; record it under another label, such as `other`, and enter its metrics by hand. Each post can be submitted once per campaign, whatever label it is sent under: different spellings of one post count as the same post, such as a `youtu.be` link and a `youtube.com/watch` link to one video, or a link that differs only by a percent-encoded character or a doubled slash.

## Data at rest

The creator roster (names, contact emails, platform handles, payout handles and notes) is encrypted with a key only this app receives from Ryu; the roster is searched after it is decrypted. Campaigns, submitted post links, view counts and payout amounts are not encrypted. Creators saved by an earlier version are encrypted when the updated app starts. If the app is updated before Ryu itself, it keeps working unencrypted until Ryu delivers its key, and its health status says it is waiting for a Ryu update.
