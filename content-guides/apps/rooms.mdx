The guest exchange returns an HttpOnly `ryu_rooms_session` cookie. Core forwards
that one app-owned cookie to the Rooms sidecar through the `/api/rooms` public
mount; Core authentication cookies and any undeclared browser cookies are never
forwarded to an app sidecar.

Revoking or rotating an invite removes existing guest sessions, including open
live event streams. Expired sessions also stop receiving room updates. Guests
must exchange a current invite to reconnect; keeping a browser tab open does not
preserve access after removal.

## Data at rest

Rooms encrypts room messages, the streamed answer, run errors and participant names with a key only this app receives from Ryu. Rooms saved by an earlier version are encrypted when the updated app starts. If the app is updated before Ryu itself, it keeps working unencrypted until Ryu delivers its key, and its health status says it is waiting for a Ryu update.
