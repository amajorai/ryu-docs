Meetings records a meeting, shows a live transcript, and writes AI notes when it ends. Each meeting is saved into the Meetings Space, where it stays editable and searchable by RAG (retrieval-augmented generation).

Speaker labeling is optional and off by default. When you turn it on, an installed local runtime uses pyannote or NVIDIA Nemotron after the meeting ends to find who spoke when. Audio stays on the Ryu node for this step.

{/* catalog:insert {"before":["### chat widget templates"]} */}

If a Desktop Meetings route opens while the app is turned off on the current node, choose **Enable** to continue; existing meeting data stays untouched. If the enable request is refused, the notice shows the reason and leaves **Enable** available to retry. The route refreshes meeting data after the app is confirmed enabled. Failed stop, rename, icon, and delete actions show an error notice and keep the existing meeting state available for an explicit retry; a failed delete does not navigate away.

{/* catalog:insert {"at":"end"} */}

## Data at rest

Meetings encrypts transcripts, speaker labels, meeting titles, participants, generated notes, and the recorded meeting audio on disk with a key only this app receives from Ryu. Ids and timestamps stay unencrypted. Recordings and transcripts saved by an earlier version are encrypted the first time the updated app starts; older audio files are encrypted in the background. If the app is updated before Ryu itself, it keeps working unencrypted until Ryu delivers its key, and its health status says it is waiting for a Ryu update.
