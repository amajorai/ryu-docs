Meetings records a meeting, shows a live transcript, and writes AI notes when it ends. Each meeting is saved into the Meetings Space, where it stays editable and searchable by RAG (retrieval-augmented generation).

Speaker labeling is optional and off by default. When you turn it on, an installed local runtime uses pyannote or NVIDIA Nemotron after the meeting ends to find who spoke when. Audio stays on the Ryu node for this step. The transcript shows the speaker labels it assigns ("Me" for your microphone, then "Speaker 1", "Speaker 2", and so on) in your interface language.

If a Desktop Meetings route opens while the app is turned off on the current node, choose **Enable** to continue. Existing meeting data stays untouched. If the enable request is refused, the notice shows the reason and leaves **Enable** available to retry. The route refreshes meeting data after the app is confirmed enabled.

Failed stop, rename, icon, and delete actions show an error notice and keep the existing meeting state available for an explicit retry. A failed delete does not navigate away.

## Data at rest

Meetings encrypts transcripts, speaker labels, meeting titles, participants, generated notes, and the recorded meeting audio on disk with a key only this app receives from Ryu. Ids and timestamps stay unencrypted.

Recordings and transcripts saved by an earlier version are encrypted the first time the updated app starts, and older audio files are encrypted in the background. If the app is updated before Ryu itself, it keeps working unencrypted until Ryu delivers its key. Its health status says it is waiting for a Ryu update.

## Recaps

Recaps are short personal summaries of conversations you choose to record. A recap is captured by a Ryu phone or watch client that supports Recaps, uploaded to your Ryu node, transcribed there, and summarized into a title, a short summary, key points, action items, decisions, and topics. The Meetings app's **Recaps** tab lists, searches, keeps, and deletes your recaps, and shows today's daily recap.

- **Off until turned on.** Recaps are off on every node by default. Someone who can record meetings turns them on from the Recaps tab. Members of an organization also need the **Can record recaps** permission, which no other permission includes.
- **Consent first.** Before your first recap, Ryu shows which transcription engine and summary model will process your audio and transcript. You are asked again when the summary model changes or the consent text is updated. You can withdraw consent at any time; Ryu then refuses new recaps and offers to delete the ones you have.
- **Always started by you.** A recap starts only when you tap Start, and the recording is always shown. A start from a hardware button or a system control must be confirmed within a few seconds, or it is discarded without being transcribed.
- **Private to you.** Only you can list, read, search, export, or delete your recaps. They never appear in the shared meetings list, the live meetings feed, or the tools the chat assistant can call.
- **Sensitive numbers are masked.** Card numbers, IBANs, US Social Security numbers, email addresses, and phone numbers are replaced with placeholders before the transcript is sent to the summary model, and again in the stored notes. Your own transcript keeps the original words.
- **Names are left out.** People's names are not kept in notes unless you turn that on, and they never appear in the daily recap or in search.
- **Audio is not kept.** Each audio chunk is decoded in memory, transcribed, and discarded. Chunks with no speech are skipped before transcription.

By default, transcripts are deleted 7 days after a recap finishes, recaps 30 days after it finishes unless you choose **Keep**, and daily recaps after 30 days. You can change these windows. Deleting a recap also removes it from that day's daily recap. You can export your recaps as JSON or Markdown, or delete all of them at once.

The daily recap is built from the day's finished recaps after your chosen time (21:00 by default) and announced with a notification that contains only the number of conversations, never their content. Recap notifications say only that a recap is ready.

**Interim summaries** are off by default. When you turn them on, a recap that is still recording can show a short "so far" summary, and the Recaps tab shows it on the open recap. A new interim summary is made at most once for every five minutes of new speech, with the same masking of sensitive numbers and the same names setting. Interim summaries are never saved: they are dropped when the recap finishes or is deleted.

Your node never stores reminders to start a recap or any location: the recap settings it accepts have no reminder or location fields, so anything like that stays on your device.

To record recaps from your phone or watch, and for the start confirmation, widgets, Siri and Spotlight options, see [Recaps on mobile](/docs/mobile/recaps).
