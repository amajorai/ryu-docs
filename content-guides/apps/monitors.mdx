Monitors watch website prices, stock, keywords, content, and uptime, plus provider quota windows and API-credit balances. Provider reset monitors detect scheduled refills and provider-initiated resets by comparing live usage snapshots. A first check records a quiet baseline; later checks notify when a selected window refills or a credit balance increases.

When no monitor is selected, the Companion can also show a **Usage pace** estimate for a subscription provider. It uses the average usage since the provider-reported window began to estimate whether a timed quota will be exhausted before its next reset. Estimates update while the Companion is open and are available when the provider reports both a window length and reset time. They are projections, so they can change as usage changes.
