## Data at rest

Group names and descriptions are encrypted with a key only this app receives from Ryu. Member lists and coordination settings stay readable.

- Groups saved by an earlier version are encrypted when the updated app starts.
- If the app is updated before Ryu itself, it keeps working unencrypted until Ryu delivers its key. Its health status says it is waiting for a Ryu update.
