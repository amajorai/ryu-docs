Group deletion is confirmed explicitly. If a delete request fails, the confirmation keeps the group and shows the error so the action can be retried.

## Data at rest

Team names and descriptions are encrypted with a key only this app receives from Ryu. Member lists and coordination settings stay readable. Teams saved by an earlier version are encrypted when the updated app starts. If the app is updated before Ryu itself, it keeps working unencrypted until Ryu delivers its key, and its health status says it is waiting for a Ryu update.
