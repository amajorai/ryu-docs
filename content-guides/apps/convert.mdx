PDF split and extract create separate files for the selected pages. Links and form references to excluded pages are removed so their page contents are not retained in those files. Selected-page rendering resources and links to the selected page or external websites are preserved.

Unused image and form resources are also removed. If a page combines inline images with shared image or form resources that cannot be safely separated, extraction reports an error; use PDF-to-image conversion for that page instead.
