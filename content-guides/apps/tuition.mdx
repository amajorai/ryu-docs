If adding a material or subject fails, Tuition keeps the entered values so you can retry. A
successful retry clears the earlier error and refreshes the saved item in the list.
While a study, skill, material, review, subject, or settings write is pending, its action is
disabled so a repeated click cannot submit the same change twice. A failed settings save keeps
the edited values for an exact retry.

## Data at rest

Tuition encrypts your study material and answers with a key only this app receives from Ryu: subject and skill descriptions, the text of your materials and their titles and links, each question's prompt, choices and answer key, your answers and their feedback, session summaries, and suggested review cards. Subject and skill names, mastery and schedule numbers stay readable so the planner and reminders can run. When you accept a suggested review card, the skill it names becomes a skill name, which stays readable like every other skill name. Data saved by an earlier version is encrypted when the updated app starts. If the app is updated before Ryu itself, it keeps working unencrypted until Ryu delivers its key, and its health status says it is waiting for a Ryu update.

Agent tools run inside the running app, which holds the key; when the app is stopped, opening Tuition in Ryu starts it again.
