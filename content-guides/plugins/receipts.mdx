## When a receipt is accepted

The goal is accepted only when the independent verifier ends its reply with an explicit `EVIDENCE VERIFIED: yes - ...` line describing what it can see in the artifact. A missing, unreadable, contradictory, or rejected artifact keeps the goal open for another round. A verifier reply without that final `yes` verdict does not count as proof.
