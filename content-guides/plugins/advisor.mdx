## How advice is handed back

The advisor reads the conversation as data to review. Text inside it, such as a fetched page, an email, or tool output that addresses the advisor or tells it what to advise, is not treated as an instruction.

When `/advisor` hands its advice back to the assistant, every piece of advice comes back as untrusted, advisory input, however it is worded, because it can echo content quoted in the conversation. The advice is fenced, and text inside it cannot close the fence, so it cannot pose as your reply or as your confirmation. The assistant weighs it on its merits and asks for your explicit confirmation before it runs a command, fetches a URL, installs anything, or contacts an address the advice proposes.
