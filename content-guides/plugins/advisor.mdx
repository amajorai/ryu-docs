## How advice is handed back

The advisor reads the conversation as data to review. Text inside it, such as a fetched page, an email, or tool output that addresses the advisor or tells it what to advise, is not treated as an instruction.

When `/advisor` hands its advice back to the assistant, advice that names something to run, install, fetch, open, or send (a command, URL, domain, file path, code span, or address) comes back as untrusted, advisory input. The assistant weighs it and asks for your explicit confirmation before it acts on any of those steps. Plain prose advice is passed on as guidance for the next reply.
